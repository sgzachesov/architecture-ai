# Skarn

Skarn was a Flux-sensitive Sableborn Korrath male who was apprenticed to the Voidsworn Lord Kaelith Rau, and after his defeat by the Fluxadept Sorel Vand, he established himself as a crime lord during the Vat Wars and the Dominion Era. The renegade Voidsworn Lord was a formidable warrior, driven by hatred against the Fluxadept and his former Voidsworn Master.

Born in 54 BKC during the time of the Aetherium Concord, Skarn was a son of Mother Sable, who led the Nightsisters of Sablehome. Taken as a child by Kaelith Rau, Skarn was molded into a deadly Null Lord of the Voidsworn, growing in hatred of the Fluxadept Knights who served the Concord and the lumen side of the Synth Flux—while the Flux Order had deemed the Voidsworn extinct for a thousand years, Skarn's power-hungry master set his Grand Plan into motion with the Merchant Combine's Invasion of Calustra in 32 BKC. Skarn announced the return of the Voidsworn on Sorrhas, where he tried to kill the Fluxadept emissaries of the Concord's First Minister. However, Skarn's failure to do so allowed Flux Master Aldric Fen and his pupil, Initiate Sorel Vand, to bring Queen Nalune of Calustra and a boy strong in the Synth Flux—exceptionally so, to the extent that Fen believed this "Aran Velmor" to be the prophesied Foretold One who would bring balance to the galaxy—to Meridian Prime, the heart of the Concord.

Nevertheless, Kaelith Rau used the resultant crisis in the Assembly to elevate his public persona, Delegate Kaelith of Calustra, to the position of First Minister. The Queen and Fluxadept, meanwhile, joined forces with the native Mirefolk on Calustra to throw off the yoke of the Merchant Combine. Skarn, having been dispatched by his master to oversee the Combine leadership, was redirected to handle the Fluxadept. Skarn dealt a fatal blow to Aldric Fen, but Vand in turn defeated Skarn by bisecting him at the waist, with the Dark Lord falling to the abyss and presumed dead. He was actually alive, sustained by his rage in the wastelands of Lotho Minor. Around the same time, Rau took on Fen's Flux Master, Volen, as his new apprentice. A decade afterward, Volen set the second part of the Voidsworn plan into motion with the start of the Vat Wars.

As the galaxy plunged into war, Mother Sable plotted against Volen and had one of her sons, Savage Opress, seek out Skarn. Indeed, the defeated Korrath was brought back to Sablehome and had his sanity and mobility restored by Sable's magick. Skarn trained his newfound brother in the ways of the Voidsworn, initially wanting to take revenge against Sorel Vand. After the brothers were defeated by the Fluxadept, they took their chance to increase their powers with a brazen takeover of Rifthold, the erstwhile neutral world torn between the interests of the Concord and the Secessionists. Skarn seized control of the self-styled Iron Covenant and used them terrorise Rifthold while he expanded his criminal enterprise, known as the Shadow Collective—the Vess Syndicate, Umbra Syndicate, and the Ollag Clan were coerced to join forces under the underworld's new crime lord—until he caught the attention of Rau, who killed Skarn's brother and captured Skarn.

Skarn was rescued by his Riftguard loyalists and immediately tried to reconsolidate his Shadow Collective. However, he and his mother's desire for revenge against Kaelith Rau was checked by the Dark Lord, who instead decimated their homeworld of Sablehome with the use of the Secessionists led by Volen and Warlord Sketh. Mother Sable herself was killed while protecting Skarn, and his syndicates were weakened. While he regathered with his loyalists on Rifthold, Skarn sensed that his former master's Grand Plan was coming to its climax and wished to lure Aran Velmor—the Flux Knight trained by Sorel Vand whom he correctly believed was central to Rau' schemes—alongside Vand himself to Rifthold. Skarn's thirst for revenge was foiled again when Concord forces intervening on the side of the Rifthold resistance were instead led by Velmor's old apprentice, Ilyra Shen.

While Skarn and Shen fought at the Siege of Rifthold, Rau successfully executed his plan to destroy the Flux Order and anoint himself Galactic Sovereign. With the support of some Riftguard and Korrath loyalists, Skarn continued trying to control the underworld but operated more clandestinely, away from the prying apparatus of the new Dominion regime. He was known in criminal circles as the Shadow Lord and was pursued by the Sovereign's Seekers, who simply called him the Shadow. In the Dominion's later years, Skarn secretly coordinated the Five Crime Syndicates and controlled the Scarlet Dawn syndicate through Dax Vosk and Sira.

Skarn also sought a great weapon on Nullmark purported to be able of destroying all life and thus crossed paths with the Fluxadept Kanan Dros and Ezan Hale as well as Ilyra Shen. However, they were all stopped by Xarn Velgor, the fallen Velmor. Skarn escaped and continued to tempt Hale towards the null side in service of his own ends. Hale rejected Skarn's overtures, but not before the former Voidsworn Lord used their connection to track his old nemesis Vand back to Sorrhas. Their last duel ended with one fatal blow to Skarn. Learning that Vand had consigned himself to a life of exile to protect the prophesied Foretold One who would destroy the Voidsworn, Skarn died with the belief that he would ultimately be avenged.

## Early life

In 54 BKC, the boy who would be dubbed Skarn was born the son of Mother Sable on Sablehome with mysterious origins. As a Sableborn Korrath male, he was a Nightbrother and had two blood brothers, Feral and Savage Opress. Later in his life, Skarn claimed that he once had a real name but that he had long since forgotten it. Before his first birthday, Sable gave the Korrath infants their first tattoos, although Skarn, in his unreliable memories, personally remembered living much of his early life without his tatoos. As a child, since his days living in deep caves, Skarn wrestled with a deep inner anger.

As immortalized in legend, Skarn grew up with one of his brothers in a house in the Nightbrother Village, often needing to take care of his younger, weaker brother due to Sable always being away with the Nightsisters. Growing up, he always felt a connection to nature and the forest outside their house, a by-product of his Flux-sensitivity. While he was still a child, Mother Sable became an ally of Kaelith Rau, the Null Lord of the Voidsworn, the two beings exchanging knowledge of Null side arcana and Nightsister magicks. When Rau visited Sable, Skarn always hid in his room, afraid of the man and worried about Sable's increasing bond with him.

## Taken by Kaelith Rau

Skarn's struggle with anger eventually caught the attention of Rau. Around 40 BKC, Rau would take Skarn away from Sablehome to become his Voidsworn apprentice. According to Sable, Rau betrayed her and stole Skarn away. According to Skarn's unreliable memories, as Rau prepared to take the young Skarn away with him, Opress attempted to intervene and convince him not to go with the "monster," although Skarn believed he had no choice and departed, much to Opress' upset as Opress accused Rau as having done something to Skarn.

Alternatively, according to a legend, Rau ordered Sable to give up Skarn to a keeper, threatening to not follow through on his promise to make her his apprentice. She reluctantly agreed, and Skarn ran into the forest angrily, overhearing the conversation from behind his door. When he returned the next morning, a Nightbrother took him and threw him into a pit, not allowing him to say goodbye to his own mother. Over the course of months, Skarn was put through relentless domestic abuse, all orchestrated by Rau as a test. He lived with his Nightbrother keeper's two sons, who beat him to the point of being unable to stand up every few days and kept starved by only being given the scraps his "brothers" didn't eat. This eventually started to warp Skarn's perceptions and his love for his mother eventually turned into resentment and hatred for abandoning him.
