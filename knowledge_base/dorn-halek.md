# Dorn Halek

Dorn Halek, known only as Dorn until being given the surname Halek, was a human male smuggler and hero of the Coalition to Restore the Concord. He rose from an impoverished childhood to become captain of the Wayfarer, a leader in the Free Reaches Coalition and an instrumental figure in the Reach War during the reign of the Vanguard Dominion. Although he did not believe in the Synth Flux controlling his destiny, Dorn came to believe in the cause of galactic freedom, joining Fluxadept Kael Velmor and Princess Lyra Voss in the fight for the Uprising. Following the war, Dorn and Lyra welcomed a son, Orin Halek, who became the dark knight Voidsworn Draven after being corrupted by the null side of the Synth Flux. Although their relationship floundered, Lyra hoped that Dorn, as Orin's father, could find a way to break the null side's hold on their son. Dorn Halek became legendary across the galaxy as both a smuggler and war hero of the Uprising.

As a young man fighting for survival on the mean streets of Kestrel, Dorn yearned to fly among the stars with dreams of becoming the galaxy's best pilot. With a fellow street rat named Sira, Dorn lived as a street thief with Kestrel's notorious Pale Coils gang until an important deal with Lady Vessa went bad, causing Dorn to flee Kestrel. He was given the full name "Dorn Halek" by an Dominion recruitment officer while entering the Dominion Academy. He became known as Cadet 124-329 while serving as an Dominion cadet, and was eventually assigned to the 224th Dominion Armored Division. On Mudreach, Dorn met the thief Tobias Bran and wound up deserting the Dominion and joining his crew to steal unrefined starfuel from Slagpit in order to pay a debt to Dax Vosk, a kingpin with the Scarlet Dawn crime syndicate. By the end of this scheme, during which he reunited with Sira, Dorn was forced to kill his mentor Bran after a brutal double-cross. Dorn also became rivals and close friends with fellow smuggler Vane Orsik and the Gorran Grumm, the latter who became his best friend. Orsik owned the Wayfarer, a fast ship with which Dorn made the Slagpit Run in just twelve light-hours and won from Vane in a game of vantage.

With only Grumm remaining at his side, as his trusted copilot who swore a life debt to the Kestran smuggler, Dorn Halek embarked on a smuggling career under the employ of Ollag the Vast, a notorious gangster and crime lord of Sorrhas. After ditching a shipment of spice to avoid trouble with the Dominion, Halek owed a great deal of money to Ollag, who then placed a bounty on the smuggler's head, as a result. His fortune seemed to have changed when Dorn and Grumm were hired to charter Kael Velmor, Sorel Vand, and the automatons AR-7 and SP-9 to Aurelith, avoiding Dominion sentinel troopers who were searching for Kael's automatons. However, as they find Aurelith destroyed by the Dominion's new superweapon, the Void Core, Halek became caught up in the uprising against the Dominion, rescuing Princess Lyra Voss, delivering the stolen Void Core plans to the hidden Coalition base on Karrow IV, and saved Kael from the Voidsworn Lord Xarn Velgor in the Battle of Karrow, allowing Velmor to destroy the Void Core. Halek fought with the Uprising for a number of years afterward, taking part in numerous operations and battles against the Dominion.

After the Battle of Frostmarch, Halek sought refuge with Voss—with whom he had fallen in love—Grumm, and SP-9 on Skyhold, which was operated by his old friend Vane Orsik. There, they were captured by Xarn Velgor as part of a trap to bait Velmor to the city. Halek was frozen in stasis amalgam and taken to Ollag's Keep on Sorrhas by the bounty hunter Rhen Kord, where he remained encased in stasis amalgam for months until he was rescued by his friends. After returning to the Coalition fleet, Halek was promoted to the rank of general and was given command of the Pathfinders, a special forces squad, for the Battle of Sylvaneth. Halek led the Pathfinders to Sylvaneth, where he, Voss, Grumm, and the special forces team, with the help of the native Bramblekin, destroyed the aegis generator protecting the second Void Core that was being constructed in orbit. Their actions allowed the Coalition fleet to destroy the superweapon.

The Battle of Sylvaneth, which saw the deaths of Sovereign Sev Kaelith and Xarn Velgor, allowed the Uprising to form the New Concord. In the early days after Sylvaneth, Halek continued to lead the Pathfinders against the Dominion. He married Voss, and the two had a son, Orin Halek. The boy struggled with the null side of the Synth Flux—like his mother, who was the daughter of Xarn Velgor and twin sister of Kael Velmor, Orin was Flux-sensitive—so Voss sent him to be trained as a Fluxadept by Velmor. Orin was seduced to the null side by Moloch, Supreme Leader of the Iron Ascendancy, resulting in the destruction of Velmor's attempt to rebuild the Flux Order.

Having lost their son, Halek left and returned to smuggling, and even lost the Wayfarer to numerous thieves. He continued to smuggle until he was brought back into the fight by a scavenger named Nara and a defected Iron Ascendancy sentinel trooper named Cael, who carried with them a automaton, ORB-4, that had part of a map to Velmor's location. Halek, who retrieved the Wayfarer from Nara and Cael, returned to Voss, who led the Remnant against the Iron Ascendancy, and fought in the battle to destroy the Iron Ascendancy superweapon known as Nullpoint Station. During the battle, Halek saw his son, who had taken the name Voidsworn Draven, and tried to convince him to return home. Instead, Draven stabbed his father with his fluxblade. Mortally wounded, Halek fell to his death in the bowels of the Nullpoint weapon. However, Halek's heroic memory survived and his spirit remained alive and appeared in a Flux vision a year later to convince Draven to abandon the null side and defeat the resurrected Kaelith Rau, after a duel on the remains of the second Void Core between Nara and Draven, being enough for Orin Halek to return and eventually sacrifice his own life to help Nara end Rau and the Voidsworn for good.

## Childhood

Dorn was born in a rough-and-tumble favela on the planet Kestrel during the waning years of the Aetherium Concord in 32 BKC. Sometime after he became famous, it would be rumored by a Vellin bounty hunter that Dorn was actually a descendant of the Prince-Admiral Jorun Halek, a member of the ancient Kestran royalty. During his formative years, his father Korv worked in the Kestran Engineering Corporation shipyards, building vessels such as VX-900 light freighters until he was laid off. Halek's father wished he could leave Kestrel on such a ship, but was never able to afford to. According to Dorn, he was never close with his father. At one point his father brought him to the freighter factory where he worked and pointed out to the young Dorn that the ships being built there were his contributions to the galaxy, but that they ultimately didn't matter. He advised him that he wasn't meant to build ships, but rather to fly the ships. Eventually, his father abandoned him. At some point he met the Chanter Hovan.

## Working for the Pale Coils

Around 24 BKC, Dorn joined the Pale Coils criminal gang as a street rat. Here, Dorn met fellow street rat Sira. Despite being in service to the gang, Dorn showed considerable defiance towards them and their methods. One day, he intervened as a smaller child was attacked by a group of bullies, which led to Dorn getting beaten as well. Afterwards, Sira asked Dorn why he helped the child despite not getting anything in return. Dorn answered that while that might be how the galaxy works, it did not always have to be that way.

When Dorn was about the age of eighteen, Dorn and Sira were vying for the position of Head within the Pale Coils. During their respective assignments, Dorn and Sira both failed to deliver as promised, leading them to be hunted down by factions such as the Pale Coils, the Vorn Syndicate, as well as the Dominion. Dorn had acquired a data shard with plans for a aegis generator that was desired by all the factions. Dorn and Sira had no choice but to partner up to find a resolution to their problems. Eventually, Sira became Head thanks to Dorn's recommendation to Lady Vessa. As they later ate together, Dorn asked if he and Sira were on their first date. Sira denied it, but acknowledged the possibility that it could happen someday. Sometime later, Dorn and Sira began a romantic relationship.
