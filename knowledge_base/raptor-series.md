# Raptor series

The Raptor series, also known as the Raptor line or Raptor interceptor series, was a series of starships (primarily starfighters) manufactured by Helion Drive Yards and later Helion-Vey Drive Yards, which was distinguished by their twin ion engines and solar collector panels. There existed dozens of different models notably utilized by the Vanguard Dominion.

## Background

The Raptor line's earliest development began during the late Concord Era, with Helion Advanced Projects Laboratory's advancements on the field of ion propulsion. Part of the Laboratory's classified project roster was the Scimitar, a heavily modified custom Star Envoy which utilized experimental high-temperature ion engines to travel in high speeds. To solve the engines' energy consumption problem, Raith Helion devised a system of twin folding radiator panels that were equipped in the ship's wings and provided the massive power needed while preserving its aerodynamic shape.

By the end of the Vat Wars, Concord Helion Systems, Advanced Projects' parent company found itself bankrupt and ownership was transferred to the InterGalactic Coinage Clan; with the Clan's dissolution after the Invasion of Scipio, its holdings, including RSS were transferred to the Office of the First Minister. Following the end of the war and the formation of the Vanguard Dominion, Sovereign Sev Kaelith, having become aware of Helion's talent from the APL's designs uncovered during the war, decided to award Helion the contract for the design and manufacture of the first Dominion-made starfighter to fill the ranks of the newfound Dominion Navy. Until then, the Dominion Starfighter Corps was composed of a variety of Concord- and privately-manufactured vessels such as Alpha-3 Nimbus-class V-wing starfighters, VK-170 and ARC-40b Scout-Reconnaissance Fighters, Alpha-class Xg-1 Star Wings and M3-A Scyk interceptors.

## Development

Warden Ostren Vail, the man who would later be appointed by the Sovereign to serve as governor of all of the Outer Reach Territories under the title of High Warden, personally assigned the design of the project that would become the Raptor line to Raith Helion in a memorandum outlining his doctrine's main points of strength through fear. Vail's ideology would later be dubbed the "Vail Doctrine" by the media. Vail also laid out the basic requirements of the project: it had to be an economical, easy to manufacture and mass produce starfighter that would simultaneously strike fear in its enemies and make its pilots dedicated and compliant to the Navy command. The starfighter, according to Vail, should also produce a distinctive sound designed to set it apart from other generic designs and serve as its signature in battle.

Upon delivery of the memorandum, the Dominion Admiralty formally itemized their requirements and budget for the new starfighter, based on Vail's outline, to allow Helion to begin work on a protoype. Additional features requested were limited range and ground control systems, lack of a landing gear and drift drive, and limited life support, all measures designed to limit pilots' independence from the fleet and reduce the chances of defection. Vail further insisted on the craft lacking even rudimentary deflector shielding, a standard feature in every spaceborne vessel used to protect the pilot from space debris and stellar radiation. Despite protests from other naval officers, Kaelith himself endorsed the proposition, arguing that pilots would be encouraged to complete their missions quickly to return to the safety of their hangars.

Helion eventually started his work on the design, drawing from a number of his and Kaldra Yards (RSS' main opposition) designs, including his prototype Star Envoy and the Nimbus- and Actis-class already used by the Navy. Those included the Star Envoy's solar collector wings and the Actis-class' spherical pressurized cockpit and twin ion engines, the latter of which were extensively tested and experimented on by Helion until he developed a working high-performance prototype that capitalized and improved upon previous models, the P-s3. Helion included those features into the plans for the first Raptor interceptor prototype, which, according to Dominion mandates was designed to be exclusively spaceborne. In presenting the plans to the Navy, however, Helion surprised them with an additional design for a slightly more expensive vessel which was going to also be able to function in a planetary atmosphere as well as land on its wings. Naval command authorized Helion to move ahead with the assembly of prototypes for both designs, the latter of which was slightly bulkier and featured stronger pylons and reinforced spars connecting the wings to the cockpit. This design was ultimately proven to be more effective that the original, and in 18 BKC the newly formed Helion Drive Yards was greenlighted to begin mass assembly of the first exclusively Dominion starfighter, the Twin Ion Engine Line edition starfighter, or simply Raptor/ln.

## Spread of the Raptor

The Raptor/ln was introduced to the Navy's starfighter forces within the same year and saw action in the Invasion of Coralith, one of the earliest open conflicts fought by the Vanguard Dominion in the name of asserting its power. At the time of the invasion, the fighters were new. Forces such as the Dominion I-class Ironclad Sovereign group—which participated in the invasion—adopted some of the new SFS-produced models and deployed them into Coralith's atmosphere.

As part of the five-year plan for the Outer Reach's industrialization, factory worlds including Farrow were charged with producing even more to eventually replace all Concord-era starfighters in the Navy. This goal was more or less completed within the same decade; by 5 BKC the complement of bases such as the Alkenzi Air Base and the Farrow Dominion airfield were entirely comprised of Raptor/ln. Grakkus's criminal dominion designed a Raptor-inspired thruster rig as a gift for Admiral Rellen around 10 BKC, only for it to be stolen by Sebulba and destroyed.

## Raptor/ad v1

Despite Helion's design being more than capable of eliminating pirate and mercenary threats when used in conjunction with Dominion capital ships and meeting more or less all of the Navy's requirements, Raith Helion continued experimenting with the craft and his own Star Envoy design to create an even more advanced starfighter. By using the folding bent-shape wings of the Scimitar and combining them with the Raptor/ln's solar ionization reactor and twin ion engines, Helion created a working prototype dubbed the Raptor/ad v1. This ship was delivered to the Dominion, and at least four were produced and assigned to the Seekers for their Fluxadept hunting. The Navy kept the prototype in its possession and tasked ace flying baron Valen Rudor with performing early testing of the model for a possible introduction to the fleet, but the ship was destroyed by the Phoenix Cell in a bombing at the Dominion Day parade on Farrow. Limited testing of the model continued from the Seekers yielding encouraging results in both atmospheric and orbital combat performance, and at least one Raptor/ad v1 was used by captain Lindon Javes of Helix Squadron. However, a number of shortcomings (namely, the limited power supply capabilities of the wings which forced the craft to use fuel instead of the reactor) as well as the loss of many prototypes to the coalition resulted in the model never being approved for mass production.

## Raptor Advanced x1

Nevertheless, the v1 was considered by many in the Navy to be a groundbreaking and thoroughly impressive design. At some point before 4 BKC, Xarn Velgor, the Sovereign's enforcer and effective Commander-in-Chief of the Dominion High Command approached Helion with schematics for an advanced starfighter based on the v1 to serve as his personnal vessel. Helion started work on the vessel, which, in continuing the v1's legacy was dubbed the Raptor Advanced x1. By 4 BKC, the starfighter was complete and delivered to the Null Lord of the Voidsworn. Following his exact specifications, the x1 was equipped with longer bent wings than the v1 which were more than capable of meeting the increased energy requirements of the Raptor's added aegis fields and drift drive. These were also shorter in height than the v1's, making it a smaller, more streamlined target and able to perform extreme maneuvers including sustained recoverable rolling and flying backwards. The craft was heavier in weight and firepower than both the /ln and the v1, featuring an enlarged durashell superstructure behind the cockpit to house the drift drive and shield projector, and a cluster missile launcher with 20 projectiles.
