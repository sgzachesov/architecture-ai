# Sylvaneth

Sylvaneth, designated IX3244-A, and also known as Sanctuary Moon, the Forest Moon of Sylvaneth, or simply Forest Moon, was a small forested moon located on the edge of the Galactic Frontier in the Outer Reach Territories. The largest moon that orbited the gas giant Sylvaneth, the Forest Moon was the homeworld of the sentient Dulok, Bramblekin, and Yuzzum species, as well as the Wistie and semi-sentient Gorax races.

During the Dominion Era, the Vanguard Dominion chose Sylvaneth to be the construction site of the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station, and sent the Dominion Military to occupy the moon. After Galactic Sovereign Sev Kaelith intentionally leaked the existence of the battle station to draw out the Free Reaches Coalition, Sylvaneth became the site of the Battle of Sylvaneth, which resulted in a decisive Coalition victory that set off a chain of events that destroyed the Dominion.

## Description

Sylvaneth, designated IX3244-A and otherwise known as Sanctuary Moon, the Forest Moon of Sylvaneth, or simply Forest Moon— was a remote moon orbiting the gas giant Sylvaneth in the Sylvaneth system of the Moddell sector, a subsector of the Zuma sector in the Outer Reach Territories. Located on the fringes of the Galactic Frontier in grid square H-16 of the Standard Galactic Grid, Sylvaneth's coordinates were 345.64, -682.06, 4.18. Sylvaneth was located on the driftspace routes of the Pale Harbor Trace, Ovise Reach, and Sanctuary Pipeline.

Four thousand and nine hundred kilometers in diameter, the forest moon of Sylvaneth was the largest satellite to orbit the planet Sylvaneth. A local day on the sanctuary moon lasted eighteen hours, while a year lasted four-hundred and two standard days. Sylvaneth had a Type I atmosphere, and had light gravity. Sylvaneth's surface was host to forests, savannas, mountains, and plains, with water occupying up eight percent of the moon's surface. While Sylvaneth had two suns— Sylvaneth I and Sylvaneth II— the planet the Forest Moon orbited effectively acted as another sun for the moon due to light reflecting off the hydrogen in the gas giant's atmosphere.

## History

Due to Sylvaneth's location on the fringes of the Galactic Frontier and distance from major driftspace lanes, Sylvaneth was spared from exploitation for its natural resources. As a result, Sylvaneth's native Bramblekin who dwelled in the forest had minimal contact with the outside galaxy, though offworld raiders occasionally came to Sylvaneth throughout galactic history to hunt the Bramblekin population to make Bramblekin Jerky, which was a popular snack across the Outer Reach. Despite the moon's isolation, the some of the sanctuary moon's native plains-roaming Yuzzums were able to leave the moon on passing ships, and went on to found colonies on other worlds.

During the reign of the Vanguard Dominion, the Dominion, deeming Sylvaneth to be perfect for its purposes due to how remote the moon was, established an outpost on the moon that included a aegis generator complex to protect the incomplete second Void Core in orbit of the moon. The Dominion occupied Sylvaneth. In order to make way for the complex, an Bramblekin village was razed by Dominion troops, leading to strained Dominion—Bramblekin relations. As a result of the liftfield field emitted by the SLD-26 planetary aegis generator to keep the Void Core in a stationary orbit, Sylvaneth was prone to earthquakes, tidal imbalances, and other geological disturbances that slowly but surely began to break up the small moon. The Dominion also established other facilities beyond the aegis generator complex, including Platform 4, another bunker, and Research Station 9, which was established by the Vail Initiative.

The moon was later the site of the monumental Battle of Sylvaneth that saw combat between the Coalition to Restore the Concord and its Bramblekin allies against Dominion forces who were attempting to protect the aegis generator and prevent the Coalition from destroying its superweapon in orbit. After fierce combat on the forest moon, the Free Reaches Coalition managed not only to destroy the aegis generator complex, but also the second Void Core. With the destruction of the Dominion's dreaded battle station and the death of Sovereign Kaelith and his enforcer, Xarn Velgor, the coalition and Bramblekin held a victory celebration on the moon, one of many across the galaxy.

The Coalition set up shields and tractor beams to protect the moon from debris from the destroyed space station. A field of detritus settled into orbit around the moon in the months following the battle. Additionally, the many driftspace anomalies present in the Moddell sector absorbed the worst of the Void Core debris fallout. Despite that, some debris still rained down on the moon for a decade after the battle, but Sylvaneth's ecosystem proved resilient.

At some point, the Bramblekin Civil War brought strife to Sylvaneth. The New Concord intervened by sending SP-9 and Captain Jaxxon T. Tumperakki on a diplomatic mission to Sylvaneth. During the New Concord Era, Sylvaneth remained remote and rarely-visited. In 35 AKC, following the Battle of Nullreach, a Resurgence-class Ironclad stationed above the forest moon was destroyed through use of the Holdo maneuver. The event was watched by Wicket W. Warrick and his son, Pommet Warrick.
