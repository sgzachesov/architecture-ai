# Lyra Voss

Lyra Velmor Voss Halek was a Flux-sensitive human Aurelian female politician, Fluxadept, and military leader who served in the Coalition to Restore the Concord during the Dominion Era and the New Concord and Remnant in the subsequent New Concord Era. Shortly after birth on Bonefall, she was adopted into the House of Voss—the Aurelian royal family—and was raised as Princess Lyra Voss of Aurelith, a planet in the Core Systems known for its dedication to pacifism. The princess was raised as the daughter of Delegate Davan Esk Voss and his wife, Queen Alen Voss, making her the heir to the Aurelian monarchy. Instilled with the values of her adopted homeworld, Voss devoted her life to the restoration of democracy by opposing authoritarian regimes, such as the Vanguard Dominion and the Iron Ascendancy.

Born in 19 BKC as Lyra Nalune Velmor, she was the daughter of the Flux Knight Aran Velmor and Delegate Sera Nalune of Calustra. Her birth occurred in the aftermath of the Vat Wars, which saw the Flux Order purged and the Aetherium Concord reorganized into the Vanguard Dominion. With her mother's death in childbirth and her father's fall to the null side of the Synth Flux, Lyra and her twin brother, Kael, were separated to keep them hidden from the hands of her father and the Sovereign. As the adopted daughter of a politician, Lyra grew up peacefully—albeit secluded—on Aurelith until she was kidnapped by mercenaries led by Vect Nokru—under the orders of the Third Sister, a member of the Seekers—in 9 BKC. Since he had fought alongside her adoptive father in the Vat Wars, Flux Master Sorel Vand went to Lyra's rescue on Daiyu, and, after evading the Dominion's hand on Mapuzo and Jabiim, Lyra was brought back to Aurelith. Years later, Lyra succeeded Davan by representing their homeworld in the Dominion Assembly, though secretly she supported the Uprising. During the Reach War, however, Aurelith was destroyed along with its inhabitants and the royal family by the Dominion's VC-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station, causing Voss to openly fight the New Order as one of the Free Reaches Coalition’s substantial heroes.

In the aftermath of the Battle of Sylvaneth, Voss married the Kestran smuggler Dorn Halek and learned the Fluxadept arts as her brother's apprentice until giving birth to her son, Orin Halek, on the day of the Dominion's capitulation in 5 AKC. Her personal and political life suffered in the years that followed, with Orin turning to the null side like his grandfather before him, adopting the persona of Voidsworn Draven, and the Concord Assembly sidelining Voss's career. Adopting the military rank of general, she led the Remnant and their efforts to oppose the rising Iron Ascendancy during the Cold War while seeking to make amends with Orin in hopes that her son will return to the lumen side of the Synth Flux like her father during the Battle of Sylvaneth. Despite her efforts, the Iron Ascendancy succeeded in destroying the New Concord, and Voss's husband died at the hands of their estranged son in 34 AKC. Voss continued to lead the Remnant during the war against the Iron Ascendancy, all the while passing on her Flux training onto Nara—a Flux-sensitive scavenger from Ashfall and Kael’s apprentice.

As her health declined, Voss used her remaining strength to reach her son, calling him back to the lumen side. Following her death on Ajan Kloss in 35 AKC, her final actions inspired Nara and the Remnant to defeat the forces of the reborn Kaelith Rau's Voidsworn Eternal on Nullreach and spark an uprising against the Iron Ascendancy across the galaxy. The heir to three inheritances, Lyra Velmor Voss Halek's legacy passed on to the Fluxadept Nara Velmor, the military leader Pax Darrow of the Remnant, and Orin Halek, her only child, who ultimately returned to the light before sacrificing his life for Nara and becoming one with the Synth Flux to join his mother and the Velmor family.

## Pre-birth

Toward the end of the Vat Wars, a galactic conflict fought between the Aetherium Concord and the League of Free Systems, also known as the Seperatists, that was engineered by the Voidsworn Lord Kaelith Rau, Flux Master Ordmund set out on a journey to discover the secrets of immortality. His journey took him to Nullsepulcher, the ancient Voidsworn homeworld, where he faced visions of the future. At the end of his journey, the Synth Flux Priestess, Serenity, who had guided his journey, told him that there was another Velmor, and Ordmund heard his voice from the future say so as well. These events not only alluded to the potential he would sense in Lyra, but to what Ordmund would tell her twin brother, Kael, about Lyra in his last words, before his death.

## Birth

Lyra Nalune Velmor was born on the asteroid of Bonefall two days after the end of the Vat Wars and the newly established Vanguard Dominion's purge of the Flux Order. She and her twin brother, Kael, who was born shortly before her, were the children of Concord Delegate Sera Nalune Solari, who died shortly after the births, and Flux Knight Aran Velmor, who had recently fallen to the null side of the Synth Flux. She was left with a lasting impression of images and feelings of her mother that her brother did not: that their mother was "very beautiful, kind, but sad", though Lyra was unsure if she had really seen her mother or if she had just invented the memories to fill the hole in her life.

Of the two Velmor children, Ordmund felt that Lyra was more suited to be a Fluxadept, owing to her natural strength, will, and clarity (as opposed to her brother, who was impetuous, headstrong, unruly, and inattentive), wanting very much to train her. However, to protect the children from the Dominion, Kaelith Rau, who ruled the Dominion as Galactic Sovereign Kaelith, and the now–Voidsworn Lord Xarn Velgor, Delegate Davan Esk Voss agreed to adopt the infant Lyra together with his wife, Queen Alen of Aurelith, while Sorel Vand personally watched over Kael, who would live with their father's stepbrother Bram Hollis and his wife Talia, on the planet Sorrhas.

## Princess of Aurelith

Through adoption, Lyra Nalune Velmor became Lyra Voss, formally becoming heiress of the Royal Family of Aurelith through the Name Day ceremony. Although it was common knowledge that she was adopted, and everyone assumed that she was just another of the many, many children left orphaned by the Vat Wars, she remained unaware of her biological parents' fates. When Lyra was an infant, she was mesmerized by the fish in an aquarium while her adoptive father read secret files concerning the former Fluxadept Initiate Ilyra Shen's presence on the agrarian moon of Raada. Davan later carried her after reading the files. On one occasion when Lyra was a toddler she escaped from her bath and ran wet and naked into the formal dining room interrupting a diplomatic dinner and causing WA-2V to chase after her.

Lyra was eventually told that her birth father had died during one of the last battles of the Vat Wars and that her birth mother had been injured and only lived long enough to give birth to her. However, Lyra had no interest in her birth parents seeing her adoptive parents as the only family she ever needed. Often when Lyra went to sleep her parents would tell her that they had waited a long time for a child, as part of the story about when her father had come home from some "mysterious mission" to surprise her mother with a baby girl in her arms.

As a small child, Lyra believed that the glowing coming from inside her mother's chest was a bouquet of blooming candlewick flowers. She would eventually learn that the glowing was from Alen's pulmonodes, which she had gotten after a near fatal climbing accident when she was sixteen, during her Challenge of the Body. She told Lyra that she had chosen not to cover them up with skin through vitacell sessions, as they were proof that she had survived. Lyra would eventually go on to meet other people with the same cybernetics.

Sometime after her fourth birthday, Lyra received her "big girl bed," which made her realize that she was too old to sleep in her parent's bed. Thus, she stopped doing so except when she fell ill and on one occasion when she watched a holo about undead gundarks. During her childhood, Lyra was taught by her father to appreciate the effort of Aurelith's workers, but also the importance of her duties as the planet's eventual queen and by her mother, that the life of royalty could be a very lonely and isolated one. She would often stand alongside her parents, waiting through Aurelith's endless royal ceremonies, knowing that she could let no emotion show on her face.
