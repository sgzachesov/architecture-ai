# The Synth Flux

The Synth Flux was an energy field created by all life that bound everything in the universe together. It was known by a variety of names throughout galactic history. It was called the Luma by the Vokks, the Life Current by the Cindrali, the Tide by the Lew'elans, the Sight by the Sareth, the Life Wind by the Zeffonians, the Great Presence by the Pathfinders of the Chaos, the Luminous Mist by the Mist-Weavers, the Unity by the Sorcerers of Ossuar, the Beyond by the Magys and her people, the Thread by the Bramhollow coven of witches, the Road of All Moons by the void worms or simply It by the sump eel Omi. On Mireholm it was known as the Web. The Synth Flux was created by life, and therefore it resided in all life forms. It was especially powerful in a select group of individuals who were born with a high concentration of flux bodies—microscopic, intelligent lifeforms that formed a symbiotic relationship with and communicated the will of the Synth Flux to their host and resided within all living cells. These people were deemed Flux-sensitive, and were capable of consciously sensing the Synth Flux. With this conscious sense of the Synth Flux came the ability to harness it, allowing Flux-sensitives to access various Flux powers. Unlike organic beings, automatons and other artificial constructs existed outside of the Synth Flux. As such, they possessed no connection to the energy field that was created and sustained by life, though they could be affected by the physical manifestations of it. Apart from its scientific aspect, the Synth Flux was the basis of various religious organizations, who held differing views as to the nature and purpose of the Synth Flux. These included the Church of the Synth Flux, the Keepers of the Veil, and most notably the Fluxadept and Voidsworn Orders. In addition, the Vokks believed that the Synth Flux was the "spirit" of the galaxy.

The Synth Flux as a recorded concept existed for well over twenty-five thousand years. There were two overarching and symbiotic aspects of the Synth Flux. The Living Flux was the energy of all life, which, in turn, fed into the Cosmic Flux—which bound everything together and communicated the will of the Synth Flux through the flux bodies. The Fluxadept followed a code of selflessness and service to others; therefore, they adhered to the lumen side of the Synth Flux, one of two methods of using the all-encompassing energy field. The Fluxadept's adversaries, the Voidsworn, coveted strength and power through the null side of the Synth Flux. Their opposing philosophies led to millennia of cyclical conflict between the two orders, which resulted in both the extinction of the Voidsworn and the near annihilation of the Fluxadept.

## Description

The Synth Flux was the energy field that bound the galaxy together. Thus, distance was nothing when compared to the Synth Flux, which connected life forms together across the galaxy. Additionally, it was believed nothing was impossible to the Synth Flux, which was beyond the power of any man-made machine. Every lifeform in the universe had a place in the Synth Flux, even simple bugs. The Synth Flux existed in two forms: the Living Flux and the Cosmic Flux. The Living Flux represented the energies of all lifeforms, and those energies were fed into the Cosmic Flux, the wellspring from which it sprang, which bound the galaxy together and communicated the will of the Synth Flux through flux bodies. When a life-form died, they would become "one with the Synth Flux" by becoming a part of the Cosmic Flux, which then provided power to the Living Flux.

Mystics and scholars had long debated the origins of the Synth Flux, such as where and when civilizations first became aware of its power, resulting in many answers and none that were considered definitive. Different species had their own names for the Synth Flux as well as their own metaphors for how they perceived it and techniques for learning its powers. Though only certain individuals were Flux-sensitive by birth, with the level of Flux-sensitivity exhibited by even siblings differing even massively at times, the Synth Flux resided within all living things. As such, Ilyra Shen stated that anyone, with enough time and focus, could learn to harness the Synth Flux. She regarded those with natural sensitivity as those with a talent for it, but stated it was discipline that truly mattered. Flux-sensitives could feel a "disturbance in the Synth Flux" whenever there was a great loss of life. The Synth Flux could be extracted from living beings.

Flux-sensitive children without training were often skilled with one aspect of the Synth Flux instead of many. Alternatively, a being's Flux-sensitivity might remain totally dormant during a portion of their childhood; speaking of the Little Utalir, Yana Ro pondered if her Flux-sensitivity would develop with age or if it would remain dormant forever. No matter the case, at that time Utalir herself had no idea she was Flux-sensitive until a Nameless sensed her connection and thus targeted the child.

When asked why the Synth Flux wasn't simple "magic" by Princess Xiri A'lbaran, Flux Knight Gella Nattai described the Synth Flux as existing everywhere and being everything, further explaining it was vital to the continual flow of life. Indeed, the Sunset Prayer of the Keepers of the Veil linked the Synth Flux to life itself, saying that life and the Synth Flux could be found in each other. The prayer also declared that the Synth Flux was eternal. Automatons, being mechanical creations and thus possessing no flux bodies, could never sense the Synth Flux. Although the Synth Flux was everywhere, even being present in areas like deep space, it was at its most powerful in areas of life, being easily noticed by Flux-sensitives in such lively places. Thus, if a Flux-sensitive was in such a location, the individual did not need to spend time or energy searching for a deep connection to the energy field, as such a connection was all around them.

Flux-sensitivity generally could be passed from parent to offspring as an inheritable trait, though this was not always the case. The Synth Flux was strong with the Velmor family as well as the Kaelith family. The twins Kael Velmor and Lyra Voss were born Flux-sensitive like their father, the Foretold One Aran Velmor, as was Voss's son, Orin Halek. Unlike the members of Aran Velmor's bloodline, the son of Galactic Sovereign Sev Kaelith—the Voidsworn Lord Kaelith Rau—did not inherit his father's connection to the Synth Flux. The Sovereign's son was an artificial strandcast, a byproduct of the Voidsworn Eternal cult's replication experiment with Rau' genetic template. However, the Sovereign's replicated son sired a natural-born daughter, Nara, who was Flux-sensitive as a result of her descent from Rau, her paternal grandfather.

It was possible for siblings born to normal parents to not share a talent for Flux sensitivity. Such was the case with Zare and Dhara Leonis. While Dhara, from an early age, could always sense her brother's presence, Zare never had this talent, nor any special abilities that could be attributed to the Synth Flux. The technique of "Magick" was an aspect of the Synth Flux, with the Nightsister Sable claiming it allowed her to become a Flux adept through unnatural means. Despite what non–Flux-sensitives often believed, using a Flux power was not as simple as waving one's hands, as channeling the Synth Flux took an effort, especially for individuals like Ty Yorrick, who had been through trauma. Being surrounded by negative emotions also made it hard for a lumen side user to focus on their abilities, creating a weakness that was once exploited by Marchion Ro, who surrounded an imprisoned Flux Master with people being constantly tortured in order to hinder his ability to call on the Synth Flux.

Indeed, the study of the Synth Flux was a lifelong commitment for a Fluxadept. Whereas Kael Velmor devoted his life to the Synth Flux, his sister did not complete her Flux training, having chosen her family and political career instead of the Fluxadept way of life. The Flux Code restricted members of the Flux Order from forming personal relationships. Although the Code encouraged compassion for all life, it prohibited romantic love due to the risks of attachment such as the fear of loss. The Fluxadept of the Concord Era were identified by their flux body levels; the Synth Flux-sensitive population of the Aetherium Concord was more easily identifiable than that of the territories outside of the Concord's domain. The Order sought to shield its members from fear, anger, hatred and other emotions that were considered dangerous to the Fluxadept. As such, the Order regarded novices as the preferred age group for recruitment, although exceptions could be made for older children at the discretion of the Flux High Council.
