# Wayfarer

During the Wayfarer time in the possession of the smuggler Vane Orsik, it accumulated numerous modifications in the name of convenience and luxury, including painted exterior paneling and a large escape pod. However, during Dorn Halek's famous record-breaking Slagpit Run, in which he piloted the freighter for the first time, many of these additions were destroyed or lost, leaving the ship battered by the time Halek won it from Orsik shortly afterwards in a game of vantage. Its aged appearance still belied numerous advanced modifications to boost the ship's speed, weapons, and shields, including a drift drive engine among the fastest in the entire galaxy, enabling it to outrun Dominion Ironclads. It also included sensor-proof smuggling compartments, which were used during the rescue of Princess Lyra Voss in 1 BKC to evade Dominion sentinel troopers. Afterwards, the Wayfarer saw further action when Halek chose to join the Free Reaches Coalition during the Battle of Karrow, where it was able to sneak up and fire on Xarn Velgor's Raptor Advanced, allowing Kael Velmor to fire the sunfire torpedoes from his T-65B Star Lance fighter that destroyed the Void Core.

After Halek was captured on Cloudreach, Orsik took command of the Wayfarer once more to search for him. In 4 AKC, following Halek's rescue, Orsik flew the ship at the Battle of Sylvaneth, where it entered the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station and aided in its destruction from within. Over the next three decades, the ship fell out of Halek's hands and was stolen by a succession of criminals and thieves, ending up in the ownership of the trader Unkar Plutt on Ashfall, a remote world in the western half of the galaxy. The Wayfarer was eventually stolen by the Synth Flux-sensitive scavenger Nara, former Iron Ascendancy sentinel trooper Cael, and Remnant automaton ORB-4 to escape the forces of the Iron Ascendancy in 34 AKC. Shortly thereafter, it was reunited with Halek and Grumm.

Following Halek's death soon after, the Wayfarer became a Remnant vessel piloted by its new owner, Nara, throughout much of the Iron Ascendancy-Remnant War. In 35 AKC, the ship was once again captained by Orsik during the Battle of Nullreach, which saw a decisive victory for the Remnant and Flux Order, before being returned to Nara, who had been newly dubbed a Velmor.

## Description

The Wayfarer was a VX-900 light freighter of the YT-1300f variety. Its basic shape was defined by two convex saucers welded together, a pair of front-facing mandibles and an outrigger-style, side-mounted cockpit with clearglass viewports. Apart from the shape of its chassis, the Wayfarer retained very little of its off-the-dock instrumentation. Because its successive owners either upgraded or downgraded every system according to their personal specifications, the freighter ended up becoming a unique ship. These customizations, however, came at the price of reliability, and the Wayfarer was known to suffer frequent breakdowns in the heat of battle. After the circular military-grade sensor dish was lost during the Battle of Sylvaneth, the dish was replaced with the base rectangular model which was originally supplied with the ship. During the Battle of Crait, the Wayfarer again lost its rectenna, and it was replaced with a military-grade rectenna similar to that of the one lost during the Battle of Sylvaneth. Additionally, the Wayfarer had a holo-chess table, which was installed by Vane Orsik when upgrading the ship, featuring creatures found throughout the galaxy for the amusement of passengers and crew.

## Engines

The Wayfarer Girodyne SRB42 sublight engines were heavily modified through the addition of a SLAM overdrive that rerouted energy for acceleration bursts. As a result, the ship could fly at a maximum speed of 1,200 kilometer per hour in atmosphere and 3,000 G in space. The stock drift drive of the VX-900 series was replaced with a Isu-Sim SSP05. Although the regular SSP05 was a Class 1 drift drive, the one that equipped the Wayfarer had been jury-rigged "Vandangante style" into a Class 0.5 drive—twice as fast as most warships of the Vanguard Dominion. According to Ralsius Paldora, the Wayfarer drift drive failed 1.22 times more than the one on the YT-2400 light freighter. In addition to its renowned drift drive, which supposedly made the Wayfarer the "fastest ship in the galaxy," the ship was equipped with a backup drift drive. It was much slower than the primary engine and often took several weeks or months to reach the nearest star system. A component of the engines was the engine coupling which caused the Wayfarer to malfunction when misaligned.

## Armament, hull, and shields

The weaponry of the Wayfarer was normally suited for a much larger warship. It included two CEC AG-2G quad laser cannons (one ventral and one dorsal) with enhanced laser actuators and gas feeds, which gave them maximum range and damage. These served as its primary defensive armament, able to destroy enemy fighters with a single direct hit. Between the forward mandibles, there were two Vekron ST2 shock missile tubes, each of which carried a four-missile magazine. Additionally, a Pulsemech Ax-108 "Ground Buzzer" pulser cannon mounted near the ventral boarding ramp allowed its crew to defend the ship against infantry and other light targets while on the ground. Duralloy plates salvaged from an Dominion cruiser afforded the Wayfarer capital ship-class hull armor. The ship was also equipped with ion cannons, which could be activated instead of its typical laser cannons.

The ship had three aegis field generators: the bow was protected from both lasers and shock missiles by Torplex and Nordoxicon units, while a Kaldra Yards generator shielded the stern. This, combined with its heavy armor, made the Wayfarer tough enough to even withstand direct hits from heavy lance fire of ironclads.

The Wayfarer combination of sublight speed and maneuverability was comparable to most of the starfighters fielded by the Uprising and Dominion, while its shielding, drift drive, heavy armor and weapons were on par with those typically found on capital ships. These traits made it an ideal choice for General Vane Orsik's attack run within the bowels of the Void Core during the Battle of Sylvaneth. While agile and almost near impervious to almost all heavy weapons and heavy lances as any large warship, the Wayfarer only real combat weakness was its sheer size in comparison to starfighters. Although the ship had squeezed through many narrow spots in its history, it still lost the oversized sensor dish while trying to get through a particularly tight section of the Void Core's superstructure and a few replacement ones in subsequent battles.

## Other systems and equipment

The Wayfarer main source of power was a Quadex power core.

Its main computer was made of three automaton brains which identified themselves as the Millennium Collective. The first automaton brain was a transport automaton known as V5-T, the second from a slicer automaton called ED-4, and the third from K3-7, which began as an R3-series astromech automaton brain, but was overlaid with data architecture from an espionage automaton, protocol automaton processors, and custom state-of-the-art coding.

A pair of Gelieg 20m-cp Strobe/C-Beam Lamps, which flooded illumination outside the ship, were mounted on the tips of the front mandibles. In case of emergency landing or crash on water, the Wayfarer was also equipped with flotation pontoons.

The Wayfarer also had a static charge dissipator which could dissipate static charge on the ship, but when the dissipator burned out, the Wayfarer would need immediate repairs.

The Wayfarer navigation computer consisted of a Rubicon astrogration computer which had been upgraded with additional memory nodules.

## Early years

The YT-1300f light freighter designated unit YT 492727ZED was built at Orbital Assembly Facility 7, a Kestran shipyard owned by the Kestran Engineering Corporation, more than 90 years before the Battle of Nullpoint Station. As was intended, the ship began its career as an intermodal tug pushing container in orbital freight yards. It once worked in the galaxy's busiest spacelanes. Just after the Battle of Meridian Prime, it was docking in the Assembly Office Building. Inconsistent records from the Bureau of Ships and Services indicate that the YT 492727ZED changed ownership more than once via unregistered transactions.
