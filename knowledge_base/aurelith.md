# Aurelith

Aurelith, located in the Core Systems, was a terrestrial planet covered with mountains. During the waning decades of the Aetherium Concord, it was ruled by Queen Alen Voss and represented in the Concord Assembly by her husband, Delegate Davan Esk Voss. Aurelith was regarded for its natural beauty, its tranquility, and the sophistication of its arts and culture.

Following the rise of Sev Kaelith's Vanguard Dominion, Aurelith played a pivotal role in the establishment of the Free Reaches Coalition, a movement that sought to restore the Old Concord's values, and was one of its earliest supporters. Because of its ties with the Coalition, the planet was destroyed when High Warden Ostren Vail decided to test the core lance of the Void Core, a moon-sized battle station developed by the Dominion, at full strength. Among the few survivors of that disaster was Princess Lyra Voss, adopted daughter of Davan and Alen Voss and heiress to the royal house of Aurelith.

## Description

From space, the planet of Aurelith, located in the star system of the same name within the galaxy's Core Systems, appeared as a blue-green orb enveloped in a white web of clouds. Its surface had vast bodies of water and was covered in snow-capped mountains, with patches of green grassy hills. Aurelith was known to the galaxy as "the planet of beauty." Aurelians were very artistic and lived for this beauty, but the planet still hid treacherous cliffs and forgotten crevasses. Famous natural landmarks of Aurelith included the Cloudshape Falls, the Glarus Lagoons, and the Isatabith rain forest.

A variety of grapevines grew on Aurelith, which notably allowed to produce Toniray and emerald wine. The local fauna included the Aurelian wolf-cat, nerfs, and a species of white-winged bird.

The cities on Aurelith were carefully designed to respect the natural beauty of their environment. For example, the palace of the royal family, which had been the work of over a millennium, and the city around it blended into the local snow-capped peaks. Aurelian architecture made use of white synthstone, which inspired the architecture of certain mining economies of the Outer Reach Territories, like the floating colony of Skyhold, and the planets Farrow and Garel.

Despite the lyrics of the traditional Aurelian lullaby "Mirrorbright" referencing a moon, the planet never possessed any natural satellites.

## The Old Concord

The peaceful world of Aurelith was among the oldest members of the Aetherium Concord, the democratic union that governed most of the galaxy, and was among the worlds that originally founded it. Upon the formation of its monarchy it was established that the heir apparent to Aurelith must complete a Challenge of the Heart, Challenge of the Mind and Challenge of the Body which would be announced on their Day of Demand and upon completion they would be officially invested as heir. In ancient times, Aurelian heirs who partook in their Day of Demand had to fight their way into the palace throne room. Before they permanently settled on Meridian Prime, the Flux High Council was known to have a council on Aurelith. Aurelith at one point had a neo-classical period, which produced unique architecture. The Eastwind Manor of House Esk was built in the neo-classical style.

Figuring that she would like the world, Yana Ro wished to visit Aurelith after the Night of Sorrow and finally breaking her ties with the Path of the Open Hand cult. Ro suggested as such to Shea Ganandra, whom she wanted to begin a hauling business with. During the High Concord Era, Aurelith was a prosperous world and, as a signatory of the Concord Defense Coalition treaties, was required to lend starships and personnel to be commanded by Concord military officers during times of crisis. These ships and personnel would then be returned to Aurelith after the crisis ended. Aurelith was called upon to fulfill this obligation in 232 BKC, to aid the Concord in fighting the Hollow, a group of marauders who caused the Great Driftspace Disaster.

At some point between 40 BKC and 28 BKC, the heir apparent of Aurelith, sixteen year old Princess Alen Voss, took part in her Day of Demand where she announced her challenges of the heart, mind and body. However during her Challenge of the Body Alen fell from the famous Alderaaian mountain Appenza Peak and would have died had it not been for the quick action of the guards watching from a distance. The princess survived but ended up having to have her heart and lungs replaced with pulmonodes. After her fall Alen was officially invested as crown princess and heir to the throne of Aurelith. Years later the people would still speak of Alen's accident.

Alen Voss eventually succeeded her mother as queen of Aurelith.

By the time of the Invasion of Calustra, Aurelith had not seen conflict in generations. The planet was represented in the Concord Assembly by Delegate Davan Corrin. That year, the then-First Minister of the Concord, Finis Halden, was ousted from office, and Corrin was nominated for the position in the resulting election. However, the Aurelian delegate was defeated by Delegate Sev Kaelith, who represented the sovereign system of Calustra.

In 28 BKC, former queen of Calustra Delegate Sera Nalune and her entourage were invited to Aurelith by Queen Alen and stayed at the royal palace for a week before returning home to Calustra.

Ten years later, Aurelith was represented in the Assembly by Viceroy Davan Esk Voss, the prince consort of the planet's ruler, Queen Alen Voss. At that time, the Concord was facing a secessionist crisis due to the existence of the League of Free Systems led by Magister Volen, pushing the galaxy to the brink of war. Through Voss, Aurelith was a member of First Minister Kaelith's Loyalist Committee, which concerned itself with the stability of the Concord during the Secessionist crisis and would later strive to limit Kaelith's increasing executive authority. That year, an army of vatborn was discovered on the planet Tidewell, which would be appropriated by the Assembly in order to combat the Secessionists. To save the Concord from the impending threat, the First Minister was granted emergency powers, a decision that was contrary to the pacifist values of Aurelith and its representative and which ultimately led to the outbreak of the Vat Wars. During the Vat Wars Viceroy Davan Voss became Aurelith's military leader.

At some point during the Vat Wars, Aurelith hosted a conference concerned with aiding war refugees. Sera Nalune, then delegate of Calustra, was nearly assassinated during the conference when the Ollag crime lord Ziro, whom Nalune had sent to prison, hired the bounty hunter Aurra Quell to take revenge. The plot, however, was thwarted by the Fluxadept apprentice Ilyra Shen. Many refugees from all over the Concord came to stay on Aurelith during the war to find their way again or to stay.

Queen Alen and Viceroy Davan would send aid to worlds that had been ravaged by the Secessionists, sending not just money and supplies but also doctors, teachers, architects, builders, medics, and automatons.

Around the time of the Battle of Kadavo, two Aurelian tourists visiting Sorrhas died in a skimmer-bike accident. The Chainfolk News implied that the two had gotten intoxicated in the cantina of Dustgate, which caused the accident.

The Vat Wars saw a change in Aurelith. Although it was still a peaceful planet with no large weaponry, the Royal Aurelith Civil Fleet had "more teeth," their officers had seen more hardship, they manufactured more practical items in larger quantities, and the planet had begun to quietly fill up due to the number of refugees.

Following the end of the Vat Wars, Viceroy Davan and Queen Alen adopted Lyra Nalune Velmor, the daughter of the now deceased Sera Nalune and former Flux Knight turned Voidsworn Lord Aran Velmor, renaming her to Lyra Voss and making her princess of Aurelith. Lyra's adoption was announced on her Name Day three or four days after her birth and was publicly celebrated on Aurelith.
