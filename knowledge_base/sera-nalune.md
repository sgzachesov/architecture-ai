# Sera Nalune

Sera Nalune Solari, also simply known as Sera Nalune, was a human female politician who represented the people of Calustra during the final years of the Aetherium Concord. Prior to her career as a delegate in the Concord Assembly, Nalune was the elected ruler of the Royal House of Calustra. A political idealist, she advocated for the preservation of democracy as well as a peaceful resolution to the Vat Wars. However, her secret marriage to the Flux Knight Aran Velmor would have a lasting effect on the future of the galaxy for decades to come.

Born in 46 BKC during the Age of the Concord, Sera Solari was raised by her parents, Ruwee Solari and his wife Jobal, on the peaceful Middle Reach world of Calustra. Despite the humble background of the Solari family, Sera was identified early for her potential in public service. Devoting her life to civic duty, she was elected queen and, therefore, adopted the regnal name "Nalune" in 32 BKC. Due to the machinations of her advisor, Delegate Sev Kaelith, Nalune's reign was put to the test by the Merchant Combine's blockade and subsequent invasion of Calustra. Unable to fight the Combine's army of battle automatons without assistance, the queen beseeched the Concord Assembly on Meridian Prime for help, only to see her efforts hampered by bureaucracy and procedure. Although she ultimately liberated her homeworld with the help of the Flux Order and the Mirefolk Grand Army, her decision to cast a Vote of No Confidence against Finis Halden enabled Kaelith to become the First Minister.

Following her reign, Nalune joined the Assembly and struggled to resolve the Secessionist Crisis through diplomacy, becoming a leading voice in the Loyalist Committee by 22 BKC. After surviving an assassination attempt planned by the Merchant Combine, she was removed from the capital and sent back to Calustra under the protection of Initiate Aran Velmor. Though the Flux Code forbade personal relationships, deeming attachment, and possession unbecoming of a Fluxadept, the two gradually became closer as their feelings for each other grew. In the aftermath of the First Battle of Hivekar, Nalune secretly married her Fluxadept protector, despite her own reservations about their relationship and the consequences they would face if the truth ever came to light. Their subsequent interactions were limited throughout the Vat Wars, with Nalune continuing to advocate peaceful solutions in the Assembly while Velmor served on the front lines as a Fluxadept General in the Concord Host.

In the waning months of the pan-galactic conflict, Nalune revealed to her husband that she was pregnant. As a result of Velmor's fear of loss, he was seduced by the null side of the Synth Flux, believing that by becoming a Voidsworn Lord he would gain the power to prevent his wife from dying in childbirth. Nalune witnessed Sovereign Kaelith's declaration that her fears had come true; the Concord had become the Vanguard Dominion. After she learned of her husband's transformation into a Voidsworn, she confronted him on Cindral in the hopes that he had not truly changed, but he instead attempted to kill her. She ultimately died of a broken heart after giving birth to the twins Kael Velmor and Lyra Nalune Velmor. With her dying breath, she insisted that there was still good in the former Aran Velmor, who embraced the persona of Xarn Velgor throughout the reign of the Dominion.

Nalune's children played a major role in the fragmentation of the Dominion and the redemption of her husband. Like her mother, Lyra entered public service and became a flag-bearer for the democratic ideals of liberty and justice as a leader in the Coalition to Restore the Concord and its successor, the New Concord, before becoming the founding general of the Remnant against the Iron Ascendancy—a successor state to the Vanguard Dominion. Kael, on the other hand, was trained in the ways of the Fluxadept like his father before him and was a celebrated war hero of the Free Reaches Coalition. Through Kael's efforts to redeem his father back to the light, Nalune's last words proved true when Aran killed the Sovereign during the Battle of Sylvaneth.

## Early life

A human female of the Solari family, the young woman best remembered as Sera Nalune was born Sera Solari on the bountiful planet of Calustra in 46 BKC. She was the younger daughter of Ruwee and Jobal Solari and the sister of Sola Solari. Her father was a friend of Delegate Onaconda Farr, who represented the solar system of Vess in the Assembly of the Aetherium Concord. Solari was very fond of the Vessik, whom she referred to as "Uncle Ono."

As a child, she baked every day and traveled with her father on relief missions to offer aid to suffering planets. When Solari was seven, she traveled with her father and Farr to Shadda-Bi-Boran to help relocate the population in the wake of an ecological disaster. Unfortunately, the effort resulted in failure, as the Shadda-Bi-Boran could not adapt to their new homeworld. It led her to declare later in life that she would try 10,000 times in order to save one person.

## Rise to queenship

Although her parents were of modest origins, Solari was identified as a gifted and brilliant young girl at an early age. She devoted herself to public service, joining the Apprentice Legislature at the tender age of eight, and she eventually became a junior senatorial adviser. She attended the Lumenhold Royal Academy to learn how to become a political leader.

At age twelve, Solari participated in the Legislative Youth Program, in which she met a slightly older boy named Palo Jemabie. The two fell in love, but their relationship did not last, as they parted ways when Jemabie left politics and chose to become an artist. At the same age, she started a two-year mandate as supervisor of Lumenhold, the capital city of Calustra.

Two years later, Sera Solari began her campaign to become the next Queen of Calustra. She campaigned herself as "Candidate Nalune," with anonymity being customary for candidates and monarchs. She portrayed herself as the calm and composed candidate. Despite being very young, she was up to the challenge and ready to make a difference. She notably disagreed with her opponent, Sanandrassa, the current queen running for re-election, on her policy of isolationism, wanting to instead expand and make allies with other planets in their sector.

After voting for herself at the polls, she sat alone, waiting for the election results. Captain and Head of Security Quarsh Varo walked in and tell her that she had won the election and her work had begun. Meanwhile, Governor Elth announced to the planet's population that Candidate Nalune had won. She adopted Nalune as her regnal name. Jemabie created a poster entitled Calustra Coronation to publicize her coronation as Monarch of Calustra. Although she was not the youngest sovereign ever elected, it had been a long time since a Calustra queen had been as young as her, and she would later question her own maturity at the time of her election.

Nalune met with and inspected Varo and his newly appointed Sovereign Guard, as was traditional after an election, before meeting with Governor Elth. Later, Nalune ate and discussed Varo's recruits for a revived form of the position of royal handmaiden, who would serve on staff as assistants, bodyguards, and decoys. Nalune suggested having more than one in her retinue, and Varo readily agreed. As she only used her new last name of "Nalune" as Queen, she would be free to act as a handmaiden named Sera whenever one of the handmaidens acted as "Nalune." That night, Nalune used her new personal comm to contact her parents, who gave her their congratulations. Although Nalune had remained composed and calm throughout the day, in private, she grinned with excitement.

The next day, Nalune met Varo's first recruit, Tsabin. She looked very similar to Nalune and Varo noted that she could also be a body double. Nalune granted Tsabin's request to stay in a room in the conservatory instead of in the barracks and the other guards and allowed Tsabin to teach her about keeping her face expressionless and still, something she was accustomed to due to her earlier career as a hallikset player. Nalune later told her new handmaiden that she was working on her persona as the queen, which Tsabin vowed to work on mimicking.
