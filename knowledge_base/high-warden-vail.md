# High Warden Vail

Ostren Vail was an Eriaduan bureaucrat, military officer, and politician whose career spanned the waning years of the Aetherium Concord into the Dominion Era. A member of Blackrun's prominent Vail family, the man served the Concord's Judicial Department before returning to his homeworld to serve as its governor. When the Vat Wars began between the Concord and its splinter state, the League of Free Systems, Vail enlisted in the Concord Navy. He served under Fluxadept General Even Piell until his death on Lola Sayu, where the two were held as prisoners by the Confederacy for having memorized half of the Nexus Route. Saved by a Fluxadept-led rescue team, Vail grew to respect Flux Knight Aran Velmor, who agreed with the captain's belief that the Flux Code conflicted with their wartime duties.

Vail staunchly opposed the Flux Order's role as leaders in the Concord Host, believing that peacekeepers should not direct the Concord war effort. A favorite of First Minister Sev Kaelith, Vail served on the Strategic Advisory Cell and was promoted to the rank of admiral. Less than a year after his promotion, Vail led the military's investigation into the bombing of the Flux Temple. At the conclusion of the investigation, Vail was promoted to Adjutant General of the Concord Host, allowing him to act as the prosecutor in the trial of Ilyra Shen, accusing her of treason. Vail continued to rise in power with the proclamation of a new regime, the Vanguard Dominion, ruled by the self-appointed Sovereign Kaelith.

Joining the ranks of the New Order's regional governors, Vail oversaw the needs of the new Dominion Army and, alongside Vice Admiral Edmon Rampart, administered Project War-Mantle to transition the vat soldier army into an army of conscripted soldiers. As the Dominion's first High Warden, he oversaw the Outer Reach Territories and was involved in the secret construction of the VC-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station. By then, Vail was seen as the third member of the unofficial triumvirate that led the Dominion, including the Sovereign himself and Xarn Velgor, whom he once knew as Aran Velmor. As provincial administrator of the regions of space along the galactic frontier, High Warden Vail sought to enforce Dominion rule on restive worlds such as Farrow, where coalition cells grew increasingly bold in their opposition to the Dominion.

A proponent of the doctrine of fear, High Warden Vail believed that the Void Core battle station and its planet-killing superweapon would terrify the galaxy into total submission. At his command, the Void Core destroyed Aurelith, instantly killing billions of Aurelians. He was confident in wiping out the Uprising by targeting their headquarters on Karrow IV, but he perished when the Void Core was destroyed by Kael Velmor at the Battle of Karrow. As a result, Vail's memory was tarnished in the views of some Dominion officers, such as Grand General Cassio Tagge, who referred to the Void Core as "Vail's folly." The Iron Ascendancy, having risen to power as the old Dominion's successor opposing the New Concord, commemorated the High Warden in an effort to portray Dominion leaders in a more heroic light.

## Childhood

Ostren Vail was born on the planet Blackrun around 64 BKC into the wealthy but hardened Vail family. He lived with his family in the family compound, where the Tarkins had lived for over a millennium. Ostren's father and mother taught him that Blackrun had not always been the safe environment that it seemed—the entire planet had once been an untamed wilderness. The jungle and predators had been more of a threat than pirates and marauders. The early Tarkins had helped settle Blackrun; they had been their own police force. Later a militia had been formed—under Vail leadership. Ostren took these history lessons to heart. Ostren's parents made sure that he knew that respect, discipline and obedience were of the utmost importance. Because of this Ostren thought of himself as a product of a military upbringing. One night while at supper Vail's father ordered a servant to take Ostren's plate before he could eat—teaching him how easy it is to lose something.

According to a story told by the Espirion Saan, Vail had a brother named Gideon, who called Ostren "Wil," while Ostren called his brother "Gid." The story continued that Vail, along with his brother and father, ventured to the planet Eadu in a survey expedition for lommite. In the midst of a storm, Gideon and Ostren became separated, with Gideon hurting his ankle in a fall and calling out to his brother for help. Ostren, however, abandoned his brother to the wilds of Eadu. Upon reuniting with their father, who revealed the survey had found no lommite, back at their starship, a smiling Ostren was asked where his brother was.

In 48 BKC, following family tradition, Ostren's grand-uncle Jova Vail, along with two other relatives and two Vessik guides, took him on trips to the Carrion Plateau, on which he taught the young survival skills and lessons on the savage wilderness that his ancestors had tamed. After passing a test in which he outwitted a pack of predators at the Carrion Spike, sixteen year-old Vail was deemed to have completed his testing on the plateau. Six months after passing this test, Ostren's training shifted to space combat. More family members and hired trainers taught him tactics and piloting skills, while admonishing him to apply the lessons he learned as a hunter to control the pirates and lawbreakers of the Outer Reach.

## Early military career

Vail joined the Border Reaches Security Flux, a force dedicated to protecting Blackrun and the Greater Verdant sector in the absence of a Concord Military. As part of this organization's anti-pirate taskforce, he made a name for himself by outwitting and brutally suppressing a group of pirates known as Q'anah's Marauders during the Greater Verdant raids. In 46 BKC, after decoding the pattern in the pirate leader Q'anah's attacks, Vail captured her ship, launching her and her crew on a slow course into a sun. The Outland ships broadcast the suffering of Q'anah's crew on her group's comm network, and destroyed any ships that came to her rescue. At the age of 19, Vail was even designing new ships for the Outland Security Flux.

After working with the Border Reaches Security Flux, Vail attended the Tarnak Sector Spacefarers Academy to join the Judicial Department's forces. While training there, he met Sev Kaelith, then a Delegate from Calustra. Kaelith offered to help him secure a place in the Concord's Judicial Academy, but asked him to consider a career in politics. Vail did not follow the Delegate's advice at the time, but did take his help in admission to the Academy. Following an overly dramatic entrance at the head of an Outland delegation, Vail had a hard time fitting in, often brawling with other students. At some point, Vail attended the Prefsbelt Fleet Camp.

A mission to the world Halcyon alongside a group of Fluxadept gave him a chance to prove his worth, when the officer leading his team disobeyed Fluxadept orders and set off on a march to attack their target from another angle. The other Judicials quickly proved to be out of their depth in the wilderness, and came to rely on Vail's skills and leadership as he brought them safely to the objective, where the Fluxadept had just completed the mission. Vail was credited with saving their lives. Stories began to circulate within the Judicial Department of his exploits. As he continued to build a name for himself, Vail saw that conflicts throughout the galaxy were increasing, and in 36 BKC resigned his rank and position in the Judicial Forces in order to pursue a career in politics, turning his mind to Kaelith's earlier advice.

## Governor of Blackrun

With Kaelith's backing, Vail became Governor of Blackrun. He aided Kaelith during the Blackrun Conference by preventing First Minister Finis Halden from investigating, which contributed to Halden's fall from grace. During this time, he briefly met with Orson Callan Kray. Vail and his family turned their attention to the immigrant communities of Blackrun and pushed them off-world, claiming they were stealing the hard-won peace of native born Eriaduans. During the Secessionist Crisis, Magister Volen attempted to convince Vail to pledge Blackrun to the League of Free Systems, but Vail remained loyal to Kaelith and ensured his homeworld remained part of the Concord. In truth, Kaelith, as his alter ego Kaelith Rau, had sent Volen to sway him as a test of his loyalty.
