# Rhen Kord

Rhen Kord was a human male crime lord and former bounty hunter whose career spanned decades, from the fall of the Aetherium Concord to the end of the rule of the Vanguard Dominion. Originally code-named Alpha, he was an unaltered vatborn of the famed Riftguard bounty hunter, Tarek Kord, who raised the boy as his son. Rhen emulated his father and genetic donor by wearing a customized suit of Riftguard armor. His personal starship was the Bloodbeak, a Firespray-31-class patrol and attack craft that once belonged to Tarek. Trained in combat and martial skills from a young age, Kord was one of the most feared bounty hunters in the galaxy during the reign of Sovereign Kaelith. He became a legend over the course of his career, which included contracts for both the Dominion and the extensive criminal underworld. After living among the Sandwraiths of Sorrhas for a time, he took over as Daimyo of Emberport during the era of the New Concord.

Rhen was created on the Far Expanse planet of Tidewell as a result of an arrangement between the Voidsworn Lord Volen and Tarek Kord, a Riftguard foundling who served as the template of the Concord Host. Unlike the vat soldiers grown from Tarek's DNA, Tarek regarded Rhen as his son. In accordance with certain Riftguard traditions, Rhen was also considered a foundling by virtue of his descent from Tarek, whose chain code documented his participation in the Riftguard Civil War and his ownership of the armor later inherited by Rhen. During the last days of the Secessionist Crisis, Rhen and Tarek retreated to Hivekar where the latter was killed in battle by Flux Master Jarek Thane. Orphaned by Tarek's death, Rhen sought vengeance by attempting to assassinate Thane, although his plan proved unsuccessful. After serving time in prison on Meridian Prime, Kord took up his late father's profession as a bounty hunter, working alongside other mercenaries, such as Trask, Gorm, and Mara Vayne, while also notably apprenticing himself to his father's infamous brief protégée, Cad Korr.

In the aftermath of the Vat Wars, Kord continued to make a name for himself while working for the gangster Ollag Desilijic Tiure, as well as the Voidsworn Lord Xarn Velgor. During the Reach War, the bounty hunter managed to track down Captain Dorn Halek to Cloudreach on a contract for the Vanguard Dominion, hoping to deliver him to Ollag's Keep on Sorrhas. During the journey, Kord stopped on the moon of Nar Shaddaa in order to make sure Halek would survive his time in stasis amalgam, but he lost the smuggler while he was away as part of the return of the Scarlet Dawn, kicking off the War of the Bounty Hunters. Ultimately, Kord regained Halek and delivered him to the impatient Ollag.

Kord tried to prevent Halek's rescue by the Coalition to Restore the Concord, only to fall into the Great Pit of Vorr when the smuggler accidentally slammed a pole into the bounty hunter's jetpack, causing it to malfunction. Although Kord survived his ordeal with the man-eating maw Lurker, his armor was sold to Cobb Vanth by Scrapper scavengers. Following a time in the company of a Sandwraith tribe, Kord eventually regained his armor by tracking down and confronting the Riftguard warrior Kest Varn on Tyth, who had taken Kord's armor from Vanth. After helping Varn to rescue the foundling Pip from the clutches of Warden Gideon, the ex-bounty-hunter returned to Sorrhas where he killed Bib Ordo and claimed the late Ollag's throne as Daimyo, with Fennec Shand by his side. Now the head of his own gotra, Kord intended to rule the territories of Emberport by winning the people's respect, though he would face several major challenges to his position owed to the two-facedness of mayor Mok Shaiz such as the Twins' bid for power and the expansion of the Vess Syndicate's spice trade. Ultimately, though, Kord overcame all obstacles - including a confrontation with his old mentor, Cad Korr - and emerged victorious with the help of his associates, gaining the appreciation and approval of Emberport's denizens.

## Creation and life on Tidewell

Around 33 BKC, an extensive vat army was secretly commissioned on Tidewell by Flux Master Coran Seth, who believed it would be necessary in order to ensure the protection of the Aetherium Concord. The army, however, was later taken over by the Voidsworn in 32 BKC shortly after the Battle of Calustra and was used as a plot to overthrow the Concord and destroy the Flux Order. Prior to the creation process, Riftguard bounty hunter Tarek Kord was recruited by the Voidsworn Lord Volen. Tarek was to be the genetic template for the vat army. Tarek agreed to become the genetic template for a considerable payment, as well as one request: the creation of a vatborn who did not have the same genetic modifications, such as behavioral conditioning and growth acceleration. The Tideweller scientists agreed and created a boy codenamed "Alpha."

Although he was born a vatborn, Alpha regarded Tarek as his father, who likewise viewed the boy as his son. Soon after referred to as Rhen, he was the very first of many vatborn modeled on the Tarek Kord template, but was only one of two known vatborn that were pure genetic copies of the original Kord template, the other being Omega.

For the next ten years, Rhen was raised on Tidewell by his father, primarily within Tipoca City and occasionally accompanied Tarek on missions to track down countless bounties. On one night, Rhen awoke to see the Bloodbeak flying off, watching his father leave on one of his missions. Despite the isolation of the cities on Tidewell he was raised in, the young Kord enjoyed the combat training his father administered to him and danger-filled life provided by the man who raised him. Tarek taught his replicated son many of the skills he had learned as a bounty hunter, so Rhen learned survival and martial skills and became proficient with a pulser as a child. He also underwent vat soldier training and became familiar with members of the Tideweller government. From a young age, Rhen also learned to pilot his father's ship, the Bloodbeak, including learning how to command and operate its weapon systems.

## Trained by Tarek Kord

For the boy's first mission in 23 BKC, Rhen and Tarek Kord alongside Neelda, Rinn, and Tiver were hired by the father of a female Lekara to capture his daughter and bring her back alive. The bounty hunters were surprised that a young boy would be joining them, but they knew not to anger Rhen's father and agreed to it. While on the way to the job, Rhen asked Tarek why they even needed a crew for the job and wished they went with a more famous bounty hunter like Cad Korr. Tarek replied that the crew was the unknown factor which was Rhen's lesson for the job.

Rhen and the other bounty hunters waited at a cantina on Waypoint to strike while the Lekara woman and her Falleen lover Griph were nearby on a balcony. When Griph told her she wasn't in the Umbra Syndicate cartel yet, Rhen stopped him, happy to know he was not part of the Umbra Syndicate. Rhen ordered them not to move, but Griph ditched his lover and ran away, causing the Lekara to almost stumble over. The bounty hunters ordered the woman to stop moving, but the Lekara tripped and fell over the edge of the balcony, but was rescued by Tarek.

While on the way back to the ship, Rhen asked his father how he knew the woman would fall. Tarek replied he didn't, but knew it was possible and advised Rhen to trust his instincts. He also told Rhen that desperate people would make ill decisions. Right then, Tiver knocked Rhen down and held him at hostage while Rinn told Tarek to give him the woman and the ship and in exchange for Rhen's life. But Tarek refused, to the surprise of the bounty hunters who were going to gut Rhen. Tarek then told them that they could try and walked away. Rhen then knocked over Tiver and gave her a lesson about searching her prisoners. Rhen proceeded to kill Tiver and Gand. Neelda complemented Rhen afterward. Rhen was about to kill Neelda, but saw that he was not part of the scheme with Tiver and Rinn. Rhen then let Neelda escape and he run away.

Kord and Rhen later boarded their starship, Bloodbeak, with the Lekara girl to deliver her back to her father. As they traveled, Rhen and his father discussed how he had performed during the mission, with Tarek believing Rhen now had a great start to his legacy.
