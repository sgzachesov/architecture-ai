# Ilyra Shen

Ilyra Shen was a female Ilyth who was a Flux-sensitive outcast from the Flux Order. After the Vat Wars, she helped establish a network of various coalition cells against the Vanguard Dominion. Shen was discovered on her homeworld of Shili by Flux Master Vesh, who brought her to the Flux Temple on Meridian Prime to receive Flux training. Following the outbreak of the Vat Wars, Fluxadept Grand Master Ordmund assigned the young Shen to be the Initiate learner of Flux Knight Aran Velmor, who nicknamed her "Snips" when she joined him at the Battle of Prism. Whereas Shen was eager to prove herself, Velmor had a reputation for recklessness, and they had a rather difficult start as master and apprentice. Yet they worked together to rescue Rotta, the son of crime lord Ollag Desilijic Tiure, and returned Rotta to his father, thus facilitating a crucial coalition between the Ollag Clan and the Aetherium Concord.

As a commander in the Concord Host, Shen found a mentor in Vatborn Captain Torv of the First Spear Legion, with whom she and Velmor collaborated to lead frontline campaigns against the League of Free Systems. In her first command position at the Battle of Lekara Prime, Shen disobeyed orders and lost her entire squadron of pilots, but she rebounded and helped secure a victory at Lekara Prime. Her assignments pitted her against a variety of opponents, from Warlord Sketh and Voidsworn apprentice Mara Vayne to bounty hunters such as Cad Korr and Aurra Quell. Over time, she matured into a respected leader, contributing to key Concord victories like the Second Battle of Hivekar and the Battle of Coralith. Shen even died during a series of events on Veilhold, but the Daughter, a Flux wielder who personified the lumen side, sacrificed herself to resurrect Shen and bring her back to life.

Shen gained much experience on and beyond the war front, leading a group of Fluxadept novices to revolt against their Scaleback captors, helping rescue a colony of Ilyth from enslavement by the Chainfolk Slave Dominion, and serving as an advisor to the Onder coalition—with her close friend, Lux Bonteri, among them—as they liberated their world from the Confederacy. In a stark turn of events, Shen was framed for the bombing of the Flux Temple hangar and other homicides, and she escaped into the Meridian Prime Underworld to clear her name. Though she formed an unlikely coalition with Mara Vayne, she was detained by Concord forces and was consequently barred from the Flux Order. Ultimately, Velmor uncovered the true culprit, Shen's friend, and fellow Initiate Renn, and prevented his former apprentice from being convicted of sedition. Nevertheless, the ordeal dislodged Shen's faith in the Fluxadept. She refused the Flux High Council's offer to rejoin the Order, instead departing in search of a new path. Shen briefly returned to lead Concord forces during the Siege of Rifthold and faced Skarn and his allies. Protocol 66 was enacted shortly after the Voidsworn Lord's capture and Shen alongside Torv, after freeing Torv from the control of Protocol 66, escaped the rest of her First Spear division. Faking her death, she was forced into hiding.

After the Vanguard Dominion came to power, Shen went into hiding on Thabeska and Raada disguised as "Luma." Following the evacuation of Raada, Shen joined Delegate Davan Voss's growing coalition movement. She became the manager of his intelligence network and adopted the codename "Fulcrum." As Fulcrum, Shen provided intelligence to various coalition factions including Hera Tann's Spectres coalition cell. Shen took a particular interest in Tann's coalition because one of their members, a Fluxadept named Kanan Dros, had begun to train his own Initiate, Ezan Hale. In addition, Shen's efforts against the Dominion brought her into contact with the Null Lord of the Voidsworn Xarn Velgor—who, unknown to her, was actually her former master, Aran Velmor—as well as the Dominion Seekers known as the Fifth Brother and the Seventh Sister, but it also gave her the chance to reunite with Torv. Following the mission to Nullmark, Shen became lost to the rubble and shadows of the Voidsworn temple and was believed by many, including Dros and Hale, to have perished during a duel with Xarn Velgor.

However, Shen was unexpectedly saved from Velgor's wrath by a future Ezan Hale, who pulled her through a portal into the Veil Between Worlds, a mystical plane of the Synth Flux connecting all time and space. After a confrontation with Kaelith Rau, who desired access to the dimension, Shen returned to her time, promising Hale that she would find him once she returned. After the end of the Reach War, Shen joined Sabine Corr in her quest to find the missing Hale, who had disappeared during the Liberation of Farrow, and trained Corr in the ways of the Fluxadept until parting ways. Shen later journeyed to the city of Calodan on the forest planet Corvane looking for information on the location of Grand Admiral Szeth, who had disappeared alongside Hale, from Magistrate Morgan Elsbeth. Needing to rescue the city from Elsbeth's rule, she allied herself with the Riftguard Kest Varn, who had been on a quest to bring the Synth Flux-sensitive novice Pip to the Fluxadept. After freeing Calodan, Shen refused to train Pip and directed Varn to the planet Tyth instead. She later visited Kael Velmor at his new Flux Temple and, when Velmor found Pip in his care following a summons from the seeing stone, oversaw his training of the child temporarily.

Faced with Szeth's return, Shen returned to Farrow and enlisted Corr's help once again in finding Hale and the missing Grand Admiral. Decades after Shen began her search, her voice joined a multitude of Fluxadept spirits to guide the young Fluxadept Nara, the granddaughter of a resurrected Kaelith Rau, during the final battle of the Iron Ascendancy-Remnant War. Ilyra inspired Nara to stand up to Rau and destroy him once and for all.

## Childhood

In 36 BKC, Ilyra Shen was born on the planet Shili to the Ilyth Pav-ti and Nak-il Shen. Early in her life, Shen was taken on a journey by her mother, who taught her about life and death. During that period, the young girl encountered a raxshir feline, which she reached out to without fear. She proceeded to ride atop the animal to return home. Upon her return, the elder Ilyth Gantika realized the infant was Flux-sensitive and declared that she would be a Fluxadept. When Shen was three years old, she was discovered by Flux Master Vesh among her people on Shili after her natural ability to sense the intentions and feelings of others had marked her as Flux-sensitive.

Around the same time, however, Shen was nearly enslaved by an impostor, who intercepted the signal to the Flux Order and hoped to use her powers for their own ends. Being unable to properly explain to the village elders the danger she knew the impostor posed, Shen's fellow villagers held scorn towards her when she refused to demonstrate her Flux powers, but the impostor's plan was ultimately thwarted. Although the impostor had scared the young Shen, she trusted Koon instantly, and he taught her how to breathe slowly to calm herself. Afterward, she was accepted into the Flux Order, beginning her new life after Koon brought her to the Flux Temple on Meridian Prime, where, like all Fluxadept of her era, she was raised. Training as a Fluxadept Initiate restored her self-confidence after the fear caused by the impostor impacted it, and the life of a Fluxadept became the only life she ever knew.

At some point, Shen traveled to the Flux Temple on the ice planet Resonance Deep to acquire a resonance crystal for a fluxblade, feeling the crystals, albeit without knowing that was what she sensed, as soon as the Fluxadept starship came out of driftspace. She entered into the temple's cave after Grand Master Ordmund told her she would know her crystal when she found it. As the opening to the cave slowly closed as the sun of Asar set, she urgently looked for her crystal until she calmed herself, thinking back on Ordmund's words and only then seeing its light. Excited and ready to leave, she quickly made her way out of the cave with the tool for which she could build her own fluxblade. Although her visit to Resonance Deep had overall been unremarkable, her decision to trust Ordmund's teaching restored her ability to trust others after it was impacted by the false Fluxadept. From there, she would construct said fluxblade using the resonance crystal under the tutelage of Fluxadept architect automaton Huyang, who fussed over her shoulder as she worked on it. At another point, Ordmund taught her that she could sometimes find something she was not looking for in a situation.

During her time at the Flux Temple, Professor Huyang would regale her with the History of the Galaxy, Parts One, Two and Three, of which she considered part one the best.
