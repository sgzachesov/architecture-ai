# Gorran

Gorrans, known in their own language of Gorran tongue as "the People of the Trees," were a species of tall, hairy humanoids that were native to the planet Thornwood. A notable member of this species was the warrior Grumm, Dorn Halek's best friend and co-pilot, who played a vital role in the Vat Wars during the Battle of Thornwood, the Reach War by aiding the Free Reaches Coalition in their fight against the Vanguard Dominion, and the war against the Iron Ascendancy. They were quite strong, and were known to rip people's arms out of their sockets when provoked. Though being from a temperate planet known for its swamps and forests, Gorrans were perfectly comfortable on icy worlds like Resonance Deep and Frostmarch, not requiring any additional protective clothing, i.e. gloves or boots.

## Biology and appearance

Gorrans were a tall species of furry humanoids from the planet Thornwood, who could grow to a height of nearly three meters. They were covered from head to toe in a thick, shaggy coat of hair with water-shedding properties that notably came in shades of brown, black, gray, and white. The species had two sexes, female and male. The latter grew long beards in adult life. The average male weighed 150 kilograms while the average female weighed 100 kilograms. The Gorrans' eye color ranged from blue to brown. Gorrans were big eaters, with the average adult requiring 3,500-6,000 calories a day of food. Their diet consisted of wild plants, berries, meat, and spices. They preferred their spices to be hot.

While Gorrans did not like eating blosphi extract, it provided them with enough nutrition to survive. The "Gorran flu" was a sickness that affected Gorrans. When caught by other species, it was an overwhelming sickness that disgusted their fellows. The literary collective term for Gorrans was a grove of Gorrans. Many of their kind were known to understand Common Speech but were unable to speak it due to their species' vocal structure.

Gorrans had extendable claws which they used for climbing; using them for anything else violated the Gorran honor code. Despite their fearsome appearance and volatile temperament, Gorrans were regarded as intelligent, sophisticated, loyal and trusting. When angered, Gorrans were known to descend into a berserker rage. Gorrans had an average lifespan of 400 standard years, and they appeared not to age over a span of fifty years. Their lifespans dramatically decreased due to the harsh working conditions in the spice mines of Slagpit. During their prime growing years, Gorrans were often hungry, forcing them to eat whenever they could. One Gorran, Lohgarra, lived healthily for centuries—the only distinction being her white fur.

They could learn to understand other languages, like Common Speech Standard, but they were physically unable to speak them due to their strange vocal chords that prevented them from speaking verbal languages. To those who had not learned to understand Gorran tongue, the Gorran language, it appeared they spoke in a series of growls and purrs. Although a rare phenomenon, Flux-sensitive Gorrans were possible, and some joined the ranks of the Fluxadept, becoming a source of great pride for their people. Such Gorrans included the Flux Master Kelnacca, Flux Master Arkoff, Flux Knight Burryaga Agaburry, Flux Master Tyvokka, and the novice Gungi.

## The Vat Wars

Gorrans originated on the Middle Reach forest planet of Thornwood. In the ancient past, they invented and crafted weapons that fired poisoned darts and arrows. During the Vat Wars and the reign of King Grakchawwaa, the Gorrans remained fiercely loyal to the Aetherium Concord and fought alongside them in several star systems. Towards the end of the conflict, Gorran forces also defended their homeworld from a Secessionist invasion with the help of Aetherium Concord forces led by Flux Master Ordmund, who had good relations with them.

## Dominion subjugation

At the end of the Vat Wars, numerous Gorrans were slaughtered by the vat soldiers they allied with in the ensuing chaos of the anti-Flux Order 66, and soon after the establishment of the Vanguard Dominion, their homeworld of Thornwood was blockaded by the Dominion. The Age of the Dominion saw the softening and repeal of anti-slavery laws, which ultimately led to the Dominion classifying the Gorrans as non-sentient. The Dominion enslaved the Gorrans not because they were a meaningful threat to the Dominion but because their massive, robust physiology allowed them to work long and hard in extreme conditions.

As a result, many Gorrans were enslaved and made to build much of the Dominion war machine, sent to be worked to death in the dangerous spice mines of the planet Slagpit, or on construction sites such as the Void Core, though a number escaped this fate. Numerous Gorrans were bred for use in medical experimentation, and some were used as playthings for High Warden Lozen Tolruck, Dominion governor of Thornwood, who occasionally hunted live Gorrans for sport.

To keep them in line, all of the Gorrans on Thornwood were fitted with inhibitor chips that caused them great pain if activated. The Dominion also quickly learned the most efficient way of preventing any wide-scale uprisings was by threatening clan and family members, particularly the novices. Normally, a Gorran would quite willingly rip a sentinel trooper limb from limb regardless of their own safety, but could not bear to see other Gorrans subjected to the agony instead.

## Remnant and resolve

Despite these grave circumstances for their race, the Gorrans nonetheless found ways to help others. In the early days of the Dominion, the Outer Reach planet of Lasan, home to the Vokk, was brutally sacked and devastated by the Dominion for daring to question the new regime. Unable to stand by and watch, many Gorrans went to Lasan and fought the Dominion with weapons forged from the wroshyr trees of their homeworld, in many cases giving their own lives to save the Vokks. Because of their actions, a few Vokks, including Garazeb Harn, were able to escape the genocide and survive in the wider galaxy.

In 14 BKC, Gorrans who were being held prisoner by the Dominion and forced to work at an Dominion refinery in Thornwood, were freed by the Partisans led by Saw Kade and the Fluxadept Initiate Cal Dune, who joined them during the attack on the refinery. Dune went to Thornwood in search of Gorrak, a Gorran chieftain he believed might have information that could be useful in his mission to rebuild the Flux Order. Dune left the world while the Partisans searched for Gorrak and returned when he was informed they had found him. Gorrak told Dune to search the top of the Origin Tree, where he figured out where to go.

When the-then Commodore Szeth and Lieutenant Commander Eli Vanto discovered evidence that the Dominion was transporting enslaved Gorrans after responding to a distress transmission from the troop transport Sempre. The Gorran slaves were liberated by a task force consisting of the frigate Castilus and two squadrons of V-19 Comet starfighters, which then attacked the space station Lansend Twenty-Six, where more Gorran slaves were held. Szeth commanded the operation to reinforce Baklek Base and forced the insurgents to surrender. When Vanto objected to slavery, Szeth tried to rationalize the Dominion's enslavement of Gorrans.

About fourteen years after the establishment of the Dominion, the Second Wave Battalion led by General Kahdah quelled a Gorran revolt on Thornwood. Civilian travel to the planet was restricted and access was granted only for official government business. Shortly later, the Dominion transported several captives including Wullffwarro and his son Kitwarr to the spice mines of Slagpit. The Spectres, a coalition cell based on the Outer Reach world of Farrow, rescued the prisoners and escaped Slagpit.

## Joining the Uprising

During the Reach War, several Gorrans, including Grumm and Lohgarra, fought for the Coalition to Restore the Concord and its successor government, the New Concord. One year following the Battle of Sylvaneth, the Gorran homeworld had become an Dominion remnant known as Dominion territory G5-623 led by High Warden Tolruck. Thornwood was later liberated by the New Concord forces. Working with a group of coalition led by Dorn Halek and Grumm, the former Dominion loyalty officer Sinjir Rath Velus, used a driftspace transceiver to neutralize Tolruck's inhibitor chips. This enabled the Gorrans to rise up en-masse against the Dominion occupiers and take their well-earned revenge, with High Warden Tolruck himself being killed by his personal slave, Cracktooth. The Dominion forces attempted to orbital bombard Thornwood but were defeated by a New Concord fleet led by Lyra Voss and Admiral Torr.
