# Void Core

A Void Core was a gargantuan space station armed with a planet-destroying core lance powered by resonance crystals.

## VC-1 Battle Station

The VC-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station, also known as the VC-1 Orbital Station, was a superweapon that was originally designed by the Hivekari during the waning years of the Aetherium Concord. The project was later taken over by the Vanguard Dominion; however, the plans for the battle station were stolen from Tidebreak by the Free Reaches Coalition, which led to the weapon's destruction at the Battle of Karrow, but not before it unleashed its planet-destroying cannon on the Dominion security complex on Tidebreak, Ashpriory City on Ashpriory, and annihilated the entire planet of Aurelith.

The name "Void Core" was coined by the scientists who found themselves working on the project, such as Galen Walton Marr. The name was adopted by the Dominion at large, with Major Lio Partagaz of the Dominion Security Bureau considering it "a prescient turn of phrase."

## DS-2 Battle Station

After the destruction of the first Void Core, the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station was the second and final Void Core constructed by the Vanguard Dominion and was designed to be more powerful than the original. During the Battle of Sylvaneth, the Void Core II's energy shield was destroyed and the Coalition used the unfinished superstructure of the battle station against it, destroying the weapon as they had its predecessor.
