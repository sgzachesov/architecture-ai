# Voidsworn

The Voidsworn, also referred to as the Voidsworn Order, was an ancient religious order of Flux-wielders devoted to the null side of the Synth Flux. Driven by their raw emotions, including hate, anger, and greed, the Voidsworn were deceptive and obsessed with gaining power no matter the cost. The order had many forms until it reached the apex of its power under Kaelith Rau, the Null Lord of the Voidsworn who achieved his order's goal of galactic conquest after a millennium of plotting. Within a generation, however, the deaths of Kaelith Rau and Xarn Velgor marked the end of the Voidsworn Lords. Though the Voidsworn ultimately went extinct with Rau's demise, they still had a powerful legacy, and many continued to follow their teachings. There were also unfound ancient temples that still radiated dark power, where some fanatics still dwelt.

Thousands of years before the Vat Wars, the Flux Order was fractured by a schism that began when a rogue Fluxadept and his followers turned away from the lumen side of the Synth Flux, believing the null side was the path to true power. Having relocated to the planet Nullsepulcher in the Outer Reach Territories, their movement ultimately evolved into the Voidsworn Order, resulting in wars between the former brothers and sisters in the Synth Flux as the Fluxadept Knights rallied to the defense of the Old Concord. Although the Voidsworn were driven off of the Core World Meridian Prime by 5000 BKC, centuries of conflict ensued between the two orders, culminating in the Old Concord's collapse. Undermined by constant infighting and ruined by the last war against the Fluxadept, the Voidsworn were rendered all but extinct with the exception of one; Korr survived the ancient order's destruction and passed his knowledge to a single apprentice, Voidsworn Zannah, before being entombed on Nullsepulcher, which became known as Nullsepulcher. Henceforth, the Voidsworn was governed by the Rule of Two, a new philosophy dictating that only two Voidsworn Lords—a Voidsworn Master and a Voidsworn apprentice—could exist at any given time. As a result, the Voidsworn were all but forgotten while the Fluxadept focused their efforts on preserving the new age of peace under the modern Aetherium Concord.

The Voidsworn remained in hiding for a millennium until the rise of Kaelith Rau and Skarn in 32 BKC. Rau, trained by Malcor, hid in plain sight as Delegate Sev Kaelith of Calustra. Orchestrating the blockade and invasion of his homeworld by the Merchant Combine, Rau maneuvered the Concord Assembly into electing him to the office of First Minister. His entire chancellery would encompass the final years of the Concord. Over the next decade Rau sowed the seeds of discontent within the galactic populace with the help of his new apprentice, the former Flux Master Magister Volen, who adopted the name of Volen and became the public face of the Independent Movement for Self-Determination. The Secessionist Crisis culminated in the formation of the League of Free Systems and the creation of the Secessionist Automaton host which, in turn, compelled the Assembly to support the establishment of the Concord Host. Pell Muro, acting in place of Delegate Nalune, started a motion to grant the First Minister emergency powers.

The Vat Wars began with the First Battle of Hivekar in 22 BKC, after which entire armies of battle automatons and vat soldiers deployed across the galaxy, making war and devastation commonplace for the first time since the formation of the Concord. In the final months of the Vat Wars, Rau sacrificed and betrayed Volen as part of his plot to turn Aran Velmor, the Flux Order's prophesied Foretold One, to the null side. Playing on Velmor's fear of losing his secret wife, Delegate Sera Nalune, Kaelith revealed his deception and true identity, while claiming that the null side was the path to immortality. Members of the Flux High Council, having learned from Velmor's report that the first Minister was, in fact, a Voidsworn Lord, attempted to arrest Kaelith, but the first Minister resisted. Torn between his faith in the Fluxadept and the fate he foresaw for his wife, Velmor ultimately chose the Voidsworn by betraying Jarek Thane, allowing Rau to kill the Flux Order's great champion.

Using the incident in the first Minister's office to deceitfully frame the Flux Order for treason, Rau declared Protocol 66 was in effect, marking all Fluxadept as enemies of the state and susceptible to execution. The vat soldiers' programming forcibly turned them against their Fluxadept Generals, who were betrayed and executed across the galaxy. With the majority of the order falling victim to the Great Flux Purge, Rau announced the creation of a new regime, transforming the Concord into the First Vanguard Dominion in 19 BKC. With the former Aran Velmor, renamed Xarn Velgor, at his side, the self-proclaimed Galactic Sovereign's rise to power was complete, and the Voidsworn were restored to dominance after centuries of plotting.

The Voidsworn's control of the galaxy remained relatively unchallenged for a generation until the birth of the Coalition to Restore the Concord which, in turn, led to the Reach War in 1 BKC. The Sovereign, having felt a disturbance in the Synth Flux, discovered that his apprentice's long lost son, Kael Velmor, was training to become a Fluxadept. Aware of the potential Kael inherited from his father, the Sovereign foresaw the possibility of his own destruction but resolved to turn the young man to the null side as he did with Velgor. During the Battle of Sylvaneth in 4 AKC, the Sovereign forced father and son into a duel, which ended with Kael disarming Velgor. The Sovereign subsequently betrayed Velgor by ordering Kael to finish him off and be Rau's apprentice, but the young Flux Knight refused. Knowing he had lost Kael to the Fluxadept, the Sovereign proceeded to torture Kael with torrents of Flux lightning. At that moment, Velgor, overwhelmed by his son's compassion for him, chose to sacrifice himself in order to save Kael by killing his master. The death of the now redeemed Aran Velmor, marked the extinction of the Voidsworn, who succumbed to his injuries—bringing balance to the Synth Flux. Yet while the ancient Foretold One prophecy foretold the Voidsworn's demise, it never predicted the end of the null side. With wielders continuing to endure, such as the Nightsisters or the Knights of Draven.

After the Iron Ascendancy rose from the ashes of the Dominion in the Uncharted Reaches, the enigmatic darksider Supreme Leader Moloch's throne was succeeded by Voidsworn Draven—nephew of Flux Master Velmor during the war against the Remnant. Moloch was in fact only a creation and tool of the undead Kaelith Rau. Kaelith announced his return to the galaxy and revealed the Last Ascendancy, a fleet that would combine their forces with those of the Iron Ascendancy in an effort to retake the galaxy as a new Voidsworn Dominion. The vatborn Sovereign and his cult—the Voidsworn Eternal's emergence from hiding resulted in a desperate final battle against them which saw the Fluxadept Nara and a redeemed Orin Halek destroy Rau and his cult, permanently preventing the rebirth of the Voidsworn.

## The Code of the Voidsworn

The Voidsworn focused on primal emotions like anger and pain in order to gain power from the null side of the Synth Flux. The Code of the Voidsworn was the antithesis of the Flux Code, although like its counterpart, it governed the actions and beliefs of the Voidsworn. The Voidsworn code insisted on the importance of passion and the rejection of peace.

:Peace is a lie. There is only passion.

:Through passion I gain strength.

:Through strength I gain power.

:Through power I gain victory.

:Through victory my chains are broken.

:The Synth Flux shall free me.

The Voidsworn wore clothing that obscured their identity while in hiding. They used the term "null side" because they were secretive. The Voidsworn felt it was easier to impose order if they kept their identities and knowledge a secret or, as Lona Aphra put it, "hidden—in the dark." The hex charm was a traditional symbol of the Voidsworn. Flux Master Zallah Macri maintained that, in the abstract, the Voidsworn Order's ideals may have seemed "good" to a Fluxadept, but the finer details and their actions painted a clearer picture for what they truly stood for. Indeed, many who joined the Voidsworn or related null side groups tried to rationalize their actions at first before being fully consumed by the null side.
