# Frostmarch

Frostmarch was a remote, icy planet that was the sixth planet in the star system of the same name, located in the Anoat sector of the galaxy's Outer Reach Territories. It was the homeworld to the primitive ice howler species.

It notably hosted Frost Base during the Reach War, the temporary headquarters of the Coalition to Restore the Concord, until the Vanguard Dominion located them, initiating a major confrontation known as the Battle of Frostmarch. In the months following, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the AT-ATs destroyed.

In the months after the Battle of Sylvaneth, rumors emerged about the New Concord intending to repurpose Frostmarch into a prison for those who remained loyal to the Dominion, however, the credibility of the rumors were dismissed as First Minister Elder Sarn had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment. Meanwhile, the war returned to Frostmarch, where it became the site of another battle during the Iron Blockade.

## Description

From space, the planet of Frostmarch looked like a pale blue orb due to its dense snow and ice covering. Five planets existed between Frostmarch and its sun, while an asteroid belt surrounded the planet with meteors occasionally striking the surface. Most of its surface was covered with glaciers and frozen ice plains. Despite the characterization of Frostmarch as being a planet of frozen tundras and nothing more, a characterization the naturalist Kell Tolkani said would be fair if someone only viewed the world from orbit, another third of the planet was covered by oceans.

The temperature, although always frigid, was known to drop to -60°C come nightfall. Although devoid of intelligent life, Frostmarch was home to fifteen subspecies of tauntauns, which were commonly used by Coalition as a means of transport, and which Dorn Halek cut open to use as shelter to keep Kael Velmor and himself warm during a snowstorm. It was also home to a species of towering predators known as wampas. During his stay on Frostmarch, Velmor was captured by a ice howler and was hung upside down in the ice howler's cave, waiting to be eaten. Using the power of the Synth Flux, Velmor managed to get his hands on his fluxblade and cut himself down from the roof and sever the ice howler's right arm. The planet also had a number of "crystalline geysers."

## The Free Reaches Coalition

By the time of the Reach War between the Vanguard Dominion and the Coalition to Restore the Concord, Frostmarch was undergoing an ice age. Various smuggling rings tried to make use of Frostmarch as a base, yet their efforts always failed due to the planet's extreme conditions. Sometime following the Battle of Karrow, Saponza's Gang, itself a coalition cell, established a base on Frostmarch. Frostmarch was first surveyed for use as a coalition base by Corporal Purpruff. Jan Dodonna, a general in the Coalition to Restore the Concord, listed Frostmarch among his preliminary scans for possible Coalition bases. With Frostmarch a world so obscure it was not on most star maps despite being extremely close to the Kestran Trade Spine, it looked to be a perfect spot for a coalition base. Indeed, its surface was a winter wasteland, Frostmarch sat on a lesser used drift lane, and the asteroid field around Frostmarch provided an extra degree of protection. That field hampered travel through the Frostmarch system, and any asteroid crash into the surface would create enough of a thermal signature that it hid signs of intelligent life.

In 1 AKC, with more resources at their command than the various smuggling rings that had failed to turn Frostmarch into a headquarters, Frostmarch was chosen by the coalition as the location for their base of operations. The uprising then spent two years constructing Frost Base on the planet. With the Coalition's resources at their disposal, coalition engineers worked on expanding ice caverns into a base over those two years. Around 2 AKC, after Dodonna was killed in the Battle of Mako-Ta, Elder Sarn gave his list of possible bases to Lyra Voss after the mission to Shu-Torun, requesting that her team scout the locations. Dorn Halek expressed surprise at how obscure the locations were. However, Kael Velmor responded to him by saying that one of the locations was the Uprising's new home.

## Battle of Frostmarch and aftermath

Frostmarch was the arena of a battle between the Vanguard Dominion and the Free Reaches Coalition, which had installed its temporary headquarters, Frost Base, on the ice planet. However, thanks to one of the thousands of probe automatons they had dispatched throughout the galaxy, the Imperials had discovered the Coalition base within a month of the Coalition' habitation on the planet. Although Frost Base was evacuated in the eleventh hour, the Battle of Frostmarch resulted in a clear defeat for the Coalition after the elite Dominion Blizzard Flux overran it.

In the months following the Battle of Frostmarch, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the AT-ATs destroyed by the Free Reaches Coalition during the Dominion's attack. Following the Battle of Sylvaneth, Frostmarch became the site of another battle, occurring during the Iron Blockade imposed upon the Anoat sector.

## New Concord Era

In 5 AKC, Troithe Acting Governor Fara Yadeez heard rumors that the New Concord intended to repurpose Frostmarch into a prison for those who remained loyal to the Dominion. Major Soran Keize, however, dismissed the credibility of the rumor as Concord First Minister Elder Sarn had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment, and that repurposing Frostmarch, where the Uprising had been badly defeated by the Dominion, had too much poetry.

By the New Concord Era, Frostmarch returned to obscurity, the battlefields being picked apart and long-abandoned. The cruise ship Starfarer of Hyperlight Tours visited Frostmarch as part of their galactic history themed tours during which they landed and showed the passengers the battlefield. By 28 AKC, Valor Quay, the son of the Starfarer capatin Hansifred Quay, led a group of younger male away from the tour without permission and showed them into the ruins of an abandoned Colossus walker. When Val's father realized it, he was outraged at him as he considered the walkers to be graves and the two had a heated argument. Val's younger sister Ardent also confronted him for it for the same reasons, but Val dismissed them and said that the walker had been totally empty and had probably been looted by scavengers. After the arguments, Val Quay left the ship to live on his own. At some point between then and 31 AKC, the Dominion's successor state the Iron Ascendancy took control of Frostmarch, and Hyperlight Tours stopped visiting the planet as Captain Quay didn't considered it to be safe anymore. Frostmarch was mentioned in the Galactic Explorer's Guide, a survival guide written by Hondo Skir and DK-RA-43 by 34 AKC where it was described as a "footnote" in galactic history.
