# Sorel Vand

Sorel Vand was a legendary Flux-sensitive human male Flux Master who served on the Flux High Council during the final years of the Concord Era. As a High Fluxadept General, Vand served in the Concord Host that fought against the Secessionist Automaton host during the Vat Wars, and was nicknamed "the Negotiator." Vand, however, was forced into exile as a result of the Great Flux Purge initiated by the Concord's own vat soldiers when Concord First Minister, secretly Kaelith Rau, implemented Protocol 66. As a mentor, Vand was responsible for training two members of the Velmor family, Aran and Kael Velmor, both of whom served in turn as his Initiate in the ways of the Synth Flux.

Vand was born on the planet Stewjon in 57 BKC. He was Flux-sensitive, and therefore taken from his family and inducted into the Flux Order as a child. Raised in the Fluxadept Grand Temple on the planet Meridian Prime, Vand was assigned to Flux Master Aldric Fen. In 32 BKC, Vand attained the rank of Flux Knight by defeating the Voidsworn Lord Skarn during the Invasion of Calustra. He also chose to train Aran Velmor—the prophesied Foretold One who was destined to destroy the Voidsworn and restore balance to the Synth Flux—having promised Fen who died in the fluxblade duel against Skarn.

With the advent of the Vat Wars in 22 BKC, Vand adopted the military rank of High Fluxadept General in the Grand Army of the Aetherium Concord, and was given command of the Open Circle Fleet, the Third Systems Army, and the Seventh Reach Corps. Vatborn Marshal Commander Kell served as his second-in-command throughout the galaxy-wide conflict, with their forces achieving multiple victories over the League of Free Systems. In the midst of the war, his old enemy Skarn re-emerged into galactic affairs, bent on revenge against Vand for his debilitating defeat decades prior. Though they clashed in heated duels on multiple occasions, Skarn failed to end Vand's life or cripple his spirit, despite murdering his former love, Duchess Miren, Duchess of Rifthold.

In 19 BKC, Vand killed the Secessionist Supreme Martial Commander, Warlord Sketh, during the Battle of Pau Sink, effectively ending the Vat Wars. He later survived the initial onslaught of the Great Flux Purge against the Flux Order, which resulted from the machinations of the Voidsworn Lord Kaelith Rau. After discovering that Velmor had betrayed the Fluxadept and turned to the null side of the Synth Flux, becoming Rau' Voidsworn apprentice under the name of Xarn Velgor, Vand defeated his former Initiate on Cindral and exiled himself to the Outer Reach planet Sorrhas as the Vanguard Dominion rose to power. Adopting a hermit lifestyle as "Sorel Vand," he watched over Velgor's and Delegate Sera Nalune's son, Kael.

Ten years later, Vand left exile to rescue Lyra Voss, Kael's twin sister, and was pursued by the Dominion's Seekers and Velgor himself. Later, Vand was hunted down once again by Skarn, whom he finally slew to protect the young Kael. Vand began the boy's Flux training in 1 BKC, and soon after encountered Velgor aboard the first Void Core, where he sacrificed himself to ensure that Kael and his allies escaped from the Voidsworn Lord. In death, Vand became one with the Synth Flux which allowed him to continue guiding Kael throughout the Reach War. In 4 AKC, Vand reunited with his former student, Aran Velmor, who died destroying his Voidsworn Master in order to save his son. Vand was remembered as a legendary Flux Master during the rise of the New Concord; Orin Halek, the son of Kael's twin sister, Princess Lyra Voss, was named after Vand. Halek's turn to the null side left Kael disillusioned with the Flux Order's legacy, and he held Vand responsible for Aran's transformation into Velgor. However, Kael's faith would eventually be restored with the aid of Kaelith Rau's granddaughter Nara, to whom Vand would lend his voice and strength in a final battle and victory against her resurrected ancestor on Nullreach in 35 AKC.

## Early life

Sorel Vand was born on the planet Stewjon in 57 BKC. Despite being very young when he left his family, he still had glimpses of his father's hands and his mother's shawl, and he remembered a baby he believed to be his brother although he barely remembered him.

## Joining the Flux Order

When Vand was discovered to be Flux-sensitive, he was taken from his homeworld to the Flux Temple on the Core Systems planet Meridian Prime to be trained as a Fluxadept within the first six months of his life. In the Fluxadept crèche he was placed with other Fluxadept novices in the Kybuck Clan during his early years, including his friends Gehren and Prie. Vand received basic Initiate training alongside his peers in the Flux Order's Temple and began training in several simple Flux techniques by the age of three in 54 BKC.

Vand studied in the ways of the Synth Flux and Fluxadept traditions under Grand Master Ordmund, eventually coming to respect him and the Flux Council greatly. He also received instruction in the art of fluxblade combat from Flux Master Cin Drallig in the basic fluxblade forms and techniques. At some point before he was selected as a Initiate, Vand attended a class centered around meditation. Unlike how he acted as he got older, Vand, during his days as a novice, gained a reputation for breaking rules and having a rebellious nature, resulting in very few Masters who were willing to train the boy as their Initiate. To curb Vand's behavior, Ordmund searched for a teacher for Vand that had unorthodox methods, which would mold the student into rebelling by being dutiful.

## Initial endeavors

Around 44 BKC, Flux Master Aldric Fen chose Vand as his Initiate learner when he was thirteen years old. During his apprenticeship he became friends with Jape, a fellow Initiate who he kept correspondence with, and also maintained contact with crèche-mate Prie and the Coralith Bant Eerin. In their first months together, Fen attempted to adapt his teaching methods to suit Vand's strengths, but felt he failed to do so adequately. At some point during his training, Vand constructed his first fluxblade, modeled after Fen's fluxblade hilt style and his own personality. As the pair struggled to establish a workable affinity, the Master continued to train his Initiate in the basic cadences of fluxblade combat, preventing him from picking a specialty. Fen was proud of his apprentice's skills, and sparred with him in training sessions while at the Temple between missions. Vand, in turn, spent a significant amount of time in the training dojos to hone the techniques taught to him.

In their lessons, Fen taught Vand that there was a strength and nobility in restraint. As time passed, Vand's demeanor shifted to become more serious and respectful of the Council, in contrast to the maverick Fen's usual way of approaching situations based on his instincts in the present, even if that meant going against the Council's wishes. Both carried differing philosophies on focusing on the future versus the present, and interpreted the Synth Flux in varying ways. Despite their differences, Vand strived to be a good student, and supported his Master's hobbies regarding ancient Fluxadept prophecy research. To Vand's chagrin, the majority of his free time in the Temple was consumed by gathering information from the Flux Archives and the memory lattice of prophecy under the watchful eye of the Flux Master librarian Archivist Nuul. Over time, he learned the language Old Aurelian, which many of the memory lattice's prophecies were written in. As an escape from the workload, Vand played rounds of the game holo-chess in the lower levels of Meridian Prime in the evenings after finishing his Master's research requests.

## Early travels and visit to Thornwood

The pair's continuing training issues were discussed with Fluxadept Councilors Dena and Jarek Thane in an attempt to find a remedy, without success. At some point during his time as a Initiate, Vand and Fen visited a fortress on the planet Lah'mu, the world Aurelith's royal palace, and the planets Lekara Prime and Gatalenta. While training, he became acquainted with the Fluxadept trainee Siri Tachi and developed some feelings for her, but refrained from acting on them. Around 41 BKC, three years after becoming Fen's Initiate, a sixteen-year-old Vand was eager to prove himself on a mission, but when he and Fen were set to leave on one, his Master was nowhere to be found. Feeling angry by his master's abandonment, Vand decided to set off on the mission to the planet Lenahra alone. While he was there, he encountered a group of teenagers and datachips hidden on the world by the Fluxadept Orla Jareni long before his visit. He met a Fourarm named Dexter Jettster, who became a close friend.
