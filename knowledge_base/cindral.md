# Cindral

Cindral was a young, small planet located in the Cindral system, within the Atravis sector of the galaxy's Outer Reach Territories. Once a garden world nourished by the Bright Star artifact, its orbit was shifted when Lady Corvax unleashed the energies of the Bright Star in an attempt to return her husband to life. The resulting gravimetric duel between the gas giants Vulkhar and Lefrani over Cindral heated the planet's core, transforming the lush world into an imbalanced volcanic hellscape. Adapting to life on their devastated homeworld, the arthropodal Cindrali evolved into two distinct subspecies that cooperated to ensure their continued survival. Legends of Corvax's search for immortality brought the ancient Voidsworn to Cindral, seeking the same secrets of eternal life. There, they built a temple over a locus in the null side of the Synth Flux, above the buried ruins of Corvax Fortress.

Acquired by the Foundry Union over three hundred years before the Vat Wars, Cindral's lava flows were mined for unique and valuable minerals. It was also home to the Umbra Syndicate crime syndicate, whose headquarters was located near the mining town that became known as Zitontown after the Falleen Ziton Moj ascended to leadership of the organization. As part of the League of Free Systems during the Vat Wars, the Foundry Union on Cindral produced battle automatons for the Secessionist Automaton host. Kaelith Rau had a secret facility on the planet where he intended to turn "harvested" Flux-sensitive children into Flux-talented spies. However, the Voidsworn Lord had the base scuttled when Flux Knight Aran Velmor and his Initiate, Ilyra Shen, arrived to rescue the novices.

Cindral served as the final redoubt of the Secessionist Council until the newly-appointed Xarn Velgor, formerly Aran Velmor, was sent by Rau to slaughter the council, effectively ending the Vat Wars. The Voidsworn Lord was confronted by his wife, Sera Nalune, and his former Flux Master, Sorel Vand. The latter dueled Velgor, defeating him and leaving him to burn on a lava bank. However, Velgor was saved when Rau arrived and carried him offworld. Cindral then came under the ownership of the Vanguard Dominion after the Foundry Union and its assets were nationalized. Rau had new facilities built on the planet, where more Flux-sensitive infants were taken and transformed into agents of the Seekers.

During this era, Velgor returned to the site of his greatest defeat and, with the help of the ancient Voidsworn Lord Voidsworn Momin, erected the massive monolith Citadel Velgor to serve as his personal sanctum, built on the site of the former Voidsworn temple. Throughout the Age of the Dominion, Velgor used the planet to fulfill his master's biddings, with the fiery world becoming the final resting place for victims of the Great Flux Purge. In addition, Velgor also undertook personal business within his new realm, attempting to wield the dark powers of Cindral to bring his deceased wife back from the dead.

Following the Null Lord of the Voidsworn, the Dominion took hold of the planet. Resource extraction efforts on Cindral were taken over by the Mining Guild, although strict Dominion travel restrictions on the system made it difficult for the guild to turn a profit and mining operations eventually ceased entirely. Umbra Syndicate also abandoned Cindral for a more low profile world when the Dominion established a garrison there. The Cindrali resisted the Dominion occupation of their homeworld and attempted to drive them from Cindral by launching multiple assaults on Citadel Velgor.

Although it hosted one of the earliest coalition victories of the Reach War, Cindral fell into obscurity following Velgor's demise at the Battle of Sylvaneth, though Umbra Syndicate did eventually return to the planet. After the destruction of the Bright Star, the planet's ecosystem slowly began to heal from the devastation it had endured for centuries prior. Many devout pilgrims journeyed to the ruins of Citadel Velgor, hoping to tap into the late Voidsworn's dark power. Decades after Velgor's death, his grandson, Voidsworn Draven, journeyed to the world to continue the legacy of his family's grisly past. The Supreme Leader of the Iron Ascendancy slaughtered the Voidsworn cult that defended Citadel Velgor and discovered his grandfather's Voidsworn wayfinder, a tool that allowed him to locate the phantom sovereign Rau on Nullreach.

## World of fire

Cindral was a young rocky planet within the eponymous system of the Atravis sector, in the Outer Reach Territories. It was a small world situated in grid square L-19 of the Standard Galactic Grid, and it lay on the Tosste Spur trade route, off the Rimma Trade Route. A part of the Western Reaches, Cindral was located 53,000 light-years from the galaxy's Core.

With an overlapping orbit, Cindral was the third orbit of its system around the primary star Priate, completing a full rotation every 36 standard hours and a revolution around its sun every 412 local days, the equivalent of 618 standard days. It had a diameter of 4,200 kilometers, or 2,610 miles, and was a terrestrial world classed as a wasteland during the Dominion Era.

Nourished by the power of the Bright Star artifact, it was once a temperate and lush garden world that thrived with lifeforms that inhabited forests. Millennia before the Dominion Era, Cindral became an unstable volcanic world with underground caves and free-flowing lava constituting 35% of its volatile surface, taking the form of lava seas, rivers, and pits interspersed with glens, molten rock plains, as well as obsidian mountain ranges and shores. Few areas were temperate enough to sustain life, and only a few landmasses were not fractured by tectonic disruption and magma flows; allowing lifeforms to evolve deep underground.

The tortured planet was powerful in the null side of the Synth Flux, and, along with valuable mineral allotropes that surfaced from the planet core as well as its obscurity to the wider galaxy, attracted various off-world interests that exploited the molten planet. Cindral remained a fiery world until the destruction of the Bright Star, at which point the planet was allowed to regrow. Such different states of the world led some to believe that spiritual forces were the catalysts to the changes in Cindral's nature. Despite the planet's rugged and dangerous nature, there was a deadly beauty to the volcanic landscapes.

## Heart of lava

Deep underground, Cindral contained lava lakes from which fumes drifted up to surface-level facilities; such fumes played tricks with the minds of organics who inhaled it, giving rise to what Lellis referred to as "shadows of the mind"—visions of fears and doubts that could manifest as ghosts of the past. Lava fumes were most potent around the vicinity of the Klegger Corp Mining Facility, and sustained exposure to it drove an individual insane. Repulsor vents were thus installed at the Mining Complex to prevent such toxic fumes from rising to indoor facilities and being inhaled by those within.

Cindral was twinned with the gas giant Vulkhar, which it closely orbited, being constantly pulled by its gravitational force; Cindral was nearly captured by the larger world as its moon, an event that was only prevented by the counterbalancing gravitational pull from the more remote gas giant Lefrani. Cindral nevertheless possessed a standard gravity. The influence of the massive magnetic field of Cindral's larger twin, however, resulted in its hellish volcanic landscape, as the power of Vulkhar's gravitational force caused massive tidal disruption that heated Cindral's interior.

Along with being engulfed in a constant gravimetric battle, Cindral housed an unstable core that caused groundquakes powerful enough to split the planet's continents apart, and its split tectonic plates allowed the planet's searing magma to push through to the surface, creating a volcanic hot spot visible as a bright red cluster from space. Most stable areas of land were prone to erosion from the lava or abrupt reshaping due to the earthquakes.

While great portions of the planet were always in motion, one of the few constant features was the central volcano. Lava poured out of the spot at nearly 100,000 liters per second and pooled at the giant volcano's base. To the north was a region named the Burning Plains, which boasted heavy volcanic activity. Another region earned its nickname, the Smoking Forest, as smoke spewed from its tree-like rock chimneys. Cindral's Berken's Flow was the largest lavafall in the galaxy, while Tulrus Island—which shared its name with the tulrus creature—was surrounded by tall mountains.
