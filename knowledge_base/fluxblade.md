# Fluxblade

The fluxblade, also referred to as a laser sword by those who were unfamiliar with it, was a weapon usually used by the Fluxadept, Voidsworn, and other Flux-sensitives. Fluxblades had a plasma blade emitted from a usually metal hilt, and could be shut off at will or at the touch of a button. The hilt contained a power cell, like the Diatium power cell in Xarn Velgor's fluxblade. It also contained a resonance crystal which had been attuned to the Synth Flux by a Fluxadept, and which amplified the energy from the power cell to create the plasma beam, as well as containing it within a blade-like field. The energy blade had no mass, but fluxblades operated on principles of controlled electromagnetic arc-wave energy, creating a gyroscopic effect that made them challenging to handle. It was a weapon that required skill and training, and was greatly enhanced when used in conjunction with the Synth Flux. Though also used by the Voidsworn, the fluxblade was synonymous with the Fluxadept, with some in the galaxy believing only Fluxadept could use fluxblades.

Fluxblades were generally used for both offense and defense. A fluxblade could cut through virtually anything, from flesh to blast doors. The only ways to block the incoming attack of a fluxblade was with a weapon made with material that conducted energy, such as an electrostaff and a Z6 riot control baton, resistant metals like ironsong and cortosis, another fluxblade or an ichor sword. When used defensively, a Flux-sensitive could deflect pulser bolts with a fluxblade, and with skill, could even reflect the shots back toward the shooter or some other target. Experienced Fluxadept could even employ their fluxblades to absorb Flux lightning. Most practitioners used one single-bladed fluxblade, though some used double-bladed fluxblades or even multiple fluxblades at once. Despite lacking Flux-sensitivity, the cyborg Warlord Sketh was capable of wielding four fluxblades at once thanks to his advanced mechanical body and enhanced cybernetic brain.

Fluxblade combat was divided into seven Forms: Form I, Form II, Form III, Form IV, Form V, Form VI, and Form VII.

## Early history

Sounding like no other weapon in the galaxy, fluxblades were used thousands of years before the age of the Aetherium Concord, seeing use in the Great Scourge of Nullmark. They were also used in the Battles of Rashfond and the Peacekeeping of Parliock, The proto-saber was an ancient type of prototype fluxblade.

## Concord Era

During the history of the Flux Order, the fluxblade was considered to be the Fluxadept's only true ally in battle. As Master Sorel Vand once declared to his Initiate Aran Velmor in the days of the late Concord, the fluxblade was a Fluxadept's life. Fluxadept saw the fluxblade as a symbol of the care that they would wield using their gifts given by the Synth Flux, and also served as a message to their opponents: mastering the fluxblade required great skill and training. The choice of the saber was also the symbol of their role and duty as protectors, not destroyers, and the fact that the Fluxadept did not seek power and only sought to stand tall against darkness. Still, Fluxadept knew they would need to be more than their fluxblades.

When Initiate Enya Keen lost her fluxblade during a mission to Dalna, her master, Char-Ryl-Roy, reminded her that she would need to act as a Fluxadept without the weapon. While finding a resonance crystal was still a sacred part of a Fluxadept's duty and Keen felt like losing her fluxblade would feel like losing part of herself, she willingly gave away the crystal if it meant finding more about the Path of the Open Hand and its plans. During the sabotage of the Steady Wing in 232 BKC, Initiate Imri Cantaros dealt with emotional struggles that lead to his fluxblade beam turning transparent and weak. Fluxblades took on a more prominent role in galactic affairs as the Vat Wars came to pass. Fluxadept, previously peacekeepers, became Fluxadept Generals of the Concord Host, and thus began using their fluxblades more often than they had in their role of peacekeeping. Despite his lack of Flux-sensitivity and formal Flux training, Warlord Sketh received instruction in fluxblade combat from Magister Volen. His cybernetic arms could split into four separate appendages, allowing him to wield up to four fluxblades simultaneously. Sketh frequently hunted Fluxadept individually, claiming their fluxblades as trophies. By the end of the Vat Wars, Sketh had amassed a collection of nearly a hundred fluxblades, many reportedly taken from Fluxadept he had captured or killed, including Flux Master Neebo and the newly knighted Nahdar Vebb.

## General perception

For the majority of the Dominion's reign, the only fluxblades that were prominently used were those of the Voidsworn Lord and Dominion enforcer Xarn Velgor and the Seekers who served him, with the Seekers eventually vanishing alongside the Fluxadept they had hunted. For a time, Velgor had remained largely hidden from the galaxy, leaving the Seekers to—as noted by the later historian Beaumont Kin—possibly be more well-known fluxblade wielders. Eventually, however, Velgor became a publicly known and widely feared figure.

The majority of the Flux Order, meanwhile, had been reduced to scattered remnants in hiding across the galaxy who largely kept their fluxblades hidden, lest they draw Dominion attention. The Voidsworn Lord Kaelith Rau, as the Sovereign of the galaxy, largely ruled from a distance and was unable to use his own fluxblades in combat often. Velgor wielded a dual-phase fluxblade, a design of an older, more dangerous generation. With the disappearance of the Seekers and through his own public emergence over the course of the Dominion Era, Velgor became the primary example of someone in the galaxy who fought with a fluxblade.

However, other than Velgor as he became an increasingly known figure, the general public saw little of fluxblades during the Dominion Era. Indeed, surviving Flux Master Sorel Vand regarded the fluxblade as a past weapon created in "a more civilized age." Delegate Daho Sejan had hope that general knowledge of the fluxblade would die off in due time, leaving only Dominion narratives about the fall of the Fluxadept to be told whenever a child asked what it was. During the Reach War, Domina Tagge of the Tagge Corporation tried to recreate the fluxblade through the Tagge protoblade.

## Survival of the Fluxadept

All the same, there were still fluxblades used by Fluxadept survivors and those they trained, like Sorel Vand, Kael Velmor, Cere Mahl, Cal Dune, Tensu Run, Ilyra Shen, Kanan Dros, and Ezan Hale. While Vand, for a period, kept his fluxblade absolutely hidden, he was inspired to take back up his weapon and found his hope for the future rekindled through his experiences with the young Kael Velmor and Lyra Voss, resulting in him effectively using his weapon against Velgor during a duel on a barren moon in 9 BKC. Meanwhile, Dune had been using his fluxblade as part of his personal crusade against the Dominion, through which he became a well-known criminal. All the while in the Great Flux Purge, numerous surviving Fluxadept were wiped out by Velgor and his Seekers.

In 1 BKC, Vand and Velgor would engage in a final duel aboard the VC-1 Orbital Station, in which Vand allowed himself to be cut down so as to give Kael Velmor, Lyra Voss, and his other allies a chance to escape aboard the freighter Wayfarer. Before his death, Vand had formally begun Velmor's Flux training while withholding the truth from him that Velgor was his own father, Aran Velmor. Instead telling Kael that Velgor had murdered his father, Vand presented the young Velmor with his father's fluxblade as a gift, with Velmor using the blade during his early training. During the assault on Cymoon in 0 AKC, shortly after Vand's death, Velmor attempted to duel Velgor for the first time, although he did not have the training necessary to stand against the Voidsworn Lord. Velgor, however, was surprised to see Velmor was using his own past fluxblade, which led to Velgor's investigation into the young man's identity.
