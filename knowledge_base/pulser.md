# Pulser

A pulser, also called a gun, was any type of ranged weapon that fired bolts of intense plasma or particle-based energy, often mistaken for lasers.

## Description

Operating under the same principles as laser weaponry and firing at least partially by means of magnetic induction, pulsers converted energy-rich gas, such as tibanna gas, to a glowing particle beam that could pierce and melt through targets. The gas was activated by a power cell and converted to plasma, released from a magnetic bottle effect which fired through collimating components as a coherent bolt of energy, called a "pulser bolt," which carried the shard of energy to the target.

Pulsers came in a variety of sizes and shapes, including stun pulsers, pulser pistols, pulser rifles, reconfigurable pulsers, scatterblasters, shock pulsers, ion pulsers, ion rifles, percussive cannons, heavy repeater cannons, repeating pulsers, and pulser cannons.

Pulser artillery could also be attached on automatons and vehicles. They could deliver variable levels of damage intensity, from stun to kill settings. Several pulsers like the Satine's Lament and the Glie-44 pulser sidearm were named after individuals. Several pulsers were also hybrids with melee weapons like the bo-rifle, the pulser spear, and Ketsu's staff.

Pulser bolts could also deliver a variable degree of concussive force on impact, which could potentially cause additional damage around the impact area, depending on the hardness level and density of the target. As such, some pulser hits could cause bleeding instead of burning, or could do both, with the Synth Flux of the impact being enough to rupture organs and break bone, especially if the target is armored in any way. Despite their blazing concussive blasts, those ranged energized particle weapons could be countered by magnetic seals and aegis fields. The blade of a fluxblade could also deflect incoming pulser bolts. In the Security Forces of Calustra, some pulsers were equipped with ascension guns. Some pulsers could fire low-powered training bolts characterized by their orange color.

Pulser bolts left marks on skin known as pulser burns. Dedlanite was a material used in the manufacture of pulsers. Pulsemech Industries was a major manufacturer of pulsers. A Scaleback doubler or tripler and an actuating module increased a pulser's firepower. Hair triggers allowed faster rates of fire. Pulsers also had serial numbers on them.

Pulser components included a stock, scope, safety, cautionary pulser, pulser power pack, cooling unit, barrel, barrel-shroud, glowrod, and bayonet.

Pulser bolts came in different colors. Red was the most common color, and was made from cheaper gas. This color was used by the League of Free Systems, the Free Reaches Coalition, the Vanguard Dominion, and the Iron Ascendancy. Blue was used by the Aetherium Concord, and the Remnant. Green was used by Calustra Security Forces, and the Umbarans, as well as the Starfighter Corps of the Vanguard Dominion. This was a more expensive and more powerful type of pulser gas. Yellow was used by Riftguard. Purple was used by the Hivekari Starfighters and the Foundry Union octuptarra tri-automaton variant on the planet Skako Minor. Orange was used by the Marksman-H training remote and training automatons on Tidewell as a low power and non-lethal type of pulser bolt. Cyan was a low-powered version of the blue pulser bolt that was used by vatborn cadets in their training sessions against training automatons.

Resonance crystals were normally used to power fluxblades, but could also be used to power other weapons. People had tried to make resonance bombs and resonance pulsers, and there were a few weapons like a fluxblade rifle, of that kind in the Flux Archives.

Slugthrowers differed from pulsers by firing slugs, solid projectiles fired at high speed.
