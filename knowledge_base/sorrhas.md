# Sorrhas

Sorrhas was a sparsely inhabited circumbinary desert planet located in the galaxy's Outer Reach Territories. Part of a binary star system, the planet orbited two scorching suns, resulting in the world lacking the necessary surface water to sustain large populations. As a result, many residents of the planet instead drew water from the atmosphere via moisture farms. The planet also had little surface vegetation. It was the homeworld to the native Scrapper and Sandwraith species and of Aran and Kael Velmor, who would go on to shape galactic history.

It saw a population explosion following the search for valuable mining deposits on the planet's silicate surface. Finding the ore to have undesirable metallurgical properties, mining firms left the planet en masse, leaving behind valuable equipment, most notably large sandcrawlers, which were later used by the Scrapper population. The planet then became a haven for smugglers and criminal activity, and fell under the influence of the Ollag Clan while scattered, isolated settlements of hard-working inhabitants endured.

Generally ignored by the galaxy at large, Sorrhas gained the notice of the Vanguard Dominion during the Reach War when an escape pod carrying two automatons in possession of top-secret information landed on the sandy Outer Reach world. The Dominion went on to establish a heavier presence on the planet, forging a tenuous relationship with Ollag Desilijic Tiure of the Ollag Clan. Open conflict soon broke out between the Dominion and a Free Reaches Coalition cell on Sorrhas that culminated in a battle that left the Dominion's hold on the world significantly weakened.

Following the death of Ollag and the withdrawal of Dominion forces after the Battle of Sylvaneth, an enormous power vacuum occurred on the world, with numerous crime syndicates like the Red Key Raiders attempting to build legitimacy as mining companies. Eventually, former bounty hunter Rhen Kord returned and took over Ollag's former territory. Coming into conflict with both the Ollag Clan and the Vess Syndicate, the new Daimyo was pulled into a gang war that threatened the future of Sorrhas. With help from the Riftguard Kest Varn, Kord rallied the denizens of the settlements Emberport and Freetown to push back against the invaders, ushering in a new era of respect between the people of the planet.

## Description

Sorrhas was a hot desert planet located in the Outer Reach, a region of the galaxy far removed from its core. It had three moons, Ghomrassen, Guermessa, and Chenini. Sorrhas orbited twin suns, Tatoo I and Tatoo II. According to Scrapper folklore, the desert known as the Dune Sea was once a true ocean. The whole planet was covered in harsh deserts, and only a small part of its northern hemisphere could sustain intelligent life. The landscape was covered with sand dunes, mountains, and canyons, which made traversing the planet very difficult. Due to the absence of surface water, those who lived on Sorrhas had to draw moisture through the dry air.

Rain was incredibly rare on Sorrhas, coming once a year, decade, or century. When the rain came, some plants were able to grow in a brief yet glorious period. Inhabiting the planet were many native species, the largest of which were the carnivorous krayt dragons. The common dune wyrm, while more plentiful, was shadowed by the greater dune wyrm, which submerged itself under the shifting sands and used its powerful limbs to traverse through the dunes. In addition, the common woolhorn and pack strider species also found a home on the world, along with dewbacks, rontos, womp rats, massiffs, and scurriers.

The cities of Sorrhas were frequently blasted by sandstorms and the inhabitants always seemed to know when one was coming. Not much distinguished the towns as the poor standard of living was common. The cities were separated from each other by large expanses of deserts. Rural life, on the other hand, was mostly small moisture farms that sold water and food for a meager living.

## Early history

Sorrhas was the homeworld of the Scrapper and Sandwraith species. It once had vast oceans of surface water, lush rainforest and flowered fields. According to the Legend of Lehon, Sorrhas had also been inhabited by a group called the Kumumgah during this time, but there was little evidence to support the story. Sometime in Sorrhas's history, the whole planet became a desert by unknown means when the oceans dried away. Much later, it would be colonized by early settlers and explorers, who mistook the planet for a third sun owing to its highly reflective silicate surface.

As a result of sporadic and unsuccessful colonization ventures, the planet became a haven for smugglers and criminal activity, while scattered, isolated settlements of hard-working inhabitants attempted to earn a living in the wastes. A long-ago mining boom saw the widespread use of sandcrawlers on the desert plains in an effort to mine valuable minerals. However, after it was discovered that most valuable minerals could be found on the surface, along with most minerals having unwanted metallurgic properties, the sandcrawlers were abandoned and eventually picked up by the native Scrappers for use as shelter and mobile bases, from which they collected scrap from thousands of years of crashed spacecraft and failed mining projects.

Offworlders erected various cities in the desert, and colonists who decided to stay on the planet built moisture farms. The B'omarr Order on Sorrhas kept their disembodied brains floating in jars that hung below their spider-like automaton bodies, and built a monastery on the planet which would eventually come under the ownership of Ollag Desilijic Tiure as his palace.

Most wealth eventually found its way into the city of Emberport, with most of it procured from gambling and off-world trade, especially through the lucrative black market. The influx of commercial ventures fueled Emberport's growth, eventually securing its regional importance as a hub of economic and financial activity. With high tariffs imposed by the Ollags, some believed themselves able to escape paying them by doing business in the planet's de facto capital, however often found themselves scammed and broke if not through gambling, through simple bad luck.

## Scum and villainy

In the last decades of the Aetherium Concord, Sorrhas was known as a haven for smugglers and criminal activity, including slavery. The sand people regularly attacked Fort Sandwraith, earning them their name of "Sandwraiths." It was home to a human slave named Mira Velmor and her son Aran.

During this period, Queen Sera Nalune of Calustra escaped the capture of her homeworld by the Merchant Combine, and she took refuge on Sorrhas with her companions and protectors, including the Flux Master Aldric Fen and his apprentice Sorel Vand. The Skywalkers befriended Nalune's party, and the young slave ended up participating in a thruster race to help his new friends obtain a new T-14 drift drive generator for their damaged starship. After discovering that Aran Velmor would make an exceptional recruit for the Flux Order, Master Aldric Fen managed to obtain his freedom from the junk dealer Zev in the city of Emberport, and the Queen's party left Sorrhas with the boy. After participating in numerous adventures, including the Invasion of Calustra, the former Tatooinian slave joined the Flux Order under Vand's tutelage.

Ten years later, Velmor, now a gifted but unruly Fluxadept student, sensed that his mother, who had stayed on Sorrhas and gotten married, was in terrible pain. Velmor returned to the desert world in the company of Nalune, who was now a delegate of the Concord and the young Fluxadept's secret lover. It transpired that Mira Velmor had been captured by Sandwraiths and was detained in one of their camps. When Velmor finally discovered his mother's location, he arrived too late, and she died in his arms from the consequences of the Sandwraiths' mistreatment of her. Enraged, the young Fluxadept slaughtered the entire Sandwraith tribe. This had serious consequences as the first step towards Aran Velmor's fall from grace. Shortly after the incident, the Vat Wars broke out, and the tortured Tatooinian Fluxadept became a major player of the pan-galactic conflict.

For the most part, Sorrhas avoided the Vat Wars. Nevertheless, a Fluxadept-led mission to Ollag's Keep occurred, with the Fluxadept hoping to rescue Rotta the Huttlet from the Secessionist League. Later during the war, Chairman Papanoida and his son, Ion Papanoida headed to Sorrhas to track down the bounty hunter Sivk, who had kidnapped members of their family.
