# Mirefall

Mirefall was a remote, unexplored planet in the Mirefall system, and one of the purest places in the galaxy within the Synth Flux. A remote world of swamps and forests, it served as a refuge for Fluxadept Grand Master Ordmund during his exile after the destruction of the Flux Order. Kael Velmor received advanced training in the ways of the Synth Flux under Flux Master Ordmund on Mirefall, and it was later the place of Ordmund's death and transformation into the Synth Flux.

## Description

Mirefall was a planet located in the Sluis sector of the Outer Reach Territories. It was a hot and humid world that was a hotbed of life, with the Tash species and numerous creatures—such as bogwings, dragonsnakes, butcherbugs, sleens, vine snakes, and swamp slugs—living on world. Flux Master Ordmund would also make a home for himself on Mirefall during his exile. Mirefall was a mysterious, mist-shrouded, swampy planet, mostly covered in swamps and bogs, interspersed with countless shallow lakes and lagoons and multiple living caves. The continental and oceanic crusts of Mirefall were only vaguely defined, and there was little in the way of volcanic activity or earthquakes. Examples of flora included the lahdia plant, the yoghurt plant, gnarltree, and yarum seed. The planet was devoid of any cities.

Among the purest locations in the entire galaxy within the energy field known as the Synth Flux, it shared a mysterious time-related quality with such locales as Veilhold and Veilwatch. The Cave of Evil was a vergence aligned with the null side of the Synth Flux on Mirefall. The Cave was able to expand its influence across Mirefall until Ordmund's arrival, which created a powerful presence in the lumen side of the Synth Flux that kept the Cave in check. As such, during the Dominion Era, Mirefall's ecosystem saw balance until Ordmund's passing, which the Cave took advantage of to spread its darkness across Mirefall once more.

Mirefall had two seasons, a dry season and a wet season. During the peak of the long dry season (over half an orbit after the rains end) the uplands become too hot for most lifeforms to survive, necessitating any that live there to migrate to the lower lands. Beneath the rapidly leafing canopies of the gnarltrees that emerge from the receding green and thick waters, the once-inundated lowlands revert back to a marshy swamp once again. As the air grows more humid, the gray overcast clouds return and before another quarter orbit passes the planet is far enough away from its sun that the dry season ends and the rains return, again flooding the lowlands and covering the trees in water, making the uplands habitable once more.

## Concord Era

During the High Concord Era, an academic paper was written that described Mirefall as a world strong in the Synth Flux.

During the Concord Era, Halka Four-Den Alpha Team was dispatched to the planet Mirefall in order to chart and map the extending borders of the Aetherium Concord and learn more about it flora and fauna. After three months, Four-Den and her Alpha Team were overwhelmed by diseases, dangerous wildlife, and fast-growing plants. No trace of the team was discovered.

Near the end of the Vat Wars, Fluxadept Grand Master Ordmund traveled to the planet Mirefall after hearing the voice of deceased Flux Master Aldric Fen. He ventured into the swamp, where he communed with Fen, who appeared manifested as a group of fireflies. He told Ordmund of how to communicate with the living after death through the connection between the Living Flux and the Cosmic Flux. Then, Ordmund entered a cave, where he saw a vision of the Fluxadept fighting the vatborn and then the mysterious Voidsworn Lord.

## Ordmund's exile

After his failure to defeat the Voidsworn Lord Kaelith Rau following the formation of the Vanguard Dominion, Ordmund exiled himself to Mirefall. Using an E3-standard starship lifeboat, he began his exile, where he continued his studies of the Synth Flux. The lifeboat served as Ordmund's initial shelter but it started to degrade within a year due to Mirefall's swampy environment. As the first shelter was slowly consumed by the swamp, Ordmund built a new home for himself. He positioned it near to the null side cave but out of range of its influence and would visit every few years. However, the Mirefall wet season created a need for the Flux Master to regularly relocate to the planet's highlands for the duration.

At some point, former Sava Madelin Sun arrived on Mirefall in search of Ordmund. After saving her from a monster, Ordmund used the Synth Flux to lead Sun into the dark cave. While inside, she saw visions which dissuaded her from looking for him. She then left the planet as Ordmund watched from afar.

## Training ground of Kael Velmor

Years later, Kael Velmor of the Coalition to Restore the Concord traveled to Mirefall under the direction of late Flux Master Sorel Vand in hopes of finding Ordmund and being trained in the ways of the Flux Order. Arriving with his astromech AR-7, Velmor crashed into a bog upon landing and happened upon the Fluxadept, though he was unaware it was, in fact, Ordmund at the time. The old master took Velmor back to his hut for dinner, where he revealed his true identity to Velmor. Initially reluctant to train Velmor due to his age and lack of patience, Ordmund was eventually convinced to teach him by the Synth Flux spirit of Vand.

One night during Velmor's training, an Dominion probe automaton from Project Swarm arrived on planet. The automaton's operator, Maela, initially believed there was no reason to look further into the planet during the unit's patrol until she spotted Velmor's Star Lance in the swamp. Although she realized the outline of what she saw resembled an Star Lance, she had realized the cruelty of the Dominion after witnessing the aftermath of the Battle of Frostmarch, so she deleted the footage before moving on to her next automaton.

During his training, Velmor entered the Cave of Evil and confronted a vision of Xarn Velgor, who he struck down, only to see his own face in Velgor's mask. Later, Velmor ended his training early, however, once he sensed his friends Dorn Halek and Lyra Voss were in danger. Although Ordmund and Vand attempted to reason with Velmor to stay, the student left the planet, promising to return and finish his training.

He would keep his word, and after learning Velgor was his father during their duel on Skyhold, saving Lyra and Grumm from Velgor on Skyhold, and rescuing Halek from Ollag the Vast on Sorrhas, he would return to Mirefall to complete his Flux training. Upon arriving, he found Ordmund sick and dying, who told him that his training was complete, but all he had to do was confront Velgor one last time, and the old master became one with the Synth Flux. The ghost of Vand then revealed to Velmor the truth that Lyra was, in fact, a Velmor and Kael's sister before Kael left the planet to confront Velgor and the Sovereign in hopes of turning Velgor back to the lumen side. The Cave of Evil, meanwhile, took advantage of Ordmund's death to once again spread its power across Mirefall.

## New Concord Era

Sometime after the skirmish on the Minemoon, Voidsworn Draven, nephew of Kael Velmor, along with his master and Supreme Leader of the Iron Ascendancy, Moloch, came to Mirefall in order to face Draven's darkest fears. In the Cave of Evil, he experienced a vision of his uncle Kael, whom he dueled and killed, and a vision of his parents Lyra and Dorn. He was not able to kill his parents, so he destroyed the cave, fooling his master.

By 34 AKC, herbs from Mirefall had made their way to Oga's Cantina on Fringe Post, where they became a popular ingredient in the so-called Mirefall Slug Slinger alcoholic beverage. During or after 34 AKC, the freighter Wayfarer landed on Mirefall. There, the Gorran crewmember Grumm attempted to take some time to himself to rest aboard the ship, only for the automatons ORB-4 and AR-7—the very same automaton who had accompanied Kael Velmor to Mirefall—discovered a Kowakian monkey-lizard aboard the ship.

## Inhabitants

Mirefall was the homeworld of the Tash species, who grew herbs. Fluxadept Grand Master Ordmund made his way to Mirefall and adopted it as his homeworld during the Dominion Era. Another being of sorts on Mirefall was the so-called Cave of Evil, a living Flux vergence strong in the null side, but confined to its place on the planet.
