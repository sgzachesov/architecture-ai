# Sentinel trooper

Sentinel troopers (STs)—also known as Dominion sentinel troopers and often derogatorally referred to by some members of the free Reaches Coalition as "bucketheads"—were the elite shock troops of the Vanguard Dominion, and were part of the Sentinel Corps, an independent military branch operating under the Dominion Army. These elite, obedient and zealous soldiers wore armor consisting of white duraweave plates over a black body glove, and their standard issue weapon was the E-11 medium pulser rifle. In addition to the standard variant trooper, various specialized units such as scout troopers and snowtroopers comprised the ranks of the corps. Alongside Dominion-class Ironclads and Raptor interceptors, sentinel troopers served as reminders of the absolute power of the Dominion. In addition, sentinel troopers would serve with the regular infantry to enforce oppressive rule over the galaxy.

Taking their name from the "storm" of galactic history, vat soldiers served as the first generation of sentinel troopers, and were gradually replaced by Dominion recruits known as TK sentinel troopers. Vat soldiers were composed of the vatborn who formed the backbone of the Concord Host from its inception at the end of the Vat Wars when the Voidsworn Lord Kaelith Rau declared the Aetherium Concord at an end and himself Galactic Sovereign. Into the early years of the Dominion Era, the replication operation on Tidewell was shut down as the Dominion turned to conscription and voluntary recruitment in order to fill the sentinel trooper ranks. The remaining vatborn were gradually retired from service due to their rapid aging, their legacy inherited by natural-born humans who were also trained to render total allegiance to the Sovereign and his New Order.

Sentinel troopers were spread throughout galaxy, from the galactic capital of Meridian Prime in the Core Systems, to major installations and vital worlds, enforcing widespread terror and mass oppression. These infantry fought many battles against the forces of the Coalition to Restore the Concord during the Reach War, including the Battle of Tidebreak, the Battle of Frostmarch, and the Battle of Sylvaneth. Shortly before the Karrow Concord, Princess Lyra Voss was captured by sentinel troopers who served under the Sovereign's lieutenant, Xarn Velgor. However, Voss was rescued by Kael Velmor and Dorn Halek who, disguised as sentinel troopers, infiltrated the first Void Core. The Sovereign stationed a legion of sentinel troopers on Sylvaneth to protect the aegis generator of the second Void Core, although they were defeated by the Coalition's Bramblekin allies.

With the deaths of Sovereign Kaelith and Lord Velgor, Counselor to the Dominion Gallius Dray took control of the Dominion, which rallied to his homeworld of Ashfall to make a final stand against the New Concord during the Battle of Ashfall, however the Dominion collapsed, with its replacement of the New Concord consequently demobilizing sentinel trooper forces.

Despite this, during the New Concord Era, a variant known as Remnant Sentinel troopers continued under the Dominion Remnant as the core infantry. By the rise of the Iron Ascendancy, a new generation of sentinel troopers emerged from the Uncharted Reaches as standard soldiers.

## Origin

At the dawn of the Vanguard Dominion, the first generation of sentinel troopers consisted of replicated soldiers who served the Aetherium Concord during the Vat Wars. Under the leadership of the Fluxadept Knights, vat soldiers fought the automaton armies of the League of Free Systems across the galaxy. Though they were loyal to the Fluxadept as well as the Concord, the vatborn were secretly programmed to obey Protocol 66, a military directive authorizing the complete liquidation of the Flux Order.

With the exception of a handful of survivors, including Fluxadept Generals Ordmund and Sorel Vand, most of the Fluxadept were killed by their own troops at the command of Kaelith Rau, the Voidsworn Lord who orchestrated the Vat Wars and controlled the Concord Assembly through his secret identity of First Minister Sev Kaelith. With the Dominion claiming the galaxy was brought from chaos to order during to its rule, one account of the origins of the name "sentinel trooper" was that it came from the troopers emerging from the "storm" of galactic history. Another origin came from the final days of the Concord Era: with vatborn shock troopers enforcing law on the Concord capital of Meridian Prime and taking the role once held by the Assembly Guard, onlookers began to refer to the soldiers as "sentinel troopers."

## Ending the vat soldier program

Renamed "sentinel troopers" and assigned their new role by Sovereign Kaelith himself, the vat soldiers enforced his reign during the early years of the Dominion Era No longer beholden to the authority of Fluxadept officers who exercised restraint in warfare and with their loyalty enforced by their control chips, the vatborn utilized brutal tactics under the leadership of Dominion officers and Governors, suppressing dissent and imposing the New Order over the known galaxy as the first generation of Dominion sentinel troopers. Immediately, the Dominion moved to remove sedition within its legislative branch, as vatborn were deployed to carry out the Senatorial arrests, rounding up a total of sixty-three senators who were subsequently claimed to be Fluxadept sympathizers.

The end of the Vat Wars, however, fulfilled the sole purpose behind the creation of vat soldiers. Although Kaelith supported the Tideweller government’s vat soldier program during his final years as First Minister, his priorities changed from the expensive replication production after aponting himself Galactic Sovereign. The Prime Minister of Tidewell, Lama Su, maintained that the vat soldiers possessed unrivaled combat skills and discipline. In addition, Lama Su believed that his vatborn were critical to the security of the fledgling Dominion. Despite this, Governor and Admiral Ostren Vail was confident the Dominion would be better served by a force of recruits, which he saw as a cost-effective way to enforce order in the galaxy.

In addition, there were further incentives for the Dominion to decommission its vatborn units in favor of natural-born humans. The vatborn' accelerated aging process limited their "shelf-life." Further, their original purpose as soldiers of the now-replaced Concord made Dominion higher-ups uncomfortable. Vail himself had noticed the individuality vatborn had developed under Fluxadept leadership, which made them unpredictable for Dominion purposes. As such, Vail sought a clean break from the Fluxadept-influenced past. Although Vail supported conscription and recruitment over replication, vat soldiers were initially retained by the Dominion Army that replaced the Concord Host, but rumors spread throughout the ranks that the replication facilities on Tidewell had been shut down.

## Rise of volunteers and conscripts

In the days following the end of the Vat Wars, Vice Admiral Edmon Rampart sanctioned the creation of an Elite Squad of non-vatborn human soldiers, led by the former Vatborn Flux 99 sniper CT-9904 "Crosshair." Rampart envisioned enlisting human soldiers and having them train under skilled vatborn would be the best way to generate loyal soldiers without the replication program. The squad included four highly skilled recruits enlisted to be the first wave of such soldiers. The individuals traded in their names for numbers, becoming ES-01, ES-02, ES-03, and ES-04 and undertaking early missions to eliminate insurgents throughout the galaxy, in particular, pursuing the now-rogue Vatborn Flux 99, Crosshair's old squad. The squad's success during their missions proved the potential of enlistment as the method to fill the ranks of the Dominion Army to Vail, who allowed Rampart to continue secretly enlisting more recruits under Project War-Mantle. Though Vail supported Project War-Mantle, he believed the vat soldiers would continue to remain essential until the Dominion Army was ready to retire them.
