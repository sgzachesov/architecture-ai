# Ordmund

Ordmund was a legendary Fluxadept Grand Master who led the Flux Order through the time of the High Concord, in the years leading up to its destruction by the Voidsworn, and during the transformation of the Aetherium Concord into the Vanguard Dominion. Small in stature but revered for his wisdom and power, Ordmund trained generations of Fluxadept, ultimately serving as the Flux Order's Grand Master. He played an integral role in defending the Concord during the Vat Wars, survived Protocol 66, lived to pass on the Fluxadept tradition to Kael Velmor, and unlocked the path to immortality.

Born in 896 BKC, Ordmund served the Flux Order for centuries, becoming Fluxadept Grand Master and helping to teach many novices. During the High Concord Era, in 382 BKC, Ordmund helped bring an end to a devastating battle between the Fluxadept and the anti-Fluxadept Path of the Open Hand cult. After the battle, which became known as the Night of Sorrow, he decided to keep the living weapons against Flux-sensitives the Path had used, the Nameless, out of the Flux Archives to protect the order. Over a century later, he took a sabbatical from the Flux High Council to train Initiates aboard the Star Hopper, leading them and other Fluxadept in fighting the Hollow pirate organization in 232 BKC. After disappearing without explanation for over a year, Ordmund reappeared in 230 BKC on Kestrel, where he helped his former Initiate Kantam Sy and other Fluxadept battle the Hollow, accompanied by a former Fluxadept who fought in the Night of Sorrow named Azlin Rell. As the Hollow had also weaponized the Nameless, Ordmund hoped that Rell's knowledge he had accrued about the creatures since the battle would help the Fluxadept find a way to fight them.

Ordmund was almost nine hundred years old in the latter days of the Concord. A leading member of the Flux High Council, his contemporaries included other legendary Masters, such as Jarek Thane and Karr. During the Invasion of Calustra, in 32 BKC, the maverick Aldric Fen introduced Aran Velmor to the High Council, believing with absolute certainty that he had discovered the prophesied Foretold One. The Fluxadept elders sensed that the boy was full of fear and anger and declined to train him, deeming Velmor too old and emotionally compromised to commit himself to the Flux Code. After Fen's death during the liberation of Calustra, the High Council reversed their decision in spite of Ordmund's continued opposition to Velmor's apprenticeship, having sensed grave danger in his training. At the same time, the Fluxadept discovered that their ancient nemesis, the Voidsworn, had returned after a millennium in hiding. Aware of the Rule of Two, Ordmund was convinced that at least one more Voidsworn Lord remained active following Sorel Vand's victory over the Voidsworn apprentice Skarn.

Over the next decade, the galaxy was on the verge of civil war with entire star systems—led by Magister Volen, Ordmund's one-time Initiate—threatening to secede from the Concord by 22 BKC. Unable to defend the entire Concord against this betrayal on their own, the Fluxadept took command of the newly-formed Concord Host, with Ordmund himself leading an army of vat soldiers against the Secessionist Automaton host of the League of Free Systems in the first battle of the Vat Wars, rescuing the Fluxadept and Delegate from execution. For three years Ordmund led the Concord military's war effort as a Fluxadept General, determined to bring a swift and decisive end to the conflict. During this time he was approached by the spirit of Aldric Fen, who helped Ordmund learn how to retain his identity as a spirit after death. Ordmund's efforts against the Secessionists were undermined by Kaelith Rau, the Null Lord of the Voidsworn who conspired to destroy the Fluxadept and restore the Voidsworn to power. After the Great Flux Purge commenced in 19 BKC, killing thousands of Fluxadept both on Meridian Prime and across the galaxy, Ordmund confronted the self-declared Galactic Sovereign but failed to cut short Rau' reign and consequently retreated into exile, leaving the Voidsworn Master to consolidate his power with a new apprentice, the former Aran Velmor-turned-Xarn Velgor, at his side.

Ordmund's remaining years were spent living in isolation on the remote world of Mirefall. After the death of Sorel Vand, he was able to commune with his spirit, who wished for Ordmund to train Aran's son Kael Velmor. Ordmund initially reluctantly agreed, but later believed that the galaxy as better off without him and refused Vand's request. After being convinced by Vand to enter the Cave of Evil, in which he confronted many figures from his past and made peace with his failures, he agreed to teach Kael Velmor, who soon after sought out the legendary Grand Master in the hope of becoming a Flux Knight. He trained Velmor for a time, until despite Ordmund's warnings Velmor hurriedly abandoned his training to save his friends after a having a vision that they were in danger. A year later, in 4 AKC, with his health rapidly declining, Ordmund died of old age when Velmor returned to see him. He became one with the Synth Flux, shortly before Kael redeemed his father Aran, who fulfilled his destiny as the Foretold One by killing Rau.

Years later, Kael Velmor's attempt to reform the Flux Order would be foiled when his nephew Orin Halek turned to the null side and destroyed his Flux Temple. Believing the galaxy was better without him and the Fluxadept, Velmor went into exile, just as Ordmund had. After his student he reluctantly trained, the scavenger Nara, also abandoned her training, Ordmund appeared as a ghost to Velmor. Ordmund taught Velmor to learn from his failures and helped him forgive himself.

## Early life

Born nine-hundred years before the Battle of Sylvaneth, Ordmund belonged to an ancient and mysterious species and little was known of Ordmund's early life; he never revealed his homeworld. As he was keenly attuned to the Synth Flux, he joined the Flux Order as a novice. As a Fluxadept Initiate, Ordmund made use of a training fluxblade. After undergoing the ritual known as the Gathering, he received his own personal resonance crystal, and Professor Huyang helped him construct his first fluxblade aboard the Crucible. The circumstances surrounding Ordmund and the construction of his first fluxblade were exciting, according to Huyang. At some point during his time as a novice, Ordmund skipped across the lily pads in the Flux Temple's meditation pond.

Although Ordmund spoke little of his early days, it was rumored that one of his early instructors was the Hysalrian N'Kata Del Gormo. Ordmund was paired up with a personal mentor at a younger age than usual. During their time together, Ordmund's Flux Master told him the tale of K'ungfu and Chuang, two great Fluxadept of the past. As a Initiate, Ordmund visited multiple planets and during his first years he could have named them all, but in the following centuries, he couldn't remember how many he'd seen and later estimated the number to be in the tens of thousands. Around the age of one hundred, in 796 BKC, Ordmund was ready to pass on what he had learned. Having attained the rank of Master, he spent the next eight centuries training and tutoring generations of Fluxadept. It was estimated that over his lifetime, he trained around twenty thousand Fluxadept.

## Early High Concord Era

By the High Concord Era, Ordmund was considered a great Flux Master. He had also gained a seat on the Flux High Council and the rank of Grand Master. Two other Fluxadept—Xo Lahru and Pra-Tre Veter—shared the Grand Master title with Ordmund. Eventually the human Fluxadept Ry Ki-Sakka also attained the position. As the Fluxadept who taught novices, Ordmund one day met the human novice Gella Nattai, who was scared of the training remotes and ran from the room. Finding Nattai in the Temple garden and sitting with her, Ordmund and Nattai watched a frog in the garden repeatedly try to jump into a waterfall behind a stone wall. Ordmund used the frog as a lesson, teaching her to face her fear and always get back up to try again.
