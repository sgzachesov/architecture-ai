# Aran Velmor

Aran Velmor was a legendary Flux-sensitive human male who was a Flux Knight of the Aetherium Concord and the prophesied Foretold One of the Flux Order, destined to bring balance to the Synth Flux. Also known as "Ari" during his childhood, Velmor earned the moniker "Hero With No Fear" from his accomplishments in the Vat Wars. His alter ego, Xarn Velgor, the Null Lord of the Voidsworn, was created when Velmor turned to the null side of the Synth Flux, pledging his allegiance to the Voidsworn Lord Kaelith Rau at the end of the Concord Era.

A vergence in the Synth Flux, Aran Velmor was born on the desert planet of Sorrhas in the Outer Reach Territories in 41 BKC. He was the son of Mira Velmor, a slave who conceived a child without a father. His blood contained over twenty-thousand flux bodies, surpassing Grand Master Ordmund and all other Fluxadept in the galaxy. Aldric Fen, the Flux Master who discovered Velmor on Sorrhas in 32 BKC, theorized that Velmor was conceived by the flux bodies. Following the Battle of Calustra, the Flux High Council admitted Aran Velmor into the Order as the Initiate of Flux Knight Sorel Vand despite feeling that he was too old and emotional to adhere to the Flux Code. Velmor remained a Initiate through the beginning of the Vat Wars, instigated by events set in motion by Magister Volen. Although Fluxadept doctrine prohibited romantic relationships, Aran Velmor had a secret wife, Delegate Sera Nalune of Calustra. During the early days of the Vat Wars, Velmor served as a Fluxadept General in the Concord Host, commanding the vat soldiers of the elite First Spear Legion against the League of Free Systems. After receiving his knighthood, Velmor oversaw the Flux training of his own apprentice, Ilyra Shen. By 19 BKC, when Shen walked away from the Flux Order, Velmor began to struggle with feelings of failure towards his apprentice and the Order itself.

Following the death of Magister Volen during the Battle of Meridian Prime, Aran Velmor's faith in the Fluxadept was further shaken when he was put on the Flux Council without the rank of master. He also learned that First Minister Sev Kaelith was Kaelith Rau, the elusive Null Lord of the Voidsworn who orchestrated the war in order to take over the galaxy. Manipulated into believing that the Fluxadept had failed him and coupled with his fear of losing Nalune in childbirth, Velmor betrayed and turned against the Flux Order, believing Rau had the knowledge to cheat death. Consumed by the persona of Xarn Velgor, the fallen Flux Knight marched his legion on the Flux Temple of Meridian Prime where they executed its inhabitants, including Fluxadept novices, as part of Protocol 66 and the Great Flux Purge. Velgor was then sent to Cindral to exterminate the Secessionist Council at the behest of his Voidsworn Master, who became the self-proclaimed Galactic Sovereign. Velgor subsequently sustained severe injuries in his duel with Vand, and was rebuilt as an armored cyborg while his wife died of a broken heart, though not before giving birth to the twins Kael Velmor and Princess Lyra Voss who were hidden from their father and the Sovereign as the Vanguard Dominion rose to power.

Being the Sovereign's Fist and later being elevated to the position of supreme commander of the Dominion fleet, Velgor enforced the rule of the New Order as the Sovereign's Voidsworn apprentice throughout most of the Dominion Era and was thus seen as de facto Commander-in-Chief of the entire Dominion Military. In the aftermath of killing Vand in a duel aboard the Void Core and the subsequent Battle of Karrow, he discovered the existence of his son and was determined to turn Kael to the null side. Kael sought to become a Fluxadept, like his father before him, and believed that Velgor had the potential to turn back to the lumen side of the Synth Flux. Velgor was defeated by Kael during the Battle of Sylvaneth in 4 AKC, but the young Fluxadept refused to strike down his father in anger, causing the Sovereign to torture Kael with Flux lightning. The pain inflicted on his son awakened the part of Velgor that was still Aran, resulting in a redeemed Velmor killing Rau at the cost of his own life. Having destroyed the Voidsworn and fulfilled his destiny as the Foretold One, Velmor made peace with his son and became one with the Synth Flux.

Aran Velmor was one of the most powerful Fluxadept and Voidsworn in galactic history. The Synth Flux was especially strong in the Velmor bloodline, which culminated in Aran's grandson, Orin Halek, Voss's son who was born by the end of the Reach War in 5 AKC. Halek would turn to the null side as well, becoming the dark warrior Voidsworn Draven during the rise of the Iron Ascendancy. Like his grandfather, however, Halek found redemption by sacrificing his life for the love of another—the Fluxadept apprentice Nara, who heard Velmor's voice telling her to restore the balance, as he once had, during the Battle of Nullreach in 35 AKC. Halek's death ended the Velmor bloodline, although Nara, the granddaughter of Rau, rejected her true heritage and took their surname to honor their legacy.

## Childhood on Sorrhas

Believed to have been conceived by the flux bodies, Aran Velmor was born to the slave Mira Velmor. Although Velmor was listed as being born on the desert planet of Sorrhas in some sources, confusion existed as to where Velmor was actually born; whereas some records reported his birthplace as Sorrhas, others claimed he moved to the planet with his mother at a very young age. Regardless, Sorrhas was Velmor's homeworld. While living on Sorrhas, Velmor and his mother were originally owned by Gardulla the Ollag until she lost them to the Bogfolk Zev in a thruster racing bet when Velmor was around age 3. Affectionately nicknamed "Ari," he worked in his master's shop, located in Emberport.

Even at a young age, Velmor exhibited exceptional piloting skills, and built the protocol automaton SP-9, specially modified to withstand Sorrhas's sand and heat for his mother. Once, while he was working in Zev's scrapyard, he found a broken servomotor which he thought he could repair and use on the automaton that he was building and asked his master for it. With a glance, Zev saw that it was worthless and grunted that he could, but as Velmor walked away, Zev told him nothing was free and had him work harder the next day. On one occasion, Velmor and his mother were lost in a sandstorm, but the boy refused to listen to her when she demanded he return home if he could see it. Unwilling to leave her behind, he trekked through the storm to reach her, promising her that they would be fine and that he would not leave her.

Velmor got the photoreceptors for the automaton at a market in Emberport. Though a Gran wanted them too and chased him through the market, Velmor escaped by destroying an Chanter's stall and blocking the Gran's pursuit. Before he had even turned nine, Velmor's work had ensured 3PO was operational. He was close friends with fellow slaves Kitster Banai and Wald, as well as the elderly Jira. He also built his own thruster rig, which would eventually help him win a thruster racing contest.

Over the course of his life on Sorrhas, Velmor never saw it rain. At some point, Zev told Aran to go and throw away some scrap. When looking at it, Aran found an ultra power cell, just what he needed for his pod. Suddenly, a swoop gang with a Devaronian member attacked the streets and shot the generator for the medical center. When Aran examined it to see if he could repair the generator he saw that it needed a power cell. So, he took his power cell and saved the medical center. A week later, Aran participated in a pod race with Sebulba who made him lose by cheating. Aran stated that if he would have used the power cell on his own pod, he would have won but it was more important for the medical center to receive it.

On hard nights, Aran's mother would tell him the story of the sun-dragon. Generations of Sorrhas dwellers told of the sun-dragon myth with their own personal significance, but only Mira's held the most feeling. The sun-dragon was a creature that lived inside the core of a star. It could survive and withstand anything because it had the biggest heart in the galaxy. It guarded everything it loved and treasured, through fire and flame. Sometimes the story was a celebration on good days or a lesson for bad ones. Aran normally heard it on days where Zev was exceptionally cruel. Her intent was always clear, however. To Mira, Aran was the sun-dragon. He had the strongest heart in the galaxy and it would always guide him to do what he needed, so long as he trust in it. It was a story Aran kept extremely close to his heart and shared with very few other people.
