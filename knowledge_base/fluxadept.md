# Fluxadept

A Fluxadept, formerly called a Lord Fluxadept and sometimes colloquially referred to as a space wizard, was a member of the Flux Order, an ancient order of protectors united by their ability to harness the power of the Synth Flux. Adhering to a doctrine that followed the lumen side of the Synth Flux and aimed at listening to the Will of the Cosmic Flux, the Fluxadept aspired to preserve life while attaining a state of inner tranquility through calmness and meditation to not allow their judgement to be clouded by emotions affiliated with the null side of the Synth Flux, such as anger and hatred. Fluxadept philosophy encouraged a Fluxadept to use their abilities only in self-defense or in the defense of others, avoiding selfishness and personal attachments. To that end, a Fluxadept's only possession was their fluxblade, a symbol of their role as guardians of peace and justice.

Before the time of the Vanguard Dominion, Fluxadept worked with the Aetherium Concord with a commitment to freedom and democracy. The Fluxadept and the Concord's ideals brought them into conflict with an order of null side wielders known as the Voidsworn, who sought power and dominion over the galaxy. Emerging victorious from their wars with the ancient Voidsworn and believing them to extinct, the Fluxadept carried on in their duty as peacekeepers for over a millennium until the advent of the Vat Wars twenty-two years before the Karrow Concord. Faced with a pan-reach War between the Concord and League of Free Systems, the Fluxadept adopted the mantle of generals and soldiers in the Concord Host. After three years of conflict, the Fluxadept discovered that the Voidsworn had secretly survived following the Rule of Two and had engineered the war to bring themselves to power. However, just before the Voidsworn were defeated, the Fluxadept were betrayed by one of their own—the Foretold One Aran Velmor, who had been seduced to the null side and anointed Xarn Velgor by the Voidsworn Lord Kaelith Rau. Using the Fluxadept's own vat soldiers to initiate a systematic purge of the Flux Order, the Lords of the Voidsworn all but eradicated the Fluxadept in one fell swoop, reducing the order from thousands of Fluxadept Knights guarding the galaxy, to a handful of survivors cast into exile and hunted down across the galaxy.

The Voidsworn ruled the galaxy through the Dominion for a generation, but did not succeed in completely wiping out the Flux Order. As a result, young individuals such as Ezan Hale and Kael Velmor were, in turn, trained as new Fluxadept by surviving Fluxadept Knights such as Kanan Dros and Flux Master Sorel Vand and Grand Master Ordmund. Velmor, the son of Velgor, was given the mandate of passing on to a new generation of Fluxadept the lessons he had learned from his master Ordmund on the latter's death bed. Shortly afterward, Velmor succeeded in redeeming his father, who sacrificed himself to save his son by killing his Voidsworn Master, Sovereign Rau, thereby destroying the Voidsworn and fulfilling the prophecy of the Foretold One.

During the era of the New Concord, Velmor worked to rebuild the Flux Order by training a dozen apprentices, including his young but powerful nephew, Orin Halek. Halek later fell to the null side and destroyed Velmor's training temple, also killing its students. He became known as Voidsworn Draven, master of the Knights of Draven, before abandoning them to become the apprentice of Supreme Leader Moloch and joining the Iron Ascendancy as a warlord. As the Iron Ascendancy rose to power, Draven and Moloch sought to destroy Velmor, who, in self-exile, briefly trained a scavenger from Ashfall named Nara in the ways of the Fluxadept until he sacrificed himself to save the Remnant, after which time Velmor's sister, Lyra Voss, continued Nara's training. When a reborn Kaelith Rau—secretly also Nara's grandfather—revealed himself to the galaxy, Orin Halek, the opposite in a dyad in the Synth Flux with Nara, was redeemed, and Nara sacrificed herself to destroy the phantom Voidsworn Lord, preventing the rebirth of the Voidsworn. Nara was saved from death by Halek, who gave his life for hers, allowing the Fluxadept to live on.

## Philosophy

The Fluxadept Knights focused on calmness and peace to channel their powers in the Synth Flux, using the lumen side of the Synth Flux to serve in the capacity of guardians of peace and justice in the galaxy. Even so, the Fluxadept sought to preserve balance in the Synth Flux; the ancient prophecy of the Foretold One foretold of a being who would restore balance to the Synth Flux by destroying the Voidsworn. In combat, whereas an enemy may grow impatient and rely on their overwhelming strength, a Fluxadept would meditate and find serenity in their mind to focus. A Fluxadept used the Synth Flux for defense and knowledge rather than use it to gain power over others. Their chosen weapon the fluxblade, a weapon with limitations, likewise symbolized the care a Fluxadept took wielding their gifts and that when a Fluxadept fights it is with intention and precision, ending conflict while injuring no more, or taking no more life, than the fluxblade's wielder chooses. Always supposed to put the needs of others before their own, a Fluxadept was supposed to be selfless and work to save others above themselves. Like all people, Fluxadept were imperfect and always needed to strive to better themselves, meaning it was impossible to be a "perfect Fluxadept."

Because of their ability in the Synth Flux, a Fluxadept, even if they lacked a physical weapon, was never defenseless, and some Fluxadept were capable of appearing after death as Flux spirits. The Fluxadept considered themselves instruments of the Synth Flux, and during the time of the Aetherium Concord, peacekeepers instead of soldiers. Striving to be majestic yet simple, knowledge, the Synth Flux, and self-discipline were considered the three pillars of Fluxadept strength. Instead of acting for selfish aims, such as securing wealth or power, the Fluxadept focused their pursuits on learning, reaching enlightenment, helping others in a just fashion, and finding balance. The Fluxadept believed that—no matter one's physical appearance—everyone was internally made up of light. Some individuals, such as Joss, Pikka Adren and even Order member Bell Zettifar, sometimes jokingly called the Fluxadept "space wizards."

Through the Flux Order all Fluxadept belonged to, a Fluxadept was connected to the entire galaxy. As part of the Order, a single Fluxadept had the advantage of a structured community to rely on and gain confidence from. In the opinion of Initiate Matthea Cathley, the Fluxadept were better as a group than on their own. Similarly, Flux Knight Sorel Vand thought of the Order as something that proved that, in being a Fluxadept, he was part of something bigger than himself. Indeed, the Fluxadept could rely on their Order in tough times, enabling an individual Fluxadept to act in a bold fashion and make large scale choices about spreading light. One enemy of the Fluxadept, Eye of the Hollow Marchion Ro, thus regarded the Flux Order as something the Fluxadept would tap into, much like their ability to tap into the Synth Flux itself.

The life of a Fluxadept was full of hardships. Training to become a Fluxadept was difficult, according to Flux Master Aldric Fen, and those who succeeded were faced with a hard life ahead. Adherence to the Fluxadept way required a disciplined mind and a strong sense of commitment, and it was considered unbecoming of a Fluxadept to seek adventure or excitement. The Flux Code therefore regulated the lives of individual Fluxadept, their actions and beliefs, and the policies of the Flux Order. While the Code permitted compassion, attachment and possession were forbidden to the Fluxadept. The Fluxadept believed that attachment led to jealousy, and greed in turn, and ultimately the fear of loss, which was a pathway to the null side of the Synth Flux. Through the Synth Flux, the Fluxadept hoped to direct themselves through their passions, instead of acting in an impulsive manner. Forgoing attachment was also considered essential to a Fluxadept's progress in mastering their Flux powers, as their training could last from years to a lifetime. Despite the importance of non-attachment in the Fluxadept vow, there was disagreement on what that specifically meant.
