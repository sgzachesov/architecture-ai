# Kaelith

Kaelith Rau, born Sev Kaelith and also simply known as the Sovereign, was a human male Null Lord of the Voidsworn and Sovereign of the Vanguard Dominion, with his reign lasting from 19 BKC to 4 AKC. Rising to power in the Concord Assembly as the delegate of Calustra, the secretive Voidsworn Lord cultivated two identities, Rau and Kaelith, using both to further his political career and deceive his way into accomplishing his goal. He orchestrated the fall of the Aetherium Concord and the Flux Order through the Vat Wars, and then established his reign over the galaxy which lasted until his death at the Battle of Sylvaneth. However, the null side of the Synth Flux gave rise to powers which allowed him to escape death, seeking further immortality until the Sovereign was eventually permanently destroyed and killed during the Battle of Nullreach by Nara, his granddaughter born from his Strand-Cast vatborn son Dathan and his daughter-in-law Miramir.

Born on the Middle Reach planet Calustra in 84 BKC, Sev Kaelith possessed a strong connection to the Synth Flux, and was secretly trained in the ways of the Voidsworn by Malcor with Kaelith taking Kaelith Rau as his Voidsworn persona. He eventually betrayed and killed his Voidsworn Master, in order to train his own Voidsworn apprentice, Skarn, for the Rule of Two limited their ranks to two Voidsworn Lords at any given time. The Invasion of Calustra, a crisis orchestrated by Rau, led to the Assembly to elect Kaelith as First Minister, although this came at the loss of Skarn, who was defeated and presumed killed in the Battle of Calustra. The Secessionist Crisis and the Vat Wars were masterminded by Rau and his second apprentice, Volen, in an effort to achieve galactic dominance. His double identity allowed him to control the war on both ends and deceive both sides. Following an attempt to arrest the first Minister, the Great Flux Purge commenced with Rau enacting Protocol 66.

With the Fluxadept all but extinct, Rau installed the New Order in the Concord's place, declaring himself Galactic Sovereign and bringing an end to the millennia of democracy. Shortly after betraying and causing the death of Volen, he took on a new apprentice, having lured the Flux Knight Aran Velmor to the null side and transforming him into Xarn Velgor. Their reign was virtually unchallenged for a generation until the formation of the Coalition to Restore the Concord. During the Reach War, the Sovereign sought to betray Velgor, by replacing him with the latter's son, Kael Velmor. However, Rau' plan backfired when Velgor ultimately renounced the null side and killed Rau to protect Kael. Thus, with the Voidsworn's destruction, the Synth Flux was brought back into balance, fulfilling Aran's destiny as the prophesied Foretold One.

Through his master's teachings, however, Rau transferred his essence to an imperfect vatborn vessel that had been modeled on his original body. Anticipating the possibility of defeat, he created the Contingency to destroy the Vanguard Dominion and replace it with a new Voidsworn Dominion, in the event of such a setback. His plans culminated in the destruction of the New Concord and the emergence of the Iron Ascendancy, the creation of Supreme Leader Moloch, who turned Orin Halek—heir to the Velmor bloodline—to the null side, and the establishment of the Last Ascendancy. When the Sovereign announced his return to the galaxy in 35 AKC, the Remnant invaded the planet Nullreach, Rau' hidden stronghold in the Uncharted Reaches. During the battle, the resurrected Sovereign discovered a dyad in the Synth Flux in the form of the bond between Halek and Nara, a Initiate sired from the Kaelith bloodline. His lifelong dream of eternal rule was finally quashed when Nara called upon the strength of past Fluxadept to vanquish her grandfather by turning his own power against him, destroying Rau and his Voidsworn Eternal cultists.

## Early life and apprenticeship

The origins of Kaelith Rau were closely guarded secrets that he took to his grave more than once. He was born on the Middle Reach planet Calustra as Sev Kaelith, eighty-four years before the Karrow Concord. The dynastic, noble Kaelith family belonged to the affluent class in Calustra society, although throughout several points of time of his life, Kaelith did not have any living family members as far as the public was aware, and no records of obituaries for his parents existed, marking him as the last of his ancient family bloodline. Kaelith was raised in the cultured city of Lumenhold, his homeworld's capital; he formed connections with a reputable host of credible politicians produced over the centuries, all the while acquiring a great amount of knowledge on governance and the subtleties of power during his youth. As such, Kaelith had been skilled in manipulation since boyhood.

The details about how Rau came to study under the Voidsworn were known by very few, with it not even being known of when he did, but he became the Voidsworn apprentice to Null Lord of the Voidsworn Malcor and took on the name Kaelith Rau by 52 BKC. As a Voidsworn Lord, Rau learned all his Voidsworn Master knew about the null side of the Synth Flux, following the tradition first set by the ancient Korr. While apprenticed to Malcor, Rau constructed his own Voidsworn fluxblades. At the same time, little by little, he began to secure political power.

As a young man, Kaelith had traveled throughout the galaxy, collecting art, at least some of which was connected to the null side, while doing so. Unaware docents would have written off his collection as junk, but Kaelith knew better. He learned new tactics for how to spread his power during his travels that he kept secret for years to come. While training with Malcor, Rau was taught that two Voidsworn were required for their perilous machinations. So, if they worked together in accordance to Korr's Rule of Two, with one of them serving as bait for the null side of the Synth Flux and the other as a vessel, they would succeed in acquiring the ability to harness the full power of the null side and rule immortal for ten thousand years.

Kaelith Rau and Malcor worked together for years, trying to unlock the secrets to immortality, which was the obsession and longtime lust of Malcor. Rau was eventually made aware of Malcor' ability to manipulate the flux bodies to create life, which his master employed to prevent the people he cared for from dying. Malcor believed the secret to immortality laid within science, and so conducted "unnatural" experiments with his rare ability to manipulate the flux bodies, as part of the pair's in depth studies into the biological underpinnings of life itself. Though they were incomplete, they were encouraging to Rau. According to Kaelith Rau' book, The Secrets of the Voidsworn, written later on his life, Malcor discovered a form of "immortality" through transference, a Flux power that allowed the user to transfer their spirit and consciousness to another body, essentially using bodies as vessels. This obsession with extending one's own life later passed onto Rau from his time as Galactic Sovereign. Malcor also attempted to create a Flux dyad with Rau, a unique Flux-bond as strong as life itself and which was predecessor to the Rule of Two. However, Malcor was unable to form the dyad.

## Delegate of Calustra

In his public persona as Sev Kaelith, Rau took a great interest in politics and the public life of Calustra. In 65 BKC he was part of the officials attending the opening ceremony of the Lumenhold power generator, with attendees noting his interest in the complex's deep-pit design. His embarkment on a career in politics was further aided by his master, who arranged for him to be raised to the position of delegate for his homeworld in the Aetherium Concord Assembly as part of the Calustra delegation, being elected in 52 BKC. His role also made him the delegate for the rest of the Chommell sector, meaning he represented a total of thirty-six planets. Once elected, Kaelith quickly joined incumbent First Minister Skor Kalpana's ruling party. He was followed by a grouping of aides who loyally followed him since his days on Calustra. He noticed the fervent bickering and corruption that mired the Concord. He began to establish relations with those who would become his future collaborators, such as Ostren Vail. The duo met for the first time when Vail was still a cadet of the Tarnak Sector Spacefarers Academy, and while Kaelith was still apprenticed to Malcor.
