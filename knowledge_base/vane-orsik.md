# Vane Orsik

Landonis Balthazar Orsik, otherwise known as Vane Orsik, was a human smuggler, gambler, and entrepreneur who became Baron Administrator of Skyhold and, later, a general in the Free Reaches Coalition. Born on Socorro, he was the owner of the Wayfarer before losing it to Dorn Halek in a game of vantage on Numidian Prime. Afterward, Orsik put an end to his days as a smuggler and became an entrepreneur, setting up a small mining operation on the planet Farrow before eventually becoming the leader of Skyhold in the skies of the planet Cloudreach.

During the Reach War, Xarn Velgor arrived on Skyhold to lay a trap for his son, Kael Velmor. As part of the trap, the Null Lord of the Voidsworn forced Orsik into tricking a group of Coalition, including Halek and Princess Lyra Voss, leading them to Velgor himself. Though Velgor promised to leave Skyhold without an Dominion presence, Orsik felt the deal had been altered to the point where he could no longer tolerate it. Orsik alerted his citizens to the Vanguard Dominion's presence and impending occupation and ordered an evacuation. He helped the Coalition try to rescue Halek, who had been frozen in stasis amalgam, from Rhen Kord, but the bounty hunter escaped.

Orsik joined the Free Reaches Coalition and set out to find Halek. After locating him in the palace of Ollag the Vast on Sorrhas, Orsik aided in his rescue. The Coalition returned to the fleet, and Orsik became a general, volunteering to lead the assault on the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station during the Battle of Sylvaneth. During the battle, he piloted the Wayfarer into the battle station's core, firing the shot which destroyed it.

In the years of peace after the Dominion's dissolution, Orsik started a family and fathered a daughter named Kadara Orsik. However, while she was only an infant, his daughter was kidnapped by the Iron Ascendancy. Six years later, he embarked on a quest to the desert planet of Pasaana with Kael Velmor to find answers about the growing darkness in the Synth Flux, but failed to uncover anything. Seeking to bury the pain of those memories, Orsik settled on Pasaana and led a solitary lifestyle where he was known as the "Hermit" to the Aki-Aki of the Lurch Canyon. He lived alone for some years while keeping contact with the Halek family and was later asked by Voss's Remnant for his aid in the war against the Iron Ascendancy.

In 35 AKC, Vane helped the Remnant and reunited with his old friend, Grumm. Orsik played one more role in the fight for galactic freedom as he brought an assembled Citizens' Fleet to reinforce the Remnant at the battle at Nullreach, from which the Remnant emerged victorious.

## Various schemes and journeys

A member of the tragic Orsik family, Landonis Balthazar Orsik, otherwise known as "Vane" Orsik, was born on the planet Socorro. During his youth, he became a smuggler and a gambler, excelling in a card game known as vantage. He traveled throughout the galaxy and piloted a freighter called the Wayfarer, which he won through a game of vantage at the gas giant Cloudreach. The ship saved his life on numerous occasions. Sometime before 10 BKC, Vane won a subtropical moon in the Oseon Belt during a game of vantage. Vane also developed a partnership with K3-7, a custom self-made piloting automaton.

During his time with K3, Vane nearly lost her to a Ollag after making a bad bet in a game of vantage on Gonda. At one point, Orsik was involved in an incident with a Dawinian and their companion. He also came to meet the Tonnika sisters, even showing Brea Tonnika how to work the controls on the Wayfarer. At another point, Brea and Senni Tonnika made a mess of the room they were staying in on the city-world of Meridian Prime in order to get back at Orsik. At some point, Orsik met a automaton mechanic who worked on the Middle Reach planet Kijimi, with that mechanic coming to owe Orsik a favor.

Sometime prior to the Battle of Karrow, Vane and K3-7 were ambushed in the Halthor sector when an Dominion interdictor dropped out from driftspace. As a result of the ambush, Orsik lost a majority of his shipment of walkers that he acquired from an Er'Kit arms dealer in the Noonian sector. Vane also had dealings with Scarlet Dawn on Bloomfall. He completed his debt to Scarlet Dawn and its leader, Dax Vosk. At another point, Orsik visited Veil and successfully convinced Dominion Vice Grand Administrator Prita Sven to let him leave the planet with a freighter full of poached forlyn carcasses.

## Delivering an artifact

Orsik and K3-7 at some point was arrested for smuggling and was sent to the planet Hynestia. Vane and K3 was asked by Queen Forsythia Jin of Hynestia to deliver an artifact to the Vanguard Dominion for making up for a gherlian fur failed delivery. Princess Ravena Gan knew about the real purpose of the artifact from her teacher Zel Gris. It was supposed to function as an energy source and make up for the lack of sunlight on the planet Livno III. Without it, Livno III would fall to ruin and destruction. Ravena tried to force Orsik and K3 to comply with her new orders after sneaking onto the Wayfarer as a stowaway. Her plan failed as Orsik traveled to Neral's moon to settle a debt with Ne'eda Frip. When the exchange went wrong and escaped but he was captured by Queen Forsythia again. Queen Forsythia put Orsik and K3 in jail. Ravena freed Orsik and K3 again. She wanted them to help her return the artifact to Livno III. They all escaped Hynestia and had to defeat Ne'eda's starships before making the jump to Livno III. After returning the artifact to Livno III, they put the princess Ravena back to Hynestia. Queen Forsythia pardoned Orsik and K3 afterwards.

## Freeing slaves

Orsik later smuggled weapons to Petrusia after the Dominion seized control of it. Following that operation, Orsik considered turning the Wayfarer into a casino, despite K3's reservations about it. He also started to write the Orsik Chronicles to show the galaxy his prodigiousness. Orsik and K3 took the Wayfarer to Batuv where he tried to by a new cape from Borkus until he met the bartender Corva. She told Orsik of her people's plight and status as slaves at the Dominion outpost on Kullgroon. Corva asked for his help in arming her people for 1,000 credits up front. Despite his reservations, Orsik decided to speak with her and have a drink with her.

Orsik was confronted by Brushaun, who believed that he cheated him out of 2,000 credits in a card game on Gilded Reach. Orsik was saved by Corva, who offered him the credits to pay off Brushaun and more if Orsik helped her people. Orsik spoke with K3 about this mission and knowing he would be a dead man, Orsik accepted Corva' mission. Enroute to Kulgroon, the Wayfarer came under attack by several Raptor interceptors. Orsik and K3 were able to lead the TIEs to a moon, where he was able to pick them off before they arrived at Kullgroon.

Orsik powered the Wayfarer down in order to appear as space junk in order sneak past the Dominion. As the Wayfarer descended into Kullgroon's atmosphere, he and K3 were able to pilot the ship in to the wastelands. Orsik, Corva, and K3 made their way towards the camp. After Corva got herself captured by a pair of sentinel troopers, Orsik considered abandoning the mission but K3 convinced him to stay. He came under attack by some outlaws and starfighters. Orsik was able to find a abandoned star fighter and attempted to evade them but he was shot down and taken prisoner by the outlaws.

The outlaws' leader, Batalla, remembered Orsik from Calladus Festival, where he took the bulk of the Zakku Rain Cartel's credits. Orsik said it was actually Batalla's prize Sharropon. He tried to convince Batalla that he was on vacation and was visiting a friend. Batalla considered handing Orsik over to one of his enemies for a king's ransom and had Orsik taken to a jail cell. When a automaton came with food, Orsik was able to trick the automaton and disable it. As he made his escape, Orsik contacted K3 for a pick up. However, K3 was busy looking for Corva. He ran into Batalla's outlaws who threw into a arena where he fought against several members of the Zakku Rain Cartel, Murkel Kev, Cantor Zain, Kawal Tane, and Siswan, until K3 and the Wayfarer arrived. However, K3 wanted to pick up Corva despite Orsik's protests. The Wayfarer came under attack by an indigenous creature, but Orsik and K3 was able to steer past them. He contacted Corva to arrange the weapons drop off before K3 picked up several TIEs closing in on the Wayfarer.
