# Vat soldier

Vat soldiers, also known as Vatborn soldiers, Concord troopers, Concord troops, Concord soldiers, Regs, and nicknamed the "Boys in White," were highly trained soldiers in the Concord Host. Representing the future of galactic warfare, vatborn were designed to be far superior to battle automatons. During the last years of the Aetherium Concord, vatborn formed the backbone of the Concord Military, which waged war against the automaton armies of the League of Free Systems. The Vat Wars were named after the Concord's vat army, which became symbolic of the conflict that had spread throughout the galaxy after a millennium of peace.

Vat soldiers were grown on the planet Tidewell at the request of Flux Master Coran Seth, who commissioned the Tidewellers to build an army for the Concord. After Coran Seth' death, the project was taken over by the Voidsworn. Vatborn were modeled on a human template, the Riftguard bounty hunter Tarek Kord, although their genetic structure was modified to make them less independent and more docile than their progenitor. They were also designed to age at twice the rate of natural humans, accelerating their growth and making them ready for combat in a decade's time. As the Secessionist Crisis gave way to open war, the Concord Assembly empowered First Minister Sev Kaelith to call the vat soldiers into action, leading to their debut on the battlefield of Hivekar. For three years, vatborn fought the Secessionist automatons on all fronts across the galaxy. They were loyal to their Fluxadept Generals and the First Minister; though some vatborn questioned their service, leading to isolated cases of desertion and treason, most were proud to serve the Concord as it was the only life they had ever known.

Galactic history was forever changed by a hidden behavioral modification biochip implanted in every vatborn, which was part of the Voidsworn's plot to destroy the Fluxadept. First Minister Kaelith, discovered by the Fluxadept to be the Voidsworn Lord Kaelith Rau, as well as the reason behind the Vat Wars, branded the Fluxadept as the enemies of the state and issued Protocol 66, with the latter being a call for the annihilation of the Flux Order. All across the galaxy, the vatborn turned against their generals, slaughtering them and storming the Flux Temple on Meridian Prime under the stewardship of the Voidsworn Lord Xarn Velgor. Rau subsequently assumed absolute power as the self-proclaimed Sovereign of the Vanguard Dominion. Vatborn initially served as Dominion sentinel troopers, or vatborn sentinel troopers, in the New Order but were eventually replaced by birth-born human recruits and conscripts. Their armor, weapons, and training inspired generations of armored warriors following on from their Dominion successors; with both the Iron Ascendancy's sentinel troopers and the Voidsworn Eternal's red-armored soldiers having been trained from early childhood to become super-soldiers much like the vat army. In particular, the latter were indoctrinated using a modified and improved upon version of the flash-training techniques previously used for the vatborn.

## Origin

Years before the Invasion of Calustra, Flux Master Coran Seth came to believe that the galaxy would soon become engulfed by war. He therefore advocated for the creation of an army to ensure the defense of the Aetherium Concord, but his views were ultimately rejected by his peers and he was cast out of the Flux High Council. Undeterred by the lack of support from the other Fluxadept, Coran Seth secretly contacted the Tidewellers—a species of experts in replication, native to the planet Tidewell—and commissioned them to create an army of vatborn soldiers for the Concord without authorization from either High Council or the Concord Assembly. Seth personally met with the Tideweller Prime Minister, Lama Su. However, the Voidsworn Lord Kaelith Rau learned of Seth' order through unknown means.

The Voidsworn apprentice Volen, who was actually Coran Seth' friend and the former Flux Master Volen, later claimed Seth had worked with him to create the vat army. Shortly afterward, Coran Seth was killed by the Vess Syndicate at the behest of the Voidsworn, resulting in the Voidsworn's takeover of the vat soldier project. Volen betrayed and orchestrated Coran Seth' death, and subsequently recruited the Riftguard bounty hunter Tarek Kord to become the genetic template of the vat army. Various modifications were made to the Kord genome so that the vatborn would grow faster and be more susceptible to following orders. Hyper-aggressive and volatile tendencies were tailored; the Tidewellers resorted to growth acceleration in order to create mature vatborn in half the time a human took to reach maturity. Kord's genetic structure was further modified to make the vatborn less independent and more docile than their template. However, the vatborn were also designed to be capable of creative thinking, making them far superior to battle automatons. After being created on Tidewell, some vatborn were secretly raised on Meridian Prime to become the shock troopers of the Meridian Prime Guard. Kord became a near-mythologized figure to his vatborn, who regarded him as their father figure. Kord himself, however, did not feel a sense of pride in his vatborn, instead seeing them as "livestock" made to be "cannon fodder." As such, he considered them to be the Tidewellers' creations instead of his.

Kord was paid a considerable amount of credits for allowing the Tidewellers to use his genetic material as the vat army's template; his only other demand was the creation of an unaltered vatborn of himself, which the Tidewellers found unusual but nevertheless obliged. Whereas the vat army was genetically modified for growth acceleration and enhanced docility, Alpha was an example of pure genetic replication and therefore possessed pure, first generation Kord DNA. The only other vatborn like Alpha was an adolescent female known as Omega. Kord took Alpha to raise as his son, renaming the vatborn "Rhen Kord." Omega remained in the custody of the Tidewellers and was serving as an aide of the Chief Medical Scientist Doctor Nala Se by the end of the Vat Wars. Like the rest of the Kord vatborn, they were regarded as Tideweller property by Lama Su.

Although the Tidewellers believed that the army was meant to serve the Concord, the Voidsworn intended to use the vat soldiers as their secret weapon against the Flux Order. The vat army's true purpose was to exterminate the Fluxadept, and to that end, an organic inhibitor chip was implanted into the brain of every vat soldier at the third stage of their development. Coran Seth, who had originally thought of the chip, intended for it to be a safeguard against rogue Fluxadept, but following his death, Rau and Volen retooled the chip to ensure the Fluxadept's destruction. Upon the activation of the retooled chip, the vatborn would be brainwashed into believing that Fluxadept were traitors to the Concord, forcing the vatborn to execute the Fluxadept. Volen, who posed as a Fluxadept, tricked the Tidewellers into believing Coran Seth' intention. One chip failed in the case of Tup, causing him to betray and kill a Fluxadept before the order was given. Officially, the chips were supposed to make the vatborn less aggressive and independent than Tarek Kord. The significance of the inhibitor chip was supported by the assertion that a vatborn would become mentally unstable and dangerous to everyone around him if his chip was damaged or surgically removed. The chips were, in fact, created to compel the vat soldiers to execute Protocol 66—a secret protocol that authorized the destruction of the Flux Order.

Each vat soldier had an identifying code inserted in his left wrist. During the Vat Wars, the codes were linked to the file of every individual trooper in the Concord's military database. The files, which could be accessed by an astromech automaton via the scanning of a vatborn's identifying code, contained the vatborn' designation, rank and overall service record, as well as any specialized training and the units that he was affiliated with. Most vatborn also shared their template's dark-brown eyes, black hair, and olive skin. While there were some genetic variations, most of these vatborn would ultimately remain confined to Tidewell, being embarrassments or case studies. Only five vatborn anomalies remained by the end of the war, including Nala Se's assistant Omega, an enhanced female vatborn. The other four vatborn formed the original lineup of Vatborn Flux 99. Hunter, Tech, Wrecker, and Crosshair were genetically enhanced vatborn who possessed unique combat skills, setting them apart from the standard "reg" vat soldiers as well as each other.
