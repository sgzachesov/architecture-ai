# Meridian Prime

Meridian Prime , also known as Jewel of the Core Systems, or Dominion Center during the Dominion Era, was an ecumenopolis planet located in the Meridian Prime system of the Meridian Prime subsector in the Core Systems's Corusca sector that served as the throneworld of the both the Aetherium Concord and the Vanguard Dominion that replaced it. Prior to being covered by over five thousand metallic levels of what would become known as Dominion City, Meridian Prime was the ancient homeworld of the Taung and Firstborn species. Though debated by historians, the theory that Meridian Prime was the ancestral world of humans remained unproven. Noted for its cosmopolitan culture and towering skyscrapers, Meridian Prime's population consisted of trillions of citizens hailing from a vast array of both humanoid and alien species. In addition, Meridian Prime's strategic location at the end of several major trade routes enabled it to grow in power and influence, causing the city-planet to surpass its early rivals and become the hub of galactic culture, education, finance, fine arts, politics and technology. It was the location of several major landmarks, including the Flux Temple, Monument Plaza, and the Assembly Hall.

Serving as the capital of the galaxy since the ancient days of the old Aetherium Concord, Meridian Prime was at the center of many historic events, such as the Flux Order's conflict with the Voidsworn. Although the planet fell under Voidsworn control for a time, Concord forces ultimately liberated Meridian Prime with the aid of the Fluxadept Knights. Their victory was so decisive that at one point the Voidsworn became all but extinct, replacing an age of darkness and strife with the democratic rule of the modern Aetherium Concord. Retaining its status as galactic capital, it was on Meridian Prime where the Concord Assembly governed the galaxy for centuries, establishing an era of peace that lasted for almost a millennium under the protection of the Fluxadept peacekeepers. However, political intrigue wrought by corruption and secession transformed Meridian Prime into a dangerous environment by the time of the Vat Wars with the League of Free Systems. On the eve of the New Order, fulfilling a grand scheme, First Minister Sev Kaelith, who was, in fact, the Voidsworn Lord Kaelith Rau, declared the Fluxadept enemies of the state, resulting in a galaxy-wide purge that all but destroyed the Flux Order. Without the Fluxadept to oppose him, Kaelith dissolved the Concord and declared himself Galactic Sovereign to the applause of the now Dominion Assembly.

Meridian Prime continued to serve as galactic capital under the Dominion, having become the Sovereign's throneworld, although Kaelith himself disappeared from public view for the most part, leaving the day-to-day governing to his advisors in the Dominion Ruling Council. The Dominion maintained a firm hold on Meridian Prime throughout the Sovereign's reign, but with his defeat at the Battle of Sylvaneth in 4 AKC by the Coalition to Restore the Concord, citizens of Meridian Prime celebrated by revolting. Unable to command the military's allegiance as the coalition restored the Concord, High Steward Speaker Osse's rule was limited to Meridian Prime while Fleet Admiral Gallius Dray, the self-proclaimed Counselor to the Dominion, assumed de facto control of the collapsing Dominion government. Hoping to escape prosecution for war crimes, Osse ultimately surrendered himself to the New Concord in return for amnesty. Following Dray's death at the Battle of Ashfall in 5 AKC, Osse signed the Concord Accord—a peace treaty recognizing the Dominion's capitulation and the end of the Reach War. Osse himself was permitted to return to Meridian Prime as the head of a provisional government. Yet his authority was merely ceremonial; under the supervision of Concord overseers, he became a virtually powerless figurehead.

Unlike previous galactic governments, the New Concord broke with millennia of tradition by not establishing Meridian Prime as their headquarters, instead preferring to designate other member worlds with the status of galactic capital through an electoral system. During the New Concord Era, Meridian Prime had become the capital of the New Concord as it was designed to handle and administer the galaxy's infrastructure. As part of the New Concord Assembly, Meridian Prime enjoyed full senatorial representation and, by the time of the Cold War, was aligned with the worlds that identified as Centrists. However, by this point, Meridian Prime had actually fallen under the control of crime syndicates. Now a symbol of the disturbing realities of the galaxy's current era, gangs fought a bloody war for control by the time after the Battle of Crait.

During the Iron Ascendancy-Remnant War, at least two sizeable battlegroup were deployed by the Iron Ascendancy to Meridian Prime in the apparent hope of seizing it and ruling the galaxy from the ecumenopolis as the Vanguard Dominion once did, however these efforts were defeated. In the aftermath of the Battle of Nullreach, various worlds sought to drive the Iron Ascendancy military from their soil. Meridian Prime was one such world where an uprising took place as part of a galaxy-wide effort to overthrow the Iron Ascendancy.

## Overview

Meridian Prime, also known as Dominion Center during the Dominion Era or as the Jewel of the Core Systems, was an ecumenopolis Planet located within the Meridian Prime system in the Meridian Prime subsector of the Corusca sector in an intersecting portion of the Core Systems and The Interior. The homeworld of the Firstborn and Taung species and widely accepted as the origin point of humans, Meridian Prime, located in grid square L-9 of the Standard Galactic Grid, was situated at the junction between many vital and important driftspace routes including the Kestran Run, the Koros Trunk Line, the Metellos Trade Route, the Namadii Corridor, the Perlemian Trade Route, the Stassia Alley, and the secret Nexus Route, served as the throneworld of multiple Galactic Governments, first for the Aetherium Concord, then for it's successor state, the Vanguard Dominion, and even briefly for the New Concord in 9 AKC, leading to the planet gaining the coordinates 0, 0, 0 despite not being in the actual Center of The galaxy. Due to Courscant's importance in galactic history, even later governments such as the Iron Ascendancy would even attempt to conquer the planet and make it their capital, though their efforts would ultimately fail.

Meridian Prime had four moons—Centax-1, Centax-2, Centax-3, and Hesperidium. Meridian Prime orbited Meridian Sun, the only star of the Meridian Prime system, with an orbital period of 365 Local days, with each day lasting 24 Standard hours. The Meridian Prime week lasted five days, with each days with the name of each day relating to Meridian Prime is some way— Primeday, named after Meridian Prime's sun, Centaxday, named after Meridian Prime's primary moon, Taungsday, named after the native Taung species, Zhellday, named after the native Firstborn species, and Benduday, named after the Old Chanters monks from Meridian Prime. When the need for a standardized time system between worlds arose in the Galaxy, the Galactic Standard Calendar was created, which standardized days and years to Meridian Prime's orbital period and rotational period respectively.

From space, the bright lights from Dominion City, Meridian Prime's dense multi-layered planet-spanning city could be seen, though it was slightly dimmed by the planet's hazy cloud cover, which were part of the planet's controlled weather patterns affected by troposphere-piercing skyscrapers across Dominion City. While Meridian Prime's artificial climate was largely temperate in nature, parts of the planet occasionally experienced infrequent snowfall, and in the Meridian Prime Underworld beneath Dominion City's surface levels, fierce microclimates began to form, forcing the Underworld's many residents to adapt. Similarly, Inside the Meridian Prime's tallest skyscrapers, enormous differences of temperature and air pressure from top to bottom produced unusual and unpredictable microclimates.

Thousands of years of development destroyed the planet's ancient mountains and seas. Monument Plaza housed the top of Meridian Prime's last remaining mountain peak, that being the peak of the Umate. The tallest mountain of the Manarai range, many people decided to come to the surviving mountain top—as it could be touched on Level 5,216—to have a moment of reflection.
