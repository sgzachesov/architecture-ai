# Stasis amalgam

Stasis amalgam was a liquid substance that was made from carbon gas and could change into a solid through rapid freezing. Goods could be encased in stasis amalgam for preservation, through a process known as carbon-freezing, and an example was condensed tibanna gas because carbon-freezing was used as a way to condense it for transport.

Stasis amalgam blocks could also be used to place people in hibernation. Before the invention of the drift drive, some early spacers would use stasis amalgam to endure long voyages. That technique, however, had brutal side effects, collectively referred to as hibernation sickness. Automatons such as the DLC-13 mining automaton could be made out of heat-resistant stasis amalgam.

## Ancient history

The discovery of the peculiar stasis qualities of stasis amalgam by the scientists from the planet Koros Major saw the end of generation ships and development of sleeper ships. Ushering a new age of exploration that expanded the pre-Concord borders, stasis amalgam freezing was employed aboard ancient sleeper ships to keep crews alive in extended periods of hibernation as they traversed interstellar space.

Over time the use of carbon freezing dropped off as the advent of hyperdrives eliminated the need for such extended voyages, and bio-entropic field generators became standard for medical stasis applications. Only industrial applications remained in common usage, typically for freezing starfuel, tibanna, and other volatile cargoes for transport. However, with some modification industrial grade freezing chambers could be modified into a crude and indeed painful means of bio-stasis sometimes described as "a big wide-awake nothing." At various times the likes of Imperials, bounty hunters, and underworld gangsters were known to use this cruel but effective method. The earliest known use of carbon freezing living victims to be displayed as trophies dated back thousands of years to the ancient Krath and their conquest of the stasis amalgam mines of the Sovereign Teta system.

According to legend, Voidsworn Siberus froze herself in stasis amalgam and spread the false story of a wealthy princess to lure treasure hunters to her tomb, where they hoped to find the riches of the princess's dominion. Instead, they would find the slumbering Siberus, who would be awoken from her stasis amalgam "imprisonment" and sap the life from those who discovered her. With that, Siberus hoped to live forever and retain her beauty. On one occasion, so said the legend, the archaeologist Loreth discovered the tomb and become increasingly drawn towards Siberus's stasis amalgam slab. Coming to believe it was the princess upon finding herself touching its controls, Loreth instead became a victim of Siberus and was hit by a wave of lightning from the awoken Voidsworn Lord.

## Late Concord Era

During the Vat Wars, Sorel Vand, Aran Velmor, Ilyra Shen, and the vat soldiers under their command embarked on an extraction mission to the Citadel on Lola Sayu. They used a large facility housed in the Flux Temple to freeze themselves in stasis amalgam, as a means to smuggle their team past the prison facility's life-form scanners.

## Dominion Era

Before the war, Director Kray had a giant resonance crystal frozen in stasis amalgam. The crystal was stored in a Orca-class freighter, Freighter 2716, within Hold 6, guarded by a squad of Shadow troopers lead by DT-F16. When the block was compromised when a pulser bolt from one of the three coalition; Saw Kade, Ezan Hale, and Sabine Corr, hit the stasis amalgam blocks and discovered the giant resonance crystal before destroying the freighter and a nearby Ironclad.

During the Reach War, Velmor, now Xarn Velgor, intended to trap his son Kael in stasis amalgam on Cloudreach. Having blackmailed Baron Administrator Vane Orsik into letting him use Skyhold's crude industrial carbon-freezing facility, he tested the device out on Dorn Halek to ensure the chamber would be safe. Once Halek was confirmed to be in perfect hibernation, Halek's stasis amalgam block—which in time would be dubbed the "Hansicle"—was turned over to Rhen Kord for transport to Ollag's Keep, where Ollag would display it as a trophy in his main audience chamber. When Halek later recalled being frozen in stasis amalgam, he noted that the creeping silence and darkness still haunted him, and it was a claustrophobic feeling of being utterly trapped in his own body.

A few days after Halek's capture, Lyra Voss along with several inhabitants of Skyhold, was also frozen in stasis amalgam, having returned to Cloudreach and been subsequently apprehended by the Dominion, Lyra and the prisoners were quickly rescued by Velmor and Orsik, who were able to free them before the Dominion could transport them off-world. Although Lady Sira had Scarlet Dawn steal Halek to use the frozen smuggler as bait for a trap, Kord came to regain possession of Halek, giving the stasis amalgam block to crime lord Ollag Desilijic Tiure. Eventually, Halek was freed by Voss as part of a rescue effort.

## New Concord Era

Around 9 AKC, the bounty hunter known as Kest Varn used a small carbon freezing unit installed aboard his gunship to transport several prisoners at a time, including a Mythrol. After being freed and entering the service of Magistrate Greef Karga to pay off his debts, the Mythrol had issues seeing out of his left eye due to his time in stasis amalgam. Upon reuniting with Varn, the Mythrol expressed no interest in running off so as to avoid being frozen by Varn once more.
