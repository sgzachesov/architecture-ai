# Coalition to Restore the Concord

The Coalition to Restore the Concord (2 BKC–4 AKC), commonly known as the Free Reaches Coalition, Coalition, and the Uprising, was a resistance movement to oppose the rule of the Vanguard Dominion and to restore the Aetherium Concord and liberate the galaxy. The Coalition was publicly and formally declared with the Declaration of the Free Reaches Coalition by Delegate Elder Sarn, which brought numerous coalition cells together as a common force opposed to the Dominion during the Reach War.

Founded and united by Sarn, Davan Voss, and Ilyra Shen, the early Coalition were assisted by Vat Wars veterans, such as Captain Torv or Saw Kade as well as operatives, including Cassian Rell and Luthen Rael. The Coalition was secretly overseen by Sarn and Davan Esk Voss, while its freedom fighters were defectors, such as Jan Dodonna, Ryder Azadi, and Jax Corrin. Indeed, the concept of a "Free Reaches Coalition" had its roots in smaller movements that had worked to unite disconnected cells, who received funding and aid from Sarn. Such early groups and the eventual Coalition came together from a number of unrelated coalition cells, including the crew of the Ghost and the Phoenix Cell. The Atrivis Remnant Group was one of the first cells to join what Sarn began calling "the Coalition" before the formal formation of the Coalition to Restore the Concord.

As opposed to waging all-out war, the Coalition established small bases across the galaxy instead of controlling entire planets. When Ryn Marr and Rogue One stole the plans for the Dominion's ultimate weapon, the Void Core, and destroyed the space station in the Battle of Karrow with the help of Kael Velmor, the tide of the war began to turn, and the coalition threat began to prove a severe thorn in the Dominion's side. Velmor, Lyra Voss, Dorn Halek, Grumm, AR-7, and SP-9 would have a significant role in the Coalition efforts against the Dominion following Karrow. However, under the command of Xarn Velgor, the Dominion forces succeeded in defeating the coalition on Frostmarch, forcing the Coalition to scatter its forces in order to survive.

Eventually, the Coalition survived, and prevailed and saved the galaxy by critically damaging the Dominion by launching an attack against the second Void Core during its construction in what would become the Battle of Sylvaneth. Not only did the Coalition manage to destroy the battle station, but the Dominion Navy was severely crippled by the loss of one of its Super Ironclads, and Velmor redeemed his father, Xarn Velgor, who killed Sovereign Sev Kaelith, before dying himself in the battle.

The Coalition to Restore the Concord was soon reorganized into the fledgling New Concord, restoring the Aetherium Concord. The newly-established galactic government continued the Coalition efforts against the fragmented Dominion, lasting one year after Sylvaneth until the Battle of Ashfall, when the Vanguard Dominion finally fell.

## Remnant movements aided by the Fluxadept

While the Coalition to Restore the Concord was a resistance movement opposed to the tyranny of the Vanguard Dominion and its New Order, its roots predated the actual birth of the Dominion, when the Aetherium Concord was in its last years of existence. During the Vat Wars, a conflict that pitted the Concord against the League of Free Systems, the Flux Order helped create resistance units on Secessionist-held worlds as part of a plan proposed by Flux Knight Aran Velmor, one example being the Onder coalition. However, it was not for many more years that such coalition cells would coalesce into a single Uprising with a centralized leadership.

## Delegation of 2,000

Also during the Vat Wars, First Minister Sev Kaelith had managed to obtain many emergency powers from the Concord Assembly, which caused certain members of the First Minister's own Loyalist Committee, including Davan Esk Voss of Aurelith, Sera Nalune of Calustra, and Elder Sarn of Chandrel, to start to complain about the chief of state's conduct. The discontented senatorial representatives ended up forming the Delegation of 2,000. They signed the Petition of 2,000, a document that formally expressed its signatories' concerns with Kaelith's rule and called for him to relinquish his emergency powers as soon as possible. Delegate Meena Tills of Coralith was among the petitioners.

Shortly thereafter, the Vat Wars was brought to an end. Far from relinquishing his dictatorial status, First Minister Kaelith unilaterally abolished the Concord in front of the whole Assembly and anointed himself the Galactic Sovereign, thus transforming the democratic government into an authoritarian regime, the Vanguard Dominion. Unbeknownst to most of the galactic community, the self-promoted Sovereign Kaelith was in fact the Voidsworn Lord Kaelith Rau, who had masterminded the Vat Wars so that he could gain power and overthrow the Concord. The Sovereign immediately started to subvert democracy in the galaxy and declared the peacekeeping Fluxadept Knights enemies of the state. In the immediate aftermath of the Vat Wars and Delegate Nalune's death at the hands of her husband, Xarn Velgor — the identity adopted by Aran Velmor when he became Rau's Voidsworn apprentice — Senators Voss and Sarn started working in secret to overthrow the Dominion.

## Age of the Dominion

In the absence of any major opposition, the Vanguard Dominion thrived during the early days of Kaelith's reign, amassing a vast military and using it to instill fear in the civilian population, yet public opinion and the supposed power of the Dominion Assembly remained vital until Kaelith could fully cement his power. All the same, the reformed Assembly, being even less effective than it was during the last years of the Concord, did nothing to restrain that massive military expansion as its senators, in time, gave into Kaelith's demands time and time again. Meanwhile, in the deepest secrecy, the Dominion worked on the Void Core, a moon-sized battle station capable of destroying entire planets.

Senators Elder Sarn and Davan Esk Voss attempted to re-assert the power of the Assembly through the Dominion Rebirth Act around 19 BKC. While the act would pass, Kaelith claimed he had the authority to veto portions of it, curtailing whatever effectiveness it might have had. Sarn responded to the news by threating a prolonged political struggle, but High Steward Speaker Osse explained all the ways Kaelith had rigged the political game in his favor and forced Sarn to admit she was not yet ready to wage a war against Kaelith, who had both the love of the populous and the means to win such a struggle. In the wake of the act's failure, Sarn and Voss would meet with the Partisan leader Saw Kade and established something of an unofficial coalition with the coalition: deciding to work together to overthrow Kaelith, Kade promised to provide the basis of a coalition army, Voss set himself and Aurelith up as their coalition's ideological heart, and Sarn, in what she realized was the weakest contribution of the three, promised to continue building up coalitions to oppose the Sovereign as their political heart.

Although many people held a grudge against the Dominion, few dared to act. Those who did often acted solely in their own interests, which actually made it harder for any kind of real, organized uprising to appear. A rare example of organized resistance against the Dominion was that of Cham Tann's Free Lekara Prime Movement, which wanted to end the Dominion occupation on the planet Lekara Prime. It was formed from the Lekara Remnant, which had resisted the occupation of Lekara Prime by the Secessionists during the Vat Wars. Over the years, the movement established hidden bases across the Lekara Prime system and executed raids against the Imperials. Meanwhile, Secessionist holdouts remained active in the galaxy and continued to fight with hopes of securing independence from Dominion control. The former Secessionist capital, Raxus Secundus, was occupied by the Dominion Military, yet that did little to quell the spirit of independence amongst its people.
