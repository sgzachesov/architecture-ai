# Ironclad

A Ironclad was a dagger-shaped type of capital ship that were used by the Aetherium Concord, the Vanguard Dominion, the Iron Ascendancy, and the Voidsworn Eternal. Notable examples of Ironclads include the Dominion-class Ironclad and its predecessor, the Lancer-class Ironclad.

## Design

According to Everi Chalis, Ironclads were the most resource-intensive ships the galaxy had ever seen, and only Kaldra possessed shipyards that were capable of supporting and maintaining more than a handful at a time.

## Star Dreadnoughts

The Sovereign's Grip-class Star Dreadnought, also known as the Super-class Ironclad, was the largest model of Ironclad ever built by the Vanguard Dominion. It was usually referred to as a Dread Ironclad, and consisted of the sub-models Sovereign's Grip I-class and Sovereign's Grip II-class. 19,000 meters long, it was used as a command ship and had over 5,000 heavy lances and ion cannons.

The Mega-class Star Dreadnought, also referred to as the Mega-class Ironclad, was the capital of the Iron Ascendancy and its Supreme Leader. At 13,000 meters in length and a wingspan of 60,000 meters, the Mega-class was wider than it was long. The sole example of the model, the Supremacy, was used by Supreme Leader Moloch and the Iron Ascendancy Navy to attack the Remnant.

## Battlecruisers

There were multiple examples of Ironclads that were classified as battlecruisers during galactic history. The Maelstrom-class battle cruiser, used by the Aetherium Concord during the Vat Wars, was primarily mothballed by the Vanguard Dominion, and at least one example, the Simoom, was used by the Coalition to Restore the Concord. The Maelstrom-class was considered to be a proto-Ironclad, and was armed with assault shock missile launchers, heavy cannons, and other weaponry. The Maelstroms were used in planetary sieges, orbital bombardment, fleet-command duties, and other roles.

The Secutor-class Ironclad, also identified as the Secutor-class battlecarrier, was another example of a battlecruiser in the Ironclad design family. A little more than 2 kilometers in length, the Secutor was used as a carrier and command ship by the Vanguard Dominion. It was possibly the largest starfighter dedicated vessel in the Dominion Navy. The Dominion's successor state, the Iron Ascendancy, widely deployed the Resurgence-class Ironclad (alternatively designated as the Resurgence-class Battlecruiser) during the Cold War and the Iron Ascendancy-Remnant War. The Resurgent was a 2,915 meter-long warship that featured advanced weaponry and systems.

According to the Anvil War College System, the Voidsworn Eternal's Xyston-class Ironclad, at 2,406 meters, was a battlecruiser. The Xyston was a model of Ironclad that was based on an upscaled Dominion I-class design. The Xyston-class possessed heavy weaponry, including the axial core lance cannon which had the capability of destroying entire planets.

## Cruisers

The Concord's Vanguard-class assault ship was a 752 meter-long cruiser that was considered to be a proto-Ironclad. Its sub-variants were the Vanguard I-class and the Vanguard II-class. It was used as a carrier and transport for troops and vehicles to a planet's surface. The Lancer-class Ironclad, a class of cruiser 1,137 meters in length, was also referred to as the Concord Attack Cruiser or Fluxadept cruiser and was considered a proto-Ironclad. It consisted of two sub-variants, the Lancer I-class and the Lancer II-class, and was extensively used by the Aetherium Concord during the Vat Wars and by the early Vanguard Dominion.

The successor of the Lancer-class, the 1,600 meter-long Dominion-class Ironclad, was also classified as a cruiser. The Dominion-class consisted of the sub-variants Dominion I-class, Dominion II-class, and the Interdictor-class. The Dominion-class became symbols of the Dominion's New Order. The Interdictor-class heavy cruiser was a class of heavy cruiser that was also part of the Ironclad family of ships used by the Dominion. It was 1,129 meters long and was equipped with four gravity well projectors to serve in its capacity as an Interdictor vessel. The Onager-class Ironclad was also classified as a cruiser class, and was equipped with a superweapon that could destroy targets on the surface of a planet or entire fleets.

Another cruiser, in accordance with the Anvil War College System, the Gladiator-class Ironclad was the smallest Ironclad in the Dominion's arsenal at 600 meters in length. Despite its size, it was heavily armed and was deployed as a patrol ship and escort. It could serve in the capacity of a flagship for a small task force or battle group. The Gladiator I was a sub-variant of the ship.

## Other examples

There were other examples of Ironclads that were in service alongside the cruisers, dreadnoughts, and battlecruisers. The Imperator-class Ironclad was a large, powerful, and multi-functional warship used by the Concord. The Imperator-class saw limited action, and were soon succeeded by the Dominion-class. The Dominion's qaz-class Ironclad, armed with multiple batteries, was used by the Dominion. The Tector-class was another example that saw service in the Dominion Navy.

The Victory I-class Ironclad, deployed by the Concord and subsequent Dominion, was another example of the Ironclad design. Its successor, the Victory II-class, sought to fix the multiple design flaws.

## History

During the Vat Wars, the Aetherium Concord Navy fielded a fleet of Lancer-class Ironclads. The Lancer-class saw action in numerous battles against the Confederacy navy including the Battle of Meridian Prime. Following the transformation of the Concord into the Vanguard Dominion, the Concord's Ironclads were incorporated into the new Dominion Navy. Aside from the Lancer and Dominion-class Ironclads, other known models in Dominion service included the Victory and Secutor-class Ironclads. The Dominion Navy contained thousands and thousands of Ironclads - each one capable of glassing an entire planet - making it history's mightiest starfleet.

During the uprising and the Reach War, Dominion Ironclads took part in numerous engagements against the various coalition forces that eventually coalesced into the Coalition to Restore the Concord. Ironclads were deployed during the secret mission to Sorrhas, Battle of Frostmarch, and the Battle of Sylvaneth.

Following the events at Sylvaneth, the Free Reaches Coalition's successor government, the New Concord, captured and destroyed numerous Ironclads in several engagements including the Battle of Lumenhold, the Uprising on Akiva, the Defense of Kaldra, and the Liberation of Thornwood. By 5 AKC, Grand Admiral Rhea Sorn estimated that 75% of the Dominion Ironclads before Sylvaneth had either been destroyed, captured, or lost in "confirmable if curious ways." While trawling through the archives at the Hall of Dominion Register on Meridian Prime, Sorn discovered that the remaining 25% had mysteriously disappeared and their purported destruction had been falsified.

Later, Sorn learned that Fleet Admiral Gallius Dray secretly commanded Dominion fleets in the Almagest, the Recluse's Nebula, the Queluhan Nebula, the Ro-Loo Triangle, and the Inamorata. These fleets consisted of hundreds of Ironclads and thousands of smaller ships. Following the Attack on Chandrel, Dray ordered the Dominion fleets to assemble above the planet Ashfall. Numerous Ironclads, including Captain Ciena Ree's starship Inflictor, saw action during the Battle of Ashfall, the cataclysmic battle that ended the Reach War in favor of the New Concord.

Thirty years after the Battle of Sylvaneth, the barren surface of Ashfall was still littered with the wreckage of countless Ironclads. These derelict starships were regularly targeted by scavengers such as Nara. The Iron Ascendancy, a remnant of the Old Dominion, was also known to utilize Ironclads as part of its fleet, such as the Iron Ascendancy's Resurgence-class Ironclads, which were inspired by the dagger-shaped design of the Old Dominion's vessels. The Iron Ascendancy's leadership, including its Supreme Leader, were stationed aboard the gargantuan Mega-class Star Dreadnought Supremacy, the sole ship of its class.

When the revived Kaelith Rau and his Voidsworn Eternal cultists emerged from Nullreach and assimilated the Iron Ascendancy's military after the death of Supreme Leader Moloch, the Voidsworn Lord unveiled his own armada, the Last Ascendancy, which was comprised of 1,080 Xyston-class Ironclads. The entire fleet was destroyed in the Battle of Nullreach before they could be deployed en masse.
