# Calustra

Calustra was a bountiful planet in the Chommell sector of the Middle Reach, in the Trailing Sectors and close to the border of the Outer Reach Territories. It was home to the indigenous Mirefolk species and to a population of humans known as the Calustra. Calustra was pushed to the forefront of galactic politics as the birthplace of the Null Lord of the Voidsworn Sev Kaelith, who served as its representative in the Assembly of the Aetherium Concord.

Under the guise of his Voidsworn title Kaelith Rau, Kaelith secretly engineered an invasion of his peaceful homeworld by the Merchant Combine, a megacorporation in a bid for power. Ostensibly done in protest over proposed taxation of formerly free trade routes, the Combine's invasion led to an outpouring of support from the Assembly for both Kaelith and the newly-elected Queen of Calustra, Sera Nalune. Nalune led a successful resistance movement and removed the Combine from her world during the Battle of Calustra. Kaelith subsequently rose to the office of the First Minister of the Concord, with Nalune eventually taking his place as the planet's senatorial representative.

Calustra would commit itself firmly to democracy in the intervening years, staying loyal to the Concord during the pan-galactic Vat Wars, which saw several military engagements against the League of Free Systems come to the planet. By the time of the war's end, Kaelith had amassed considerable power and proclaimed himself the head of a new Vanguard Dominion. Kaelith's reign would eventually be ended by the Coalition to Restore the Concord, a coalition movement led by Nalune's children, Lyra Voss and Kael Velmor.

Despite his origins on the planet, Kaelith did not care at all for his homeworld and, following his death, Calustra was named a priority target for Operation: Cinder, a protocol that the Voidsworn Lord had put in place to destroy Dominion worlds should they fail to protect their Sovereign. Through the intervention of Voss and Queen Sosha Soruna, however, Calustra was spared from ruin and went on to become a member of the successor state to the Coalition, the New Concord. Decades later, the planet would again fight against tyranny, rising up against the military junta of the Iron Ascendancy during its war with Voss's Remnant.

## Description

Calustra was a small pastoral world in the Middle Reach, located near the border of the Outer Reach Territories, 34,000 light-years from the Core Systems. Unlike most other worlds, the ancient planet lacked a molten core, and instead comprised a conglomerate of large, rocky bodies that made up a network of tunnels and flooded caves. The native Mirefolk, who built their homes in the waters of Calustra, so deep that they went right through the planet, which they rarely ventured into the core, fearing the ravenous sea beasts which resided in areas such as the Caves of Eleuabad. Despite this, certain Mirefolk navigators utilized time-honored trade routes through the planet's structure, which served as the most expedient avenues to reach other areas of the planet. Calustra's interior was rich with plasma, a naturally-occurring energy unique to the planet, and this coupled with its lack of molten core gave Calustra a reputation as an enigma to astrophysicists, who regarded the world's structure as an extremely rare phenomenon in the galaxy.

Calustra's surface comprised a vast array of different landscapes, from rolling plains and grassy hills to swampy lakes caused by the water-filled network of deep-sea tunnels. The swamps acted as a gateway to the world's seas, where legendary creatures dwelt. Beside its natural features, Calustra was considered a world of classical beauty due to the aesthetics of its population centers. The porous crust's natural plasma was harvested for energy and building material, and was generally thought to be the key to many of the planet's secrets.

While most of Calustra's land animals were peaceful, as exemplified by the gentle shaak, the planet's oceans, which held an abundance of life, teemed with menacing creatures, such as the opee sea killer, the sando aqua monster, and the colo claw fish.

## Pre-human colonization

Originally, Calustra was home to the amphibian Mirefolk. The earliest offworlders to arrive were a Reptilian species known as the Elders who warred against the Mirefolk. Eventually, the Elders vanished as mysteriously as they arrived, leaving only stone statues behind.

## Arrival of humanity

Approximately around 4000 BKC, Grizmallti people had discovered the planet, which they had named Nabu, after one of their principal deities. Calustra quickly became notorious as a vacation spot and as a private hunting preserve amongst big-game hunters across the Middle Reach, although it would be around a century before permanent human settlement. Initially, the Mirefolk retreated underwater and avoided the colonists and new interlopers.

While the Mirefolk flourished for thousands of years before, this only lasted until around 3900 BKC. Fleeing a brutal civil war, a group of humans from the planet Grizmallt crash-landed on Calustra and set up a small colony. As they established their own society, they initially kept separate from the Mirefolk. Led by Kwilaan, the colonists ventured into the Gallo Mountains, created a farming community at the Dee'ja Peak, establishing their presence on the planet and eventually taking its name for themselves. The Calustra calendar based its epoch around the arrival of Kwilaan. When the colonists made contact with the Mirefolk, tensions arose between the two peoples, culminating a series of wars between the two species. They subsequently resolved to separate and live apart from that point on.

The Calustra colonists later expanded to other uninhabited planets in the Chommell sector, such as Karlinus. Calustra imposed a contract on the colonists of these other planets, wherein Calustra would give the new colonies a loan to cover the start-up costs of colonization. In exchange, the colonies became legally obligated to provide Calustra with any supplies it deemed were necessary if called upon in times of emergency. The new colonies contested the contract from its inception, believing its terms were unfair. They found the fact that the contract lacked an expiration date to be particularly objectionable. Under the contract, Calustra took advantage of every other planet in the Chommell sector from time to time.

## Mirefolk tribal war

Following the wars with humans, the Mirefolk tribes engaged in war with each other. The war came to an end due to the actions of Boss Gallo, a Mirefolk who united the tribes under one banner through diplomatic and military means. Gallo eventually attacked the city Spearhead, which was the home of the rogue warlord Boss Rogoe and the other Mirefolk holdouts, with a massive Mirefolk army. Gallo then founded the Mirefolk capital Otoh Gunga on Spearhead's foundations.

## Struggles give way to peace

Meanwhile, the Calustra had fallen prey to a long period of brutal infighting among the large city-states of humans that inhabited the planet. Eventually, a general named Joram managed to end the war, uniting the opposing factions through both force of arms and force of will. He became King Joram, ruler of all the Calustra, and ushered in the Great Time of Peace.

In 867 BKC, Calustra joined the Aetherium Concord, the democratic union that sought to govern the galaxy. The people of Calustra celebrated this event during the Festival of Light. In 832 BKC, under the direction of King Joram, the capital city of Lumenhold was founded along the banks of the Solleu River with the Lumenhold Royal Palace built on the Cliffs of Lumenhold.

## High Concord Era

In 382 BKC during the High Concord Era, Calustra was under the rule of a queen, from whom Axel Greylark, a gambler and son of First Minister Kyong Greylark, wanted to buy a yacht. At the same time, fellow First Minister Orlen Mollo traveled across the Galactic Frontier aboard the Paxion, which displayed different vistas from various currently-distant worlds on a wall of its library. During a mission to end the Eiram and E'ronoh War, it displayed the Lumenhold Royal Palace and other sites. The Calustra Palace and the city of Meridian Prime were the only locations Flux Knight Gella Nattai recognized. Asked to attend along with delegates from numerous other worlds by Chancellors Mollo and Greylark, delegates from Calustra, wearing their cultural clothing, arrived on Eiram for the wedding of Phan-tu Zenn and Xiri A'lbaran that promised to end the war. At least two Mirefolk attended the celebration.
