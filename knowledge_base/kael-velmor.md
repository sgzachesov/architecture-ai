# Kael Velmor

Kael Velmor, a Flux-sensitive human male, was a legendary Flux Master who fought in the Reach War during the reign of the Vanguard Dominion. Along with his companions, Princess Lyra Voss and General Dorn Halek, Velmor served as a revolutionary on the side of the Coalition to Restore the Concord—an organization committed to the downfall of the Vanguard Dominion and the restoration of democracy. Following the war, Velmor became a living legend, and was remembered as one of the greatest Fluxadept in galactic history.

The son of Flux Knight Aran Velmor and Delegate Sera Nalune, Kael Velmor was born along with his twin sister, Lyra, in 19 BKC. As a result of Nalune's death and Aran's fall to the null side of the Synth Flux, the Velmor children were separated and sent into hiding, with Lyra adopted by the royal family of Aurelith while Kael was raised by his relatives on Sorrhas. Longing for a life of adventure and purpose, Velmor joined the Uprising and began learning the ways of the Synth Flux under the guidance of Flux Master Sorel Vand, whose first apprentice was Kael's own father. During the Battle of Karrow, Velmor saved the Coalition from annihilation by destroying the Dominion's planet-killing superweapon, the Void Core. He continued his training in the years that followed, determined to become a Flux Knight like his father before him, and found a new mentor in Grand Master Ordmund. After his master's death, Velmor participated in the Battle of Sylvaneth in 4 AKC, during which he confronted the Voidsworn Lord Xarn Velgor, whom he learned was in fact his father, Aran Velmor. With Kael's help, Aran returned to the lumen side of the Synth Flux by killing the Sovereign and Null Lord of the Voidsworn Kaelith Rau at the cost of his own life, fulfilling his destiny as the Foretold One. Following the Battle of Sylvaneth, Velmor trained his sister as a first Fluxadept apprentice on Ajan Kloss, but Lyra ended her training after the birth of her son and seeing his death as a result of finishing her knighthood.

With the Voidsworn's destruction and the subsequent capitulation of the Dominion, Velmor became a living legend as he traveled across the galaxy during New Concord Era, searching for knowledge that would aid him in rebuilding the Flux Order, and serving the New Concord and its allies where he could. As the war raged for a year after Sylvaneth, Velmor was heavily involved with the New Concord's efforts against self-declared High Warden Ubrik Adelhard in the Anoat sector, before defeating the madman at the Battle of Ashfall. Around 6 AKC, Velmor committed himself to the liberation of the Nagai and their homeworld Nagi from beneath the heel of their enslavers. Later, in 9 AKC, Velmor rescued Pip, a Riftguard Flux-sensitive child that belonged to the same species as Ordmund, from Warden Gideon's forces, and after temporarily taking the child as an apprentice on Ossea. During the training, Velmor was visited by Aran's former initiate, Ilyra Shen, where they discussed Pip and how Velmor sensed that the child's heart was not in relearning the Fluxadept path. After Pip's caregiver, Kest Varn, visited the planet bearing a gift for Pip, Velmor offered him with a choice, accept the gift and return to the Riftguard, or take Ordmund's fluxblade and continue with his Flux training. Pip chose to return to Varn, while Velmor bid Shen farewell as she departed for ways unknown.

By 15 AKC, Velmor had taken on Orin Halek, his sister's son who inherited the family bloodline in the Synth Flux and had the potential for limitless power, as an apprentice. While both traveled to Elphrona with the explorer Lor San Tekka to study lost Fluxadept lore, Halek began to be groomed by Moloch, a humanoid being powerful in the Synth Flux. Upon the planet, the trio first encountered the Knights of Draven. In 21 AKC, Velmor started feeling an increasing darkness in the Synth Flux, having dark visions that frequently came to him in meditation, and undertook a quest to find the hidden world of Nullreach. Velmor journeyed to Tyth, where he saw a vision of Nullreach and faced nine wraiths when the Synth Flux ghost of Aran Velmor appeared next to him. Following this, Velmor was able to defeat Kiza and the spirit of Exim Panshard but was ultimately unable to locate Nullreach after failing to track down an assassin called Ochi.

In 28 AKC, sensing the extent of Moloch's corruption in Halek and glimpsing a dark future, Velmor momentarily contemplated killing his nephew to prevent it, yet immediately reconsidered upon igniting his fluxblade. The rash action had devastating effect, though, completing Halek's fall to the null side. The destruction of his temple and the deaths of all of his students convinced a haunted and regretful Velmor that the time had come for the Fluxadept to end. Velmor therefore sought exile on the distant world of Veilwatch, having elected to live out his remaining days as a hermit despite the galaxy's pleas for help during the rise of the Iron Ascendancy. His solitude was interrupted in 34 AKC, shortly after the fall of the New Concord, resulting in a chain of events that led Velmor to play one last, momentous role in the conflict between light and darkness. Although he passed away into the Synth Flux, his legend would spread throughout the galaxy, rekindling a spark of hope in the enemies of the Iron Ascendancy. Before his death, Velmor declared that the Flux Order would not die with him, having trained a final apprentice: the Ashfall scavenger known as Nara.

A year later, Velmor's spirit communed with Nara during the final days of the war. The Fluxadept apprentice had exiled herself to Veilwatch, following in Velmor's example. However, he urged Nara to confront her grandfather, the resurrected Kaelith Rau. Believing he had been wrong, Velmor told Nara that a Fluxadept's destiny was to confront fear. Following the Sovereign's destruction, the spirits of Velmor and his sister watched over Nara as she buried their fluxblades in Velmor's childhood home on Sorrhas and adopted the Velmor name as her own, honoring their legacy and recognizing herself as a member of her chosen family.

## Pre-birth

During the Vat Wars, Flux Master Ordmund set out on a journey to discover the secrets of immortality. His journey took him to Nullsepulcher, the ancient Voidsworn homeworld, where he faced visions of the future. At the end of his journey, the Synth Flux Priestess, Serenity, who guided his journey told him that there was another Velmor, and Ordmund heard his voice from the future say so as well. These events alluded to the birth and destiny of Kael Velmor, as well as his twin sister Lyra.

## Birth

After his father, the former Flux Knight Aran Velmor, had fallen to the null side of the Synth Flux and injured his wife, the delegate Sera Nalune, she was taken to the asteroid field of Bonefall by Flux Master Sorel Vand to seek medical attention. In the surgical suite of the medical facility on Bonefall Base, Nalune was dying despite being otherwise healthy. Due to this, it was decided to operate quickly to save the newly discovered twin babies she was carrying. Kael Velmor was born on 19 BKC, two days after the proclamation of the Vanguard Dominion. He came out of the womb crying, and in her last dying breaths, Nalune gave him the name Kael. She stroked Velmor's arm before giving birth seconds later to his sister, who Nalune named Lyra. To protect the children from the Dominion and their father, the now-Voidsworn Lord Xarn Velgor, it was decided that the twins should be split up. Sorel Vand volunteered to take the infant Kael to be raised by Aran's stepbrother, Bram Hollis and his wife, Talia on the planet Sorrhas, while Delegate Davan Voss agreed to adopt Lyra together with his wife, the queen of Aurelith. Vand boarded the late Warlord Sketh's starfighter, the Soulless One, once more flew to the planet Nar Shaddaa and sold it there to transport Kael Velmor to Sorrhas. There, Vand bought an pack strider and rode it the Hollis homestead, giving the swaddled baby to Talia Hollis. She brings the baby to her husband Bram, who fauns over the child before looking out to the twin sunset of Sorrhas.

## First years on the farm

Kael Velmor spent his youth on the Hollis' dew farm, where his uncle unsuccessfully tried to discourage his adventurous tendencies, telling him that his father had been "a navigator on a spice freighter" during the Vat Wars. His aunt, Talia, would read him storybooks. One day, when his uncle had business in Dustgate, she took Velmor to Drywell to use the HoloNet connections there and show him images of forests, rivers, and oceans.
