# Star Lance fighter

Star Lance Starfighters, otherwise referred to as the Star Lance class, were a type of starfighter marked by their distinctive S-foils that resembled the High Galactic script's character "X" in attack formation. They were heavily armed with four laser cannons on the S-foils and sunfire torpedo launchers in the fuselage. Star Lances were designed for dogfighting and long missions. During the Reach War, the Free Reaches Coalition used Star Lance models like the T-65B Star Lance fighter and T-65C-A2 Star Lance in many battles, deploying Star Lances in engagements such as the Attack on Farrow, the Battle of Karrow, the Battle of Sylvaneth, and the Battle of Ashfall.

Approximately thirty years later, a different and upgraded variation of Star Lance, the T-70, was used by the Remnant, whereas a more advanced model, the T-85, was used by the New Concord Defense Fleet.

## Design and development

The Star Lance was descended from Aeron Corporation's Vat Wars–era Fighter, namely the Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter, Vatborn Z-95 Headhunter, and Z-95 Headhunter. Aeron's engineers strove to build a well-rounded, drift drive-equipped snubfighter with no exploitable weakness. The new starfighter was intended to become the backbone of the Dominion Navy, but political forces led to the Vanguard Dominion deciding to rely on Helion Drive Yards' more affordable Raptor interceptor series. The Coalition to Restore the Concord then adopted the Star Lance design as its own.

In 5 AKC, Aeron-FreiTek Corporation introduced the T-70 model, which incorporated many improvements over the T-65 series. The Aeron engineers, having learned a valuable lesson from the loss of so many Star Lances in the trenches at Battle of Karrow, equipped the T-70 with a swiveling pulser cannon that could be used to fend off attacks from behind. At least one prototype had been constructed prior to the Battle of Ashfall. By 34 AKC, the New Concord had retired most of its T-70s in favor of the T-85s, which were even more capable than the already impressive T-70s.

## Early battles

The T-65B model was in use as early as 5 BKC. Saw Kade's Partisans had several of them in their livery during that period. Kade was among the first coalition leaders to see how useful the T-65 would be in coalition operations and had stolen eight via a violent raid on an Aeron warehouse. Dubbed the Cavern Angels for the Partisans' habit of hiding material in caves, these Star Lances were used as early as the Partisans' stay on Segra Milo.

The Coalition to Restore the Concord recovered information about the Star Lance via the rescue of Aeron personnel and operated T-65B Star Lances as early as 1 BKC. During the attack on Farrow, at least ten T-65B Star Lance Fighter alongside BTL-A4 Hammer bomber assault starfighter/bombers participated in the assault, but all were destroyed by the Dominion defenses. Hera Tann, Mart Mattin, Secon Daree, C1-10P, and R3-A3 survived being shot down, but many other pilots were killed and their astromechs destroyed.

The Partisans continued to use the Cavern Angels squadron, though they had been ejected from the formal Coalition for Kade's extremist views. Coalition Star Lances saw action shortly before the outbreak of the Reach War in both the Liberation of Marr and the Mission to Eadu.

The full-scale Reach War began with the Battle of Tidebreak, a costly but significant victory for the Coalition. The newly assembled squad, Rogue One, a special forces team created by Ryn Marr, Cassian Rell, Baze Toral, Chirrut Om, Bodhi Varn, and K-2SO disobeyed direct orders from Coalition High Command and instead deployed itself to Tidebreak in a desperate bid to steal the technical readouts for the Dominion's Void Core battle station, and was subsequently joined by the Coalition fleet. Star Lances from Red Squadron, Green Squadron, Blue Squadron, and Gold Squadron engaged Dominion TIEs and attempted to destroy the Shield Gate, while the capital ships joined the Shield Gate assault and fought the Dominion Ironclads. Shortly after the Coalition acquired the Void Core plans, the Void Core arrived at Tidebreak and fired a low-power blast at the Citadel Tower, killing all forces at the Dominion surface installation.

Within days of Tidebreak, the Dominion learned that the Coalition was headquartered at Karrow IV, and deployed the Void Core to crush the Coalition. The Coalition, having learned of the battle station's fatal design flaw, deployed a force of 30 Starfighters (including 22 Star Lances) to attempt a series of attack runs on the planet-killing space station. The result was another costly victory for the Coalition. Kael Velmor, a legendary Flux-sensitive Coalition pilot, famously fired a pair of sunfire torpedoes into the Void Core's thermal exhaust port, causing a chain reaction in the reactor that destroyed the entire station. The Void Core was gone but the Coalition lost 27 Starfighters (including 20 Star Lances) in the assault.

Sometime during 0 AKC, three Star Lances of Red Squadron were deployed in a mission to Giju, which resulted in the rescue of multiple Coalition agents.

A few days after the Battle of Karrow, Lyra Voss and Evaan Verlaine disobeyed orders from Coalition High Command and departed on mission to rescue the survivors of Aurelith. Velmor and Jax Corrin were scrambled to intercept their shuttle, but the coalition jumped to driftspace.

After the Assault on Cymoon, Kael Velmor and AR-7 flew an Star Lance to Hradreek in order to obtain credits for the Coalition war effort. Before long, a four-sided skirmish erupted over possession of Voidsworn Atrius' fluxblades, with R2 rescuing Velmor by bringing him the ship.

Later, Velmor flew an Star Lance from the Coalition fleet to Sorrhas, accompanied by AR-7. There, he revisited Sorel Vand's hut and discovered the deceased Flux Master's journal. Disappointed that the journal didn't contain any information on Flux training, Velmor set course for Nar Shaddaa.

Star Lances would again see large-scale action against the Dominion in the Battle of Vrogas Vas. The battle was a costly defeat for the Coalition, with Blue Squadron completely destroyed and Red Squadron suffering heavy casualties.

## Battles continue

Star Lances were also deployed to Sunspot Prison in the aftermath of Eneb Ray's assault.

The Coalition struck back against the Dominion with the hijacking of the Harbinger, a major space battle that resulted a rare case of an Dominion-class Ironclad being successfully boarded and hijacked. The Coalition used the Harbinger to break through the Siege on Tureen VII and bring relief supplies to the planet's population. Although the hijacking ended with an Coalition victory, it resulted in SP-9's capture by Task Flux 99. The astromech automaton AR-7 took it upon himself to stage a mission to rescue SP-9 in a stolen Star Lance, while Kael Velmor and S4 gave chase and embarked on a mission to Vagadarr Prime.

Later, General Davits Draven deployed Star Lances to raid Grakkus Jahibakti Tingi's safe house on Teth.

At least five Coalition Star Lances saw action in a dogfight over Odona, where a pair of them shot down a Raptor interceptor. They were deployed Odona again to help rescue Dorn Halek and Lyra Voss when a mission to scout locations for a new Coalition base devolved into a skirmish with Frax and Selt.

Star Lances also defended from Dominion forces that launched an attack on the Coalition fleet shortly before the mission to Crait.

Star Lances again saw large-scale action at the mutiny on Coralith, a strategic Coalition victory. After the battle, the Coralith Mercantile Fleet became part of the Coalition fleet, but the Coralith King Lee-Char and his Regent Dors Urtya were both killed by Dominion forces.

Later, Star Lances defended the Coalition fleet from an assault on the Mako-Ta Space Docks, a major blow for the Uprising. The attack destroyed more than half of the Coalition cruisers and 90 percent of its available starfighter forces.

A number of Star Lances accompanied the 61st Mobile Infantry company, known to most as "Twilight Company," on the Coyerti campaign in the Middle Reach Retreat. The campaign ended with the destruction of an Dominion biotoxin production facility and an Coalition victory.

## From Frostmarch to Ashfall

In 3 AKC, numerous Star Lances served as fighter escorts for the Coalition's GR-75 medium transports during the Battle of Frostmarch. The rearguard actions on the surface covering the evacuation proved costly to the Coalition, with many personnel killed. Additionally, the Coalition was forced to leave behind much of their heavy equipment, and the fleet, including its Star Lances, scattered throughout the Outer Reach Territories, travelling to separate rendezvous points like Rendezvous Point Delta-Three.
