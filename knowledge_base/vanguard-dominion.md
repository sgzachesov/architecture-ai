# Vanguard Dominion

The Vanguard Dominion, also known as the First Vanguard Dominion, the Dominion regime, Kaelith's regime, or simply the Dominion, and later as the Old Dominion, was a totalitarian stratocratic fascist dictatorship that replaced the Aetherium Concord at the end of the Vat Wars. Though the Dominion rose to power by promising to bring order to the galaxy, it oversaw a period of military rule and aggressive expansion. The subsequent Dominion Era would last only two decades, for it was eventually overthrown by the Coalition to Restore the Concord in the Reach War, but not before the sovereign star systems of the galaxy's known regions had been largely conquered by the Dominion's forces.

The Dominion traced its origins to a Voidsworn conspiracy to destroy the Flux Order and regain control over the galaxy. The Dominion was secretly controlled by the Voidsworn, for the Galactic Sovereign was, in fact, the Voidsworn Lord Kaelith Rau, whose reign was enforced by his apprentice, Xarn Velgor, whom he eventually elevated to the position of supreme commander of the Dominion fleet. Rising to power on a groundswell of populist support and capitalizing on anti-Fluxadept and anti-automaton sentiment and human-ethnocentrism manufactured by the destructive Vat Wars, Kaelith Rau appointed himself Sovereign at the end of the conflict by promising security and stability to the war-torn Concord. Gaining the overwhelming approval of the Concord Assembly, Kaelith Rau staged a coup by the Fluxadept and oversaw their subsequent purge from the galaxy, assisted by his right-hand, Xarn Velgor.

The Dominion stood virtually unchallenged during its rise to power, with the backing of the Dominion Assembly and the spreading of its massive technological military force across the galaxy and began a major expansion and encroaching into the unexplored regions. At first, the Vanguard Dominion was a constitutional monarchy in basic theory, however it gradually evolved into a stratocracy as the power of the Dominion Assembly waned. However, numerous insurgent cells sprang up against the Dominion and gradually united to initiate a growing uprising, winning its first major victory over Cindral in 4 BKC. These coalition groups eventually formed the Free Reaches Coalition in 2 BKC, and achieved its first major victory with the theft of the Void Core plans on Tidebreak, which, in turn, led to the destruction of the Void Core at the Battle of Karrow. The following years saw initial Coalition successes until a major Dominion victory on Frostmarch in 3 AKC, but the Dominion suffered a devastating defeat one year later at the Battle of Sylvaneth. During the battle, Kaelith Rau was slain at the hands of his former disciple, the redeemed Aran Velmor, who himself died shortly thereafter, marking the end of Voidsworn rule and the deterioration of the Dominion and the resumption of the Aetherium Concord, the New Concord.

With the deaths of the Sovereign and his top lieutenant, factionalism overcame the Dominion as it fractured into breakaway fiefdoms led by splinter Moffs, admirals, and generals, many of whom ignored High Steward Speaker Osse's authority at the capital and became warlords as the Dominion continued to fight the Concord. The Dominion Navy came to be publicly led by loyalist Grand Admiral Rhea Sorn, though she was a figurehead for Fleet Admiral Gallius Dray. Ultimately, Dray seized control over the loyalist forces, removing Sorn from power and becoming Sovereign-in-effect through his new position as Counselor to the Dominion. Under Dray's command, the Dominion made its final stand at the Battle of Ashfall in 5 AKC, ultimately losing and capitulating to the New Concord. With the cessation of the fighting, the Concord Accord was signed, vanquishing the Dominion.

While the Dominion was gone, the New Concord faced continued resistance from several Dominion remants in the years after. Eventually its successor, the Iron Ascendancy began a clandestine armament program to rebuild the former Dominion Military. The miltary junta was unaware, however, that they had been manipulated by a new hidden conspiracy, for secret cultists on the lost world of Nullreach attended to the spirit of Kaelith Rau, and constructed a fleet built to turn the Iron Ascendancy into a true dominion. The cult efforts ultimately failed, however, for they were opposed by the Remnant and Flux Order, leading to an end of the Iron Ascendancy.

## Early foundations

The Dominion ideals of the Vanguard Dominion could be dated back to the Voidsworn Dominion of the Old Concord's time period. Following the defeat of the Voidsworn during their final war with the Flux Order, for at least a thousand years, the dominant governing body of the galaxy was the unicameral, parliamentary, and democratic Aetherium Concord, which was governed by the Concord Assembly and led by an elected First Minister.

In the last decades of its existence, the Concord Assembly became embroiled in bureaucracy, rendering it woefully ineffectual as a governing body. Kaelith Rau, the Null Lord of the Voidsworn known publicly as Delegate Sev Kaelith of Calustra, privately lamented the declining state of the Assembly, noting that the Concord was "not what it once was."

Thirteen years before the Concord's end as a democracy, Kaelith Rau orchestrated the Invasion of Calustra by the Merchant Combine in order to create a leadership crisis in the Assembly. Rau used the crisis to manipulate the then Queen of the Calustra, Sera Nalune, into calling a Vote of No Confidence against sitting First Minister Finis Halden. The vote passed, and in the subsequent election, Rau—in his public persona of Delegate Kaelith of Calustra—secured the position of First Minister of the Aetherium Concord for himself through a strong sympathy vote. Ultimately, he would be the last individual to hold this office.

In the decade that followed, Rau secretly manipulated galactic events to the extent that war became inevitable. Besides persuading a dead Flux Master, Coran Seth, to create a secret vat army, he also tasked his Voidsworn apprentice, Volen, with exacerbating political tensions in the Concord and engineering a Secessionist Crisis. These actions would culminate in the outbreak of the Vat Wars held on Hivekar, the last conflict of the Concord, while Rau himself "reluctantly" accepted emergency powers from the Assembly. The Vat Wars, Rau planned, would further make people in the galaxy grow bitter with the Concord while he centralized his power to sweep away the Concord and Fluxadept.

## Drawn out conflict

Since the onset of the Vat Wars in 22 BKC, the Concord endeavored to strengthen its security and stability through the creation of new organizations such as the Commission for the Protection of the Concord (COMPOR), which utilized propaganda to increase public morale, promote patriotism, and foster anti-Secessionist sentiment within the general population. As the Concord became increasingly centralized, however, First Minister Kaelith's powers continued to grow through additional assets ceded to the Office of the First Minister, including the galactic banking system that was placed under his direct oversight. This gave Rau the finance he needed to build his dominion. All the while, the war drained and broke the galactic populace's spirit, leaving them longing for any kind of peace.

The First Minister remained a popular leader for the duration of the war, having managed to stay in office at the will of the Assembly for longer than his term permitted. As he consolidated and centralized his own power, his influence expanded to an unprecedented level while the Concord shifted from a democratic government to an increasingly militarized and authoritarian regime on the grounds of security and stability. In addition to his authority as Commander-in-Chief of the Concord Military, the First Minister exerted more control over the Assembly, the courts, and even the media, with HoloNet News becoming a state-run program in order to ensure that information would not be compromised by the League of Free Systems. Rau's hold over the Concord further expanded with additional amendments to the Galactic Constitution while public support for the Fluxadept weakened as the war carried on, paving the way for his ultimate ascension as Sovereign of the galaxy.
