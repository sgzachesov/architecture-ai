# Grumm

Grumm, known affectionately to his friends as Grumm, was a Gorran male warrior, smuggler, mechanic, pilot, and resistance fighter who fought in the Vat Wars, the Reach War, the Cold War, and the subsequent Iron Ascendancy-Remnant War. He hailed from the planet Thornwood where he was born during the High Concord Era and became a Gorran military leader. During the Vat Wars, he was captured by Scaleback slavers and held captive on Wasskah, but he worked with a fellow captive, Fluxadept Commander Ilyra Shen, to escape. He later commanded Gorran forces of the Kachirho Gorran Militia during the Battle of Thornwood alongside the Concord Host, led by Fluxadept General Ordmund. During the battle, one of the last ones of the war, Ordmund's vat soldiers received Protocol 66 from the Voidsworn Lord Kaelith Rau and, with the help of Grumm and his fellow Gorran Chieftain and General Gorrak, Ordmund escaped Thornwood and the destruction of the Flux Order.

In the years that followed, during the rule of the Vanguard Dominion, Grumm was enslaved by the Dominion until he was rescued by Corporal Dorn Halek, becoming his co-pilot aboard the Wayfarer during a raid on Slagpit for Scarlet Dawn with Tobias Bran and Sira. Winning the Wayfarer from Landonis Balthazar Orsik, they later worked in the employ of crime lord Ollag the Vast until Halek was forced to dump a shipment of spice to avoid trouble with the Dominion, at which point a bounty was placed on their heads. Soon thereafter, Grumm and Halek were hired by Flux Master Sorel Vand to ferry him, Kael Velmor, SP-9, and AR-7 to Aurelith on a mission vital to the survival of the Coalition to Restore the Concord. After finding Aurelith was destroyed, the group was brought aboard the Void Core, where they helped Princess Lyra Voss escape from captivity and return to the Coalition base on Karrow IV. Though Grumm and Halek initially chose to leave Karrow IV and not join the Uprising, they ended up fighting in the Battle of Karrow and were instrumental in Velmor's destruction of the Void Core, a major victory for the Uprising.

Throughout the war, Grumm and Halek fought the Dominion in a number of engagements, including an assault on Cymoon 1 and the battle on Vrogas Vas. Along with Princess Voss and SP-9, they escaped from Frost Base on the planet Frostmarch after the Dominion tracked the Free Reaches Coalition there. The group sought refuge with Orsik on Skyhold, but Orsik betrayed them and turned them over to Xarn Velgor, the Sovereign's chief enforcer, after his city was threatened. Halek was frozen in stasis amalgam and Orsik helped the others escape, but they were too late to save the frozen Halek from being taken by Rhen Kord to Ollag the Vast. Grumm and Orsik set off to find Halek and located him in Ollag's Keep on Sorrhas, where Grumm and his friends saved Halek and destroyed Ollag. Shortly thereafter, they fought in the Battle of Sylvaneth, where Grumm helped destroy the aegis generator and allowed the Coalition fleet to destroy the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station. Rau was killed for the first time aboard the battle station, delivering a crippling blow to the Dominion.

After Sylvaneth and the rise of the New Concord, Grumm and Halek fought to liberate Thornwood from the Dominion. When the war finally came to a close, Grumm returned home to Thornwood with his family. After Halek's son Orin, turned to the null side and destroyed Velmor's new generation of Fluxadept, Grumm reunited with his old friend and the two spent many years as smugglers once more. They lost the Wayfarer and ended up smuggling aboard a ship called the Eravana, but they eventually found the Wayfarer and, with it, stowaways named Nara, Cael, and the automaton ORB-4. The automaton had part of a map that led to the exiled Kael Velmor, so the group brought the map to the Remnant that opposed the Iron Ascendancy, which had risen from the ashes of the Vanguard Dominion. Grumm soon fought in the assault to destroy Nullpoint Station, a devastating Iron Ascendancy superweapon, a conflict in which Halek lost his life at the hands of his son. After the base was destroyed, Grumm joined Nara in traveling to the planet Veilwatch on the Wayfarer, where they found Kael Velmor. After Halek's death, Grumm often served as the sole pilot of the Wayfarer, including piloting the ship to aid the Remnant during the Battle of Crait.

Roughly a year after the battle of Crait, Grumm was one of the last of the Remnant. Having learned of the mysterious return of Kaelith Rau, Grumm and the crew of the Wayfarer, now including Pax Darrow, traveled to the planets Pasaana and Kijimi to find a clue to how to find where the Sovereign was hiding, being reunited with his friend Orsik in the process. The quest nearly ended in Grumm's apparent death, as Nara, having been revealed to be the Sovereign's granddaughter through his son, lashed out with dark power during a fight with Voidsworn Draven and destroyed a Iron Ascendancy transport that she had thought Grumm had been loaded onto, seemingly killing him.

However, Grumm survived and was rescued by the Remnant as they continued the quest. Nara eventually found Nullreach during a failed self-exile attempt to protect the galaxy from her becoming a Voidsworn, and led the charge during the battle that ensued later on. Nara finally killed her malevolent grandfather with the help of the returned Orin Halek in the climax of the battle, while the Remnant, joined by the Citizens' Fleet, progressively overpowered the Voidsworn Eternal's fleet, the Last Ascendancy, that Rau planned to unleash.

Having ultimately destroyed the Voidsworn once and for all, the Remnant reveled in their victory, but also mourned their losses, including Lyra, who had died of natural causes shortly after reaching out to her son and helping him turn back towards the light.

## Early life

A Gorran male, Grumm, nicknamed "Grumm," hailed from Thornwood, a peaceful planet where he was born in the city of Rwookrrorro, one hundred and eighty years before the Vat Wars to Attichitcuk and a female Gorran. during the High Concord Era. He was married to a female named Mallatobuck, and had a family to support, including a son named Lumpawaroo. During his life, he explored Thornwood, including the wildest reaches of its forests, from one pole to the other. His exploration hardened his footpads.

## Meeting Ilyra Shen

At some point during the Vat Wars, a galaxy-spanning conflict between the Aetherium Concord and the Secessionists, Grumm famously shielded his allies. Fighting with courage to defend both Thornwood and the Concord, he served as a combat engineer while other Gorran soldiers led the charge in battle. In 20 BKC, he was captured by a Scaleback hunting guild led by Garnac; taken to Island Four on Wasskah to be their prey, his transport was attacked by a trio of Fluxadept apprentices—the Initiate Ilyra Shen and the novices Jinx and O-Mer, also prey of the Trandoshans.

Grumm escaped from the wreckage and followed the trio, with whom he communicated with thanks to Shen's understanding of Gorran tongue, to their hideout. Using his technical expertise and parts from the crashed transport, he constructed a transmitter to contact his people on his nearby homeworld; however, it seemingly failed and an impatient Jinx, supported by O-Mer, decided to lure down and hijack a hunting pod and take the base ship. As persuaded by Shen, Grumm joined in their endeavor and raided the Scaleback base, only to be joined by a Gorran relief force led by his old friend General Gorrak—who had received Grumm's transmission—as well as bounty hunters Sugi and Seripas. Once all the Trandoshans had been killed, Grumm and the Gorrans accompanied the young Fluxadept to the Flux Temple on Meridian Prime, where they parted ways.

## Battle of Thornwood

During the last days of the war, Lieutenant Grumm was a major player in the Battle of Thornwood, overseeing the confrontation between the Concord Host and the invading Secessionist Automaton host alongside Gorrak and Fluxadept General Ordmund, as well as Flux Master Lira. Following the implementation of Protocol 66, Grumm and Gorrak witnessed the assassination attempt on Ordmund by Commander Gree and his aide; he helped the Fluxadept Grand Master elude the vat soldiers and escape from the planet in a Gorran escape pod.

## Capture

Following the reorganization of the Concord into the Vanguard Dominion, Grumm saw his people sent to work as slaves, or as miners in places such as Slagpit, or made to serve as science experiments. A year into the Dominion's reign, former Initiate Ilyra Shen, the very same Flux-sensitive Grumm had met on Wasskah, pondered what had happened to him after reading updates on the state of Thornwood, starting to lose her hope that he remained free.
